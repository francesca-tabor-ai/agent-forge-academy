# Building and Governing AI Content Pipelines

##  Course Overview

**Duration:** 6 weeks  
**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Understanding of AI/ML systems, software development, regulatory compliance basics

---

##  What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

 Understand the EU AI Act and its risk-based approach to AI governance  
 Navigate the five phases of the AI life cycle from ideation to deployment  
 Identify prohibited practices and high-risk domains for AI content pipelines  
 Implement data governance strategies aligned with EU regulations  
 Build robust, safe, and transparent AI content systems  
 Conduct conformity assessments and manage post-market monitoring  
 Design systems with human oversight and explainability  
 Handle serious incidents and maintain compliance throughout the system lifecycle

---

##  Course Modules

### [Module 1: Foundations of the AI Life Cycle](Module_01_Foundations_of_the_AI_Life_Cycle.md)
- Defining AI Systems and General Purpose AI (GPAI)
- The Five Phases of the AI Life Cycle: ideation, qualification, framing, development, industrialization, and run phase
- The Risk-Based Approach: understanding unacceptable, high-risk, limited risk, and minimal risk classifications
- **Lab 1:** Risk classification exercise for AI content pipeline scenarios

### [Module 2: Scoping, Ideation, and Prohibited Practices](Module_02_Scoping_Ideation_and_Prohibited_Practices.md)
- Compliance by Design: integrating ethics into ideation phase
- Identifying Banned Practices: social scoring, manipulative techniques, biometric categorization
- High-Risk Domain Scoping: determining if content pipelines fall into regulated domains
- **Lab 2:** Compliance assessment for AI content pipeline use cases

### [Module 3: Data Strategy and Pipeline Governance](Module_03_Data_Strategy_and_Pipeline_Governance.md)
- Data as the Strategic Foundation: managing data explosion and ensuring accuracy
- Sourcing and Open Data: leveraging European data spaces for sovereign AI innovation
- Dataset Quality and Integrity: establishing protocols for training, validation, and testing datasets
- Bias Detection and Mitigation: technical strategies for examining and addressing unwanted biases
- **Lab 3:** Data governance framework implementation

### [Module 4: Development, Safety, and Robustness](Module_04_Development_Safety_and_Robustness.md)
- Technical Documentation and Model Cards: comprehensive system documentation
- Engineering for Robustness and Predictability: managing distribution shift and ensuring resilience
- Cyberattack Resilience: protecting against jailbreaking, prompt injection, and adversarial attacks
- Copyright and Intellectual Property: compliance with Union copyright law
- **Lab 4:** Security and robustness testing framework

### [Module 5: Transparency and Human Agency](Module_05_Transparency_and_Human_Agency.md)
- Disclosure and Marking of AI Content: ensuring users know they're interacting with AI
- Designing for Human Oversight: building operational constraints and override mechanisms
- Explainability and Interpretability: addressing black box models with interpretability tools
- **Lab 5:** Transparency and human oversight implementation

### [Module 6: Deployment, Monitoring, and Life Cycle Management](Module_06_Deployment_Monitoring_and_Life_Cycle_Management.md)
- Conformity Assessments and Certification: understanding CE marking and notified body assessments
- Impact Assessments: conducting Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIA)
- Post-Market Monitoring: systematic collection and analysis of system performance data
- Managing Serious Incidents: implementing incident reporting channels within 15-day requirements
- **Lab 6:** Complete compliance framework and monitoring system
