# Automated Suitability & ESG Preference Matching

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Understanding of financial advisory, regulatory frameworks (MiFID II), basic machine learning concepts, Python programming

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: The Suitability Challenge in the Digital Era](Module_01_The_Suitability_Challenge_in_the_Digital_Era.md)
- Why traditional suitability frameworks break down at scale
- Cognitive overload in advisory workflows
- From manual questionnaires to algorithmic matching
- Regulatory expectations vs. real-world workflows
- **Exercise 1:** Map a traditional suitability workflow and identify automation bottlenecks

### [Module 2: Risk Profiling at Scale: From PRR to Personalization](Module_02_Risk_Profiling_at_Scale_From_PRR_to_Personalization.md)
- Automated engines matching Product Risk Ratings (PRR) to client profiles
- Risk capacity vs. risk tolerance vs. risk willingness
- Translating client answers into machine-readable risk profiles
- Mapping PRR bands to client risk buckets
- **Exercise 2:** Design a simplified PRR-to-client risk matching logic

### [Module 3: ESG Preferences: Turning Values into Data](Module_03_ESG_Preferences_Turning_Values_into_Data.md)
- Convert qualitative sustainability preferences into structured inputs
- ESG preference categories under MiFID II
- Environmental, social, and governance objectives explained
- Client preference hierarchies (hard exclusions vs. soft preferences)
- **Exercise 3:** Build a preference tree for three different ESG-focused clients

### [Module 4: The European ESG Template (EET): Anatomy of a Data Beast](Module_04_The_European_ESG_Template_EET_Anatomy_of_a_Data_Beast.md)
- Understand how 600+ ESG data points power automated matching
- Structure of the EET and key data sections
- Mandatory vs. optional ESG fields
- Principal Adverse Impacts (PAIs) and double materiality
- **Exercise 4:** Identify which EET fields are critical for automated ESG matching

### [Module 5: Article 8 & Article 9: Automating Regulatory Classification](Module_05_Article_8_and_Article_9_Automating_Regulatory_Classification.md)
- Reduce advisor error by embedding regulatory logic into AI
- What legally distinguishes Article 6, 8, and 9 funds
- Common misclassification risks
- Rule-based vs. AI-assisted classification
- **Exercise 5:** Create a decision tree to validate Article 8/9 eligibility

### [Module 6: AI Matching Engines: Architecture & Logic](Module_06_AI_Matching_Engines_Architecture_and_Logic.md)
- Learn how AI reduces cognitive load without replacing human judgment
- Rules engines vs. machine learning models
- Matching logic: risk × ESG × client constraints
- Explainability and audit trails
- **Exercise 6:** Sketch a high-level system architecture for an ESG suitability matcher

### [Module 7: Explainability, Trust & Regulatory Defensibility](Module_07_Explainability_Trust_and_Regulatory_Defensibility.md)
- Ensure AI outputs can be explained to clients and regulators
- Why "black box" AI fails in financial advice
- Model transparency vs. commercial IP
- Suitability reporting automation
- **Exercise 7:** Draft an AI-generated suitability explanation for a client report

### [Module 8: From Prototype to Production](Module_08_From_Prototype_to_Production.md)
- Prepare for real-world deployment
- Data sourcing and vendor reconciliation
- Scaling to millions of client-product matches
- Monitoring drift in risk and ESG data
- **Capstone Project:** Design a full Automated Suitability & ESG Preference Matcher for a digital advisory platform
