## 11.1 Home & Furniture

### Industry Characteristics

**Product types:**
- Furniture (chairs, tables, sofas)
- Home decor (pillows, rugs, art)
- Kitchenware (dishes, utensils, appliances)
- Lighting (lamps, fixtures)

**Visual requirements:**
- Lifestyle settings (rooms, spaces)
- Context and scale
- Material accuracy
- Styling and arrangement

### Adaptation Strategy

**Key differences from fashion:**
- Products are static (not worn)
- Context is critical (room settings)
- Scale matters (size relationships)
- Materials are prominent

**Workflow adaptations:**
- **Scene focus:** Emphasize room/space context
- **Scale references:** Include size indicators
- **Material accuracy:** Detailed material descriptions
- **Styling:** Room arrangement and decor

### Prompt Adaptations

**Fashion prompt:**
```
"A fashion model wearing [PRODUCT] in [STYLE], 
professional photography"
```

**Furniture prompt:**
```
"[PRODUCT] in a [ROOM_TYPE] setting, [STYLE] aesthetic, 
[MATERIAL] material, [COLOR], properly scaled, 
lifestyle photography, high quality"
```

**Key additions:**
- Room/space context
- Material specifications
- Scale indicators
- Lifestyle setting

### Best Practices

**For home & furniture:**
- Use room/space references
- Emphasize material accuracy
- Include scale context
- Focus on lifestyle appeal
- Test material representation

## 11.2 Beauty & Cosmetics

### Industry Characteristics

**Product types:**
- Skincare (creams, serums, cleansers)
- Makeup (lipstick, foundation, eyeshadow)
- Fragrance (perfumes, colognes)
- Tools (brushes, applicators)

**Visual requirements:**
- Product accuracy (colors, textures)
- Lifestyle integration
- Brand aesthetic
- Detail and precision

### Adaptation Strategy

**Key differences from fashion:**
- Products are smaller
- Colors are critical
- Texture and finish matter
- Lifestyle integration important

**Workflow adaptations:**
- **Color accuracy:** Precise color matching
- **Texture focus:** Material and finish details
- **Lifestyle integration:** Beauty routines, settings
- **Detail emphasis:** Close-ups, product focus

### Prompt Adaptations

**Fashion prompt:**
```
"A fashion model wearing [PRODUCT], 
professional photography"
```

**Beauty prompt:**
```
"[PRODUCT] in [COLOR], [TEXTURE/FINISH], 
[LIFESTYLE_CONTEXT], product photography, 
high detail, accurate colors, brand aesthetic"
```

**Key additions:**
- Color specifications
- Texture/finish details
- Lifestyle context
- Detail emphasis

### Best Practices

**For beauty & cosmetics:**
- Prioritize color accuracy
- Emphasize texture and finish
- Include lifestyle context
- Focus on product details
- Test color representation

## 11.4 Adapting Prompts Across Industries

### Universal Prompt Structure

**Base template:**
```
[PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION] + [CONTEXT/SCENE] + 
[STYLE/AESTHETIC] + [QUALITY_PARAMETERS] + 
[BRAND_ELEMENTS] + [CONSTRAINTS]
```

### Industry-Specific Adaptations

**Fashion:**
- Focus: Model, styling, garment
- Context: Studio, lifestyle, editorial
- Style: Brand aesthetic, trends
- Quality: Professional photography

**Home & Furniture:**
- Focus: Product, room, materials
- Context: Room settings, spaces
- Style: Interior design, decor
- Quality: Lifestyle photography

**Beauty & Cosmetics:**
- Focus: Product, colors, textures
- Context: Beauty routines, settings
- Style: Brand aesthetic, trends
- Quality: Product photography, detail

**Lifestyle & CPG:**
- Focus: Product, context, appeal
- Context: Usage, lifestyle, settings
- Style: Brand consistency
- Quality: Lifestyle photography

### Adaptation Framework

**Step 1: Understand Industry**
- Product characteristics
- Visual requirements
- Brand expectations
- Use cases

**Step 2: Identify Key Elements**
- What matters most?
- What's different from fashion?
- What needs emphasis?
- What can be simplified?

**Step 3: Adapt Prompt Template**
- Modify base template
- Add industry-specific elements
- Adjust emphasis
- Test and refine

**Step 4: Test and Iterate**
- Generate test images
- Compare with industry standards
- Refine prompts
- Document learnings
