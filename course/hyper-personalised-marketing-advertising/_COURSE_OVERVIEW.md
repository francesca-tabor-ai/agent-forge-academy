# Course Overview: Hyper-Personalised Marketing & Advertising with AI

## Course Information

**Course Level:** Upper Undergraduate / Graduate / Executive  
**Course Format:** Lectures · Case Studies · Applied Labs · Capstone Project

## Prerequisites

- Foundational understanding of marketing principles
- Basic familiarity with data and digital platforms (recommended, not required)

## Course Description

This course examines how Artificial Intelligence enables hyper-personalised marketing and advertising at scale. Students explore how AI systems use data, machine learning, and automation to deliver individualized messages, offers, creatives, and experiences across channels in real time. The course balances strategy, technology, ethics, and hands-on application, preparing learners to design responsible and effective AI-driven personalization strategies.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Explain the principles and evolution of hyper-personalised marketing
2. Apply AI and machine learning concepts to marketing and advertising use cases
3. Design data-driven customer journeys and personalised ad strategies
4. Evaluate performance using appropriate measurement and attribution models
5. Identify ethical, legal, and privacy risks associated with AI personalisation
6. Develop and present an end-to-end AI-powered personalization strategy

## Course Structure

This course is organized into 11 modules, each building upon previous concepts while introducing new dimensions of AI-powered personalization. The course culminates in a capstone project where students develop and present a comprehensive AI-driven personalization strategy.

## Assessment

- **Module Activities & Labs:** 40%
- **Assignments & Case Studies:** 30%
- **Capstone Project:** 30%

## Key Topics Covered

- Evolution from mass marketing to hyper-personalisation
- Data infrastructure and customer data platforms
- AI and machine learning for marketing applications
- Dynamic segmentation and audience intelligence
- AI-generated creative and content optimization
- Multi-channel personalization strategies
- Customer journey orchestration
- Measurement, attribution, and optimization
- Ethics, privacy, and responsible AI
- Personalized pricing and loyalty programs
- Future trends in AI-powered marketing
