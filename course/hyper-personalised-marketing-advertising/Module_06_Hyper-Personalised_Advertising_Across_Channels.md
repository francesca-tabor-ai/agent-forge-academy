## Cross-Channel vs Omnichannel Personalisation

### Cross-Channel Personalisation

**Definition:** Personalizing within each channel independently, with some coordination between channels.

**Characteristics:**
- Channel-specific strategies
- Independent optimization
- Some data sharing
- Coordinated but not unified

**Example:**
- Email: Personalized product recommendations
- Social: Dynamic product ads
- Display: Retargeting campaigns
- Each optimized separately

### Omnichannel Personalisation

**Definition:** Unified, consistent personalization across all channels with shared data and coordinated strategies.

**Characteristics:**
- Unified customer view
- Consistent messaging
- Coordinated timing
- Seamless experience

**Example:**
- Customer browses product on website
- Receives personalized email with same product
- Sees retargeting ad on social media
- Gets push notification from app
- All coordinated, consistent messaging

### Choosing the Right Approach

**Cross-Channel When:**
- Limited data integration
- Channel-specific goals
- Different audiences per channel
- Resource constraints

**Omnichannel When:**
- Unified customer data
- Consistent brand experience priority
- High customer lifetime value
- Advanced technology stack

## Platform Constraints and Walled Gardens

### Walled Garden Environments

**Definition:** Closed platforms that control data, targeting, and measurement within their ecosystem.

**Major Walled Gardens:**
- Google (Search, YouTube, Display)
- Meta (Facebook, Instagram)
- Amazon (Advertising)
- Apple (App Store, Search Ads)
- Microsoft (LinkedIn, Bing)

### Platform Constraints

**1. Data Limitations:**
- Limited data export
- Restricted audience sharing
- Measurement limitations
- Attribution challenges

**2. Targeting Restrictions:**
- Platform-specific targeting
- Privacy restrictions
- Policy limitations
- Reduced granularity

**3. Creative Constraints:**
- Format requirements
- Approval processes
- Policy compliance
- Limited customization

**4. Measurement Challenges:**
- Platform-specific metrics
- Limited cross-platform view
- Attribution differences
- Data discrepancies

### Strategies for Walled Gardens

**1. First-Party Data:**
- Build your own data
- Customer data platforms
- Direct relationships
- Less platform dependence

**2. Multi-Platform Strategy:**
- Diversify across platforms
- Don't rely on single platform
- Test and compare
- Optimize portfolio

**3. Measurement Frameworks:**
- Unified measurement approach
- Incrementality testing
- Multi-touch attribution
- Business outcome focus

**4. Platform Partnerships:**
- Work with platform teams
- Access beta features
- Strategic relationships
- Co-marketing opportunities
