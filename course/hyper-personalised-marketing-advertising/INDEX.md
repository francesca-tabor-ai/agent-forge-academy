# Course Index: Hyper-Personalised Marketing & Advertising with AI

## Module 1: Foundations of Hyper-Personalised Marketing
- From mass marketing to segmentation to 1:1 personalisation
- Limitations of traditional personalization approaches
- Role of AI in scaling relevance and automation
- Overview of the modern personalization ecosystem
- **Activity:** Personalization audit of a real brand or platform

## Module 2: Data Infrastructure for Personalisation
- First-party, second-party, and third-party data
- Behavioral, contextual, transactional, and psychographic data
- Customer Data Platforms (CDPs) and data unification
- Data quality, bias, and governance challenges
- **Lab:** Customer data mapping and architecture design

## Module 3: AI & Machine Learning for Marketers
- Core machine learning concepts (non-technical focus)
- Supervised vs unsupervised learning in marketing
- Recommendation engines and predictive modeling
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) and personalization
- **Assignment:** Translate an AI model into a marketing use case

## Module 4: AI-Driven Segmentation & Audience Intelligence
- Dynamic and real-time segmentation
- Micro-segmentation and personalization at scale
- Lookalike modeling and audience expansion
- Context-aware and intent-based targeting
- **Lab:** Building AI-informed personas from behavioral signals

## Module 5: Personalised Creative & Content with AI
- Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO)
- AI-generated copy, visuals, and video
- Personalisation across emails, ads, and landing pages
- Maintaining brand voice and creative governance
- **Project Milestone:** Personalized creative strategy

## Module 6: Hyper-Personalised Advertising Across Channels
- Search, social, display, video, CTV, and in-app personalization
- AI-driven media buying and bidding strategies
- Cross-channel vs omnichannel personalisation
- Platform constraints and walled-garden environments
- **Case Study:** AI-optimized advertising campaigns

## Module 7: AI-Orchestrated Customer Journeys
- Personalised customer journey design
- Real-time decision engines and next-best-action systems
- Website, app, and e-commerce personalization
- Conversational AI and chat-based marketing
- **Lab:** Journey mapping with AI decision logic

## Module 8: Measurement, Attribution & Optimization
- KPIs for hyper-personalised marketing
- Incrementality and lift measurement
- Multi-touch attribution models
- A/B testing vs AI-driven experimentation
- **Assignment:** Design a personalization measurement framework

## Module 9: Ethics, Privacy & Responsible AI
- Consumer trust, transparency, and consent
- Bias, discrimination, and algorithmic fairness
- Explainability and accountability in AI systems
- Privacy regulations and compliance considerations
- **Discussion:** Is hyper-personalisation manipulation or value creation?

## Module 10: AI in Pricing, Promotions & Loyalty
- Personalized pricing strategies and risks
- AI-driven offers, timing, and promotions
- Loyalty program personalization
- Churn prediction and retention optimization
- **Case Study:** Successes and failures of personalized pricing

## Module 11: The Future of Hyper-Personalised Marketing
- Cookieless advertising and identity solutions
- Predictive and anticipatory personalization
- Autonomous marketing systems and AI agents
- Human-AI collaboration in strategy and creativity
- **Project Milestone:** Future-ready personalization roadmap
