# AI in Pricing, Promotions & Loyalty

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- **personalized pricing strategies Evaluation**: Evaluate personalized pricing strategies and associated risks
- **AI-driven offers, timing, and promotions Development**: Design AI-driven offers, timing, and promotions
- **loyalty program personalization Implementation**: Implement loyalty program personalization

## Introduction

Pricing, promotions, and loyalty programs are powerful tools for personalization, but they also raise significant ethical and business considerations. This module explores how AI enables sophisticated personalization in these areas while navigating the complex trade-offs between profitability, fairness, and customer trust.

## Personalized Pricing Strategies and Risks

### What is Personalized Pricing?

**Definition:** Adjusting prices for individual customers based on their characteristics, behavior, willingness to pay, or other factors.

**Also Known As:**
- Dynamic pricing
- Price discrimination
- Value-based pricing
- Personalized pricing
