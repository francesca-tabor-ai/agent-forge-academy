# Paid AI Ads Readiness

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 6-8 weeks  
**Level:** Senior / Director / Agency Lead  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Experience in paid media, media strategy, or digital advertising

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- **Anticipate Paid**: Anticipate paid AI ad mechanics before platforms formalise them
- **Position Brands**: Position brands as first movers in scarce AI inventory
- **Translate Trust**: Translate trust and content into media advantage
- **Educate Agencies**: Educate agencies and internal teams confidently
- **Protect Budgets**: Protect budgets from reactive, inefficient spend
- **Design, Simulate,**: Design, simulate, and operationalise AI-native media strategies ahead of paid AI ad inventory launches

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Paid AI Ads Will Break the Old Media Model](Module_01_Why_Paid_AI_Ads_Will_Break_the_Old_Media_Model.md)
**From Keywords to Answers**

- How AI recommendations differ from ads
- Why keywords collapse into intent clusters
- The shift from "rank + bid" to "confidence + trust"
- Lessons from early AI recommendation surfaces
- **Practical Output:** Paid AI Ads Threat & Opportunity Brief

### [Module 2: AI Personas & Intent Clusters](Module_02_AI_Personas_and_Intent_Clusters.md)
**Who AI Is Answering For**

- Persona formation inside LLMs
- Intent escalation paths (research → comparison → decision)
- Pre-purchase vs post-purchase AI intents
- How personas differ by model and surface
- **Practical Output:** AI Persona Map and Intent cluster hierarchy

### [Module 3: Organic AI Recommendations as the Pre-Paid Signal](Module_03_Organic_AI_Recommendations_as_the_Pre_Paid_Signal.md)
**Winning Before You Can Buy**

- Share of AI answers as a leading indicator
- Why organic dominance predicts paid efficiency
- Competitive displacement inside AI answers
- Weak signals of future monetisation
- **Practical Output:** AI Recommendation Share Baseline and Priority intent list

### [Module 4: Answer-Level Bidding Strategy](Module_04_Answer_Level_Bidding_Strategy.md)
**Designing the New Auction Logic**

- Answer slots vs keyword inventory
- Persona-weighted bidding
- Contextual confidence thresholds
- Defensive vs offensive answer strategies
- **Practical Output:** Answer-Level Bidding Framework and Hypothetical bid allocation model

### [Module 5: Trust, Citation & Creative Requirements](Module_05_Trust_Citation_and_Creative_Requirements.md)
**What Paid AI Will Actually Reward**

- Citation eligibility and suppression risk
- Trust signals: evidence, reviews, authority, data freshness
- Creative formats: Conversational responses, Visual summaries, Comparison snippets
- Why bad creative damages organic visibility too
- **Practical Output:** AI Ad Creative Readiness Checklist and Trust signal gap analysis

### [Module 6: Simulating AI Ad Inventory & Competition](Module_06_Simulating_AI_Ad_Inventory_and_Competition.md)
**Practicing Before the Game Exists**

- Simulating limited inventory scenarios
- Mapping competitor strengths by intent
- Budget pressure points and scarcity dynamics
- First-mover vs fast-follower strategies
- **Practical Output:** AI Ads Simulation Board and Competitive win/loss scenarios

### [Module 7: Operating Model & Internal Readiness](Module_07_Operating_Model_and_Internal_Readiness.md)
**Avoiding Panic on Day One**

- Working with paid media teams
- Educating agencies on AI-native buying
- Procurement and contract readiness
- Aligning brand, legal, and finance expectations
- **Practical Output:** Paid AI Ads Readiness Playbook and Internal education deck

### [Module 8: Measurement, KPIs & Speed-to-Launch](Module_08_Measurement_KPIs_and_Speed_to_Launch.md)
**What Success Looks Like on Day Zero**

- AI intent coverage readiness
- Trust signal completeness by persona
- Speed-to-launch benchmarks
- Share of organic AI recommendations as pre-paid proxy
- Early ROI narratives without click data
- **Capstone Project:** Complete Paid AI Ads Readiness Plan
