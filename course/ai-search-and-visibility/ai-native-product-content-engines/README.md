## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Product Content Became AI Infrastructure](Module_01_Why_Product_Content_Became_AI_Infrastructure.md)
**From Persuasion to Proof**

- Understand why AI systems evaluate products differently than humans
- Reframe PDPs as machine-consumable evidence layers
- How LLMs read and interpret product data
- Why "brand voice" collapses under AI scrutiny
- Transparency, constraints, and tradeoffs as ranking signals
- **Practical Output:** Product Content Risk Assessment, List of AI trust failures in your current PDPs

### [Module 2: Where AI Gets Product Truth](Module_02_Where_AI_Gets_Product_Truth.md)
**The Real Sources of Recommendation**

- Learn how AI systems source and weight product information
- Identify where your product narrative actually comes from
- Amazon Rufus and marketplace Q&A
- Reviews as objection graphs, not sentiment blobs
- Reddit, forums, and UGC as "truth amplifiers"
- Images and video as evidence, not decoration
- **Practical Output:** Product Truth Source Map, Influence weighting by source type

### [Module 3: AI-Surfaced Product Questions](Module_03_AI_Surfaced_Product_Questions.md)
**What Buyers Ask AI (But Not You)**

- Systematically extract real buyer questions surfaced by AI
- Detect blind spots between brand content and AI queries
- Rufus question patterns
- Review-based objection mining
- Forum skepticism and edge-case use
- Pre-purchase vs post-purchase AI questions
- **Practical Output:** AI Product Question Repository, Coverage gap analysis by SKU

### [Module 4: Scoring Product Pages for AI Trust](Module_04_Scoring_Product_Pages_for_AI_Trust.md)
**Truthfulness, Completeness, Evidence Density**

- Learn how to evaluate PDPs the way AI does
- Replace subjective content reviews with scoring systems
- Truthfulness: claims vs verifiable support
- Completeness: who should buy vs shouldn't
- Evidence density: specs, comparisons, visuals, constraints
- Risk of omission vs over-claiming
- **Practical Output:** AI PDP Scorecard, Baseline scores for priority SKUs

### [Module 5: Designing AI-Readable PDPs](Module_05_Designing_AI_Readable_PDPs.md)
**Structuring Content for Machines and Humans**

- Build PDP architectures that AI can parse confidently
- Balance conversion with clarity and evidence
- Claim–proof pairing
- Comparison tables and decision shortcuts
- Visual evidence (use, scale, materials, failure modes)
- Writing for synthesis, not skimming
- **Practical Output:** AI-Optimised PDP Template, Rebuilt PDP for one flagship product

### [Module 6: Automated Product Content Generation](Module_06_Automated_Product_Content_Generation.md)
**Scaling Without Losing Trust**

- Use AI to generate product content without hallucination or drift
- Implement guardrails and human-in-the-loop systems
- Structured inputs vs freeform generation
- Auto-generation of PDP sections, infographic images, video scripts, UGC prompts
- Version control and content governance
- **Practical Output:** Automated PDP Generation Workflow, Human review checkpoints and escalation rules

### [Module 7: Cross-Channel Consistency](Module_07_Cross_Channel_Consistency.md)
**One Product, Many Surfaces**

- Ensure product truth is consistent across every channel AI reads
- Amazon vs DTC vs marketplace content divergence
- How inconsistencies erode AI trust
- Canonical product truth models
- Syncing updates across platforms
- **Practical Output:** Product Truth Canonical Schema, Channel consistency audit

### [Module 8: Measurement & KPIs](Module_08_Measurement_and_KPIs.md)
**Proving Content Is a Revenue Lever**

- Measure product content performance through an AI lens
- Tie trust improvements to commercial outcomes
- AI product answer completeness score
- Conversion uplift from AI-referred traffic
- Returns and negative review reduction
- Question coverage as a leading indicator
- **Practical Output:** AI Product Content Dashboard, SKU-level performance review framework

### [Module 9: Operating Model & Team Integration](Module_09_Operating_Model_and_Team_Integration.md)
**Making It Work Inside the Org**

- Operationalise AI-native product content across teams
- Avoid silos between content, product, and commerce
- Working with e-commerce and marketplaces
- Partnering with product management
- Directing creative and video teams with AI-first briefs
- Governing agencies and external vendors
- **Capstone Project:** AI-Native Product Content Engine Plan (Standards, Tooling, KPIs, Org workflows, 6–12 month rollout roadmap)
