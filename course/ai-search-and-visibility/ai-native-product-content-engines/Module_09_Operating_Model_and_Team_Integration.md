## 9.1 The Organizational Challenge

### Traditional Silos

**Content Team:**
- Focuses on brand voice
- Creates marketing copy
- Optimizes for conversion
- **Problem:** Doesn't consider AI needs

**Product Team:**
- Manages product specifications
- Owns product data
- Defines features
- **Problem:** Doesn't think about content implications

**E-commerce Team:**
- Manages marketplace listings
- Optimizes for search
- Handles channel requirements
- **Problem:** Doesn't coordinate with content team

**Creative Team:**
- Creates visuals and videos
- Focuses on brand aesthetic
- Optimizes for engagement
- **Problem:** Doesn't consider AI evidence needs

### The Integration Need

**AI-Native Content Requires:**
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Shared understanding of AI needs
- Coordinated content strategy
- Unified product truth

## 9.6 The AI-Native Content Operating Model

### Organizational Structure

**Option 1: Centralized Team**
- Dedicated AI-native content team
- Owns all product content
- Coordinates with other teams
- **Pros:** Consistency, expertise, efficiency
- **Cons:** May create silos

**Option 2: Embedded Model**
- AI-native content experts embedded in teams
- Shared standards and processes
- Coordinated execution
- **Pros:** Integration, ownership
- **Cons:** Consistency challenges

**Option 3: Hybrid Model**
- Central team for strategy and standards
- Embedded experts for execution
- Regular coordination
- **Pros:** Balance of consistency and integration
- **Cons:** Coordination complexity

### Processes and Workflows

**1. Content Creation Workflow**
- Product truth definition
- Content generation
- Quality review
- Approval and deployment

**2. Update Workflow**
- Change identification

**3. Quality Control Workflow**
