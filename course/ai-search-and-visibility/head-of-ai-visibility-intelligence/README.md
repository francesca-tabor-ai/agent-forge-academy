# Head of AI Visibility & Intelligence

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8-10 weeks  
**Level:** Senior / Director / Executive  
**Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Marketing leadership experience, understanding of SEO/content/PR fundamentals, strategic thinking

### [Module 1: The AI Visibility Paradigm Shift](Module_01_The_AI_Visibility_Paradigm_Shift.md)

- How LLMs generate answers (training, retrieval, synthesis)
- The collapse of SEO, PR, content, and CRO into AI perception
- **Exercise 1:** AI Visibility Landscape Map for your brand

### [Module 2: AI Ecosystem & Model-Specific Dynamics](Module_02_AI_Ecosystem_Model_Specific_Dynamics.md)
- How Visibility Differs by Platform
- ChatGPT: synthesis-first, citation-light
- Gemini: search + knowledge graph bias
- Perplexity: citation-heavy, competitive displacement
- Amazon Rufus: product truth and review gravity
- **Exercise 2:** Platform-specific visibility risk matrix

### [Module 3: Intent Clusters & AI Question Mapping](Module_03_Intent_Clusters_AI_Question_Mapping.md)
- What AI Is Actually Asked
- Moving beyond keywords to intent clusters
- Informational vs evaluative vs transactional AI prompts
- Persona-driven question patterns
- Hidden intents brands systematically miss
- **Exercise 3:** AI Intent Cluster Map

### [Module 4: AI Answer Graphs](Module_04_AI_Answer_Graphs.md)
- The Core Intelligence Asset
- Modeling AI answers as systems, not outputs
- Answer variants and versioning
- Detecting answer drift vs true regression
- Mapping competitors inside AI answers
- **Exercise 4:** Prototype AI Answer Graph for critical intents

### [Module 5: Monitoring, Volatility & Alerting](Module_05_Monitoring_Volatility_Alerting.md)
- Seeing Change Before It Hurts Revenue
- Building continuous AI answer monitoring
- Visibility volatility vs normal AI variance
- Competitive displacement detection
- Citation loss and trust decay signals
- **Exercise 5:** AI Visibility Monitoring Framework

### [Module 6: Remediation Strategy & Prioritisation](Module_06_Remediation_Strategy_Prioritisation.md)
- Turning Insight into Action
- Content gaps, product data gaps, evidence gaps, PR/authority gaps
- When to update FAQs vs PDPs vs imagery vs third-party content
- Aligning fixes with AI trust mechanisms
- Avoiding content spam and misaligned fixes
- **Exercise 6:** AI Visibility Remediation Playbook

### [Module 7: Cross-Functional Orchestration](Module_07_Cross_Functional_Orchestration.md)
- Making the Org Actually Move
- Working with SEO & content teams
- Working with product marketing & ecommerce
- Working with PR and comms
- Integrating external AI visibility vendors
- **Exercise 7:** RACI model and operating cadence

### [Module 8: Measurement & KPIs](Module_08_Measurement_KPIs.md)
- What to Report to the CMO and Board
- Share of AI answers by intent cluster
- Visibility volatility index
- Competitive displacement events
- Time-to-detection benchmarks
- **Exercise 8:** Executive AI Visibility Dashboard

### [Module 9: Tooling & Build vs Buy Decisions](Module_09_Tooling_Build_vs_Buy_Decisions.md)
- Stack Design Without Vendor Lock-In
- Audits vs continuous systems
- Vendor strengths and blind spots
- When to build internal Answer Graphs
- Data governance and IP considerations
- **Exercise 9:** AI Visibility Tech Stack Blueprint

### [Module 10: The 12-Month AI Visibility Roadmap](Module_10_The_12_Month_AI_Visibility_Roadmap.md)
