# Leadership OS: Scaling Data & AI Organizations

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 6–8 weeks (cohort-based, executive-style delivery)  
**Level:** Executive / Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours per week  
**Primary Role:** Director / VP of Data & AI  
**Prerequisites:** Senior leadership experience, understanding of data/AI fundamentals, business strategy experience

## Course Objective

This course teaches how to design and operate a **Leadership & Strategy Operating System** — the strategic, organizational, and execution framework that allows data and AI leaders to move beyond delivery and into **enterprise-level impact**.

**Outcome:** Learners can **lead data and AI organizations end-to-end**, aligning technology, product, and analytics with measurable business outcomes.

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: The Role of the Data & AI Executive](Module_01_The_Role_of_the_Data_AI_Executive.md)
**Theme:** From technical leader to enterprise leader
- Evolution of the Data & AI leadership role
- Owning outcomes vs delivering capabilities
- Partnering with Product, Engineering, Finance, and Operations
- Data & AI as competitive advantage
- Common leadership failure modes
- **Hands-on:** Assess current leadership scope vs required enterprise scope

### [Module 2: Data & AI Strategy Design](Module_02_Data_AI_Strategy_Design.md)
**Theme:** Strategy before solutions
- Business strategy vs data & AI strategy
- Identifying high-value AI and analytics bets
- Horizon planning (now / next / later)
- Build vs buy vs partner decisions
- Aligning GenAI, ML, analytics, and data platforms
- **Hands-on:** Design a 3-year data & AI strategy aligned to business goals

### [Module 3: Operating Models & Org Design](Module_03_Operating_Models_Org_Design.md)
**Theme:** Structure determines outcomes
- Centralized, federated, and hybrid models
- Data product teams vs functional teams
- Platform vs embedded roles
- Talent mix and career ladders
- Decision rights and accountability
- **Hands-on:** Design an operating model for a multi-team data organization

### [Module 4: KPI Trees, OKRs & Strategic Alignment](Module_04_KPI_Trees_OKRs_Strategic_Alignment.md)
**Theme:** Turning strategy into execution
- KPI trees and value decomposition
- Aligning metrics across product, data, and business
- OKRs for data and AI teams
- Avoiding metric theater
- Linking delivery to financial outcomes
- **Hands-on:** Build a KPI tree and OKRs for a strategic initiative

### [Module 5: Portfolio Prioritization & Investment Decisions](Module_05_Portfolio_Prioritization_Investment_Decisions.md)
**Theme:** Choosing what not to do
- Managing a portfolio of data, ML, and AI initiatives
- Prioritization frameworks (impact vs effort, risk-adjusted ROI)
- Balancing innovation and reliability
- Capacity planning and sequencing
- Killing projects and reallocating investment
- **Hands-on:** Prioritize a real-world portfolio of data & AI initiatives

### [Module 6: Communicating AI Value to Executives](Module_06_Communicating_AI_Value_to_Executives.md)
**Theme:** Influence, not explanation
- Translating technical work into business language
- Financial framing of data & AI impact
- Executive storytelling and decision memos
- Managing expectations and uncertainty
- Board-level AI conversations
- **Hands-on:** Present a data & AI initiative to a mock executive committee

### [Module 7: Scaling Culture, Talent & Trust](Module_07_Scaling_Culture_Talent_Trust.md)
**Theme:** Leadership beyond structure
- Building a data-driven culture
- Talent development and succession planning
- Incentives and performance management
- Partnering with risk, legal, and compliance
- Sustaining momentum as the org scales
- **Hands-on:** Design a leadership plan for scaling culture and talent

### [Capstone Project: Build a Leadership OS](Module_08_Capstone_Project_Build_a_Leadership_OS.md)
**Theme:** End-to-end executive data & AI strategy
- Define a business context and strategic goals
- Design a data & AI strategy and operating model
- Build KPI trees and OKRs
- Prioritize a portfolio of initiatives
- Prepare an executive communication plan
- **Final Deliverables:** Complete strategy document, org design, KPI tree, portfolio roadmap, executive presentation
