## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Traditional Distribution Analytics No Longer Work](Module_01_Why_Traditional_Distribution_Analytics_No_Longer_Work.md)
- Understand the limits of backward-looking sales and flow reporting
- Why flows lag investor intent
- The decline of product-push distribution
- Advisor behavior vs. end-investor behavior
- **Exercise 1:** Identify where current distribution dashboards fail to explain inflows

### [Module 2: Alternative Data for Distribution Insight](Module_02_Alternative_Data_for_Distribution_Insight.md)
- Learn how non-traditional data reveals early demand signals
- Platform traffic and fund search behavior
- Investment forum sentiment and narratives
- Social media mood vs. actual flows
- **Exercise 2:** Map alternative data sources to potential inflow drivers

### [Module 3: Predictive Inflow Forecaster](Module_03_Predictive_Inflow_Forecaster.md)
- Anticipate which funds and categories will attract capital
- Signal aggregation and weighting
- Linking search intent to allocation behavior
- Separating noise from durable trends
- **Exercise 3:** Design a simple inflow forecast model using search and sentiment data

### [Module 4: Automated Peer Benchmarking Engine](Module_04_Automated_Peer_Benchmarking_Engine.md)
- Diagnose why a fund underperforms its peers in distribution
- Defining the true peer group
- Fees (TERs), performance, and rating asymmetries
- When 5-star ratings don't translate into flows
- **Exercise 4:** Analyze a hypothetical fund with weak inflows despite strong performance

### [Module 5: From Insight to Actionable Distribution Strategy](Module_05_From_Insight_to_Actionable_Distribution_Strategy.md)
- Turn analytics into concrete sales and marketing actions
- Prioritizing levers: fees, positioning, channels
- Advisor vs. platform-driven distribution
- Product re-positioning vs. product rationalization
- **Exercise 5:** Create an action plan based on AI-generated benchmarking insights

### [Module 6: Narrative Performance Generator](Module_06_Narrative_Performance_Generator.md)
- Enable advice-led, not product-led, marketing
- Translating quantitative data into human narratives
- Explaining performance in different market regimes
- Tailoring messages for advisors vs. end clients
- **Exercise 6:** Generate three different performance narratives for the same fund and market context

### [Module 7: Governance, Compliance & Trust in Distribution AI](Module_07_Governance_Compliance_and_Trust_in_Distribution_AI.md)
- Ensure insights and narratives remain compliant and credible
- Marketing vs. advice boundaries
- Preventing selective disclosure and over-claiming
- Explainability of AI-generated insights
- **Exercise 7:** Design a compliance review process for AI-generated marketing narratives

### [Module 8: Building the Distribution Intelligence Platform](Module_08_Building_the_Distribution_Intelligence_Platform.md)
- Scale from analytics tools to a strategic capability
- Integrating forecasting, benchmarking, and narratives
- Dashboards for sales, marketing, and product teams
- Measuring impact: inflows, conversion, advisor engagement
- **Capstone Project:** Design a Distribution & Marketing Intelligence platform for an asset manager
