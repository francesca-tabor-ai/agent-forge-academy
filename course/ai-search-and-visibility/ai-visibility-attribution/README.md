# AI Visibility Attribution

**Role:** Director of AI Attribution & Measurement  
**Course Level:** Senior / Director / Executive  
**Duration:** 9 modules (8–10 weeks)  
**Outcome:** Ability to design attribution systems that prove how AI-driven discovery shapes intent, drives qualified demand, and influences revenue, pipeline, and LTV.

## Course Overview

This course teaches you how to design and implement attribution systems that accurately measure AI's impact on business outcomes. You'll learn why traditional attribution models fail for AI, how to track AI-originated intent, and how to connect AI influence to revenue, pipeline, and LTV in ways that executives and boards will trust.

## Modules

1. [Why AI Breaks Traditional Attribution](Module_01_Why_AI_Breaks_Traditional_Attribution.md) - Understanding why clicks are the wrong primitive
2. [AI Discovery → Intent Formation](Module_02_AI_Discovery_Intent_Formation.md) - What happens before the visit
3. [Designing AI-to-Site Attribution Models](Module_03_Designing_AI_to_Site_Attribution_Models.md) - From answer to outcome
4. [Tracking Intent Continuation On-Site](Module_04_Tracking_Intent_Continuation_On_Site.md) - Did the conversation continue?
5. [Measuring Qualified Demand](Module_05_Measuring_Qualified_Demand.md) - Quality beats volume
6. [Revenue, Pipeline & LTV Attribution](Module_06_Revenue_Pipeline_LTV_Attribution.md) - Making AI visible in the P&L
7. [Cost Efficiency & ROI](Module_07_Cost_Efficiency_ROI.md) - What AI really costs (and saves)
8. [Executive & Board-Level Reporting](Module_08_Executive_Board_Level_Reporting.md) - Making AI measurement credible
9. [Operating Model & Data Governance](Module_09_Operating_Model_Data_Governance.md) - Making attribution stick

## Graduate Profile

Graduates of this course can:

- Prove AI's role in driving revenue and demand quality
- Defend AI budgets with credible data
- Replace click-based thinking with intent-based measurement
- Align marketing, finance, and leadership around AI impact
- Prepare the organisation for paid AI attribution models
