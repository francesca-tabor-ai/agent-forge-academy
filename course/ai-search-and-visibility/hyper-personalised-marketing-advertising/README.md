# Hyper-Personalised Marketing & Advertising with AI

## Course Overview

This course examines how Artificial Intelligence enables hyper-personalised marketing and advertising at scale. Students explore how AI systems use data, machine learning, and automation to deliver individualized messages, offers, creatives, and experiences across channels in real time. The course balances strategy, technology, ethics, and hands-on application, preparing learners to design responsible and effective AI-driven personalization strategies.

**Course Level:** Upper Undergraduate / Graduate / Executive  
**Course Format:** Lectures · Case Studies · Applied Labs · Capstone Project

## Prerequisites

- Foundational understanding of marketing principles
- Basic familiarity with data and digital platforms (recommended, not required)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Explain the principles and evolution of hyper-personalised marketing
2. Apply AI and machine learning concepts to marketing and advertising use cases
3. Design data-driven customer journeys and personalised ad strategies
4. Evaluate performance using appropriate measurement and attribution models
5. Identify ethical, legal, and privacy risks associated with AI personalisation
6. Develop and present an end-to-end AI-powered personalization strategy

## Course Structure

This course consists of 11 modules:

1. **Foundations of Hyper-Personalised Marketing** - Evolution from mass marketing to AI-powered personalization
2. **Data Infrastructure for Personalisation** - Data types, CDPs, and data governance
3. **AI & Machine Learning for Marketers** - ML concepts, recommendation engines, and NLP
4. **AI-Driven Segmentation & Audience Intelligence** - Dynamic segmentation and lookalike modeling
5. **Personalised Creative & Content with AI** - DCO, AI-generated content, and brand governance
6. **Hyper-Personalised Advertising Across Channels** - Search, social, display, video, CTV, and in-app
7. **AI-Orchestrated Customer Journeys** - Real-time decision engines and next-best-action systems
8. **Measurement, Attribution & Optimization** - KPIs, incrementality, and AI-driven experimentation
9. **Ethics, Privacy & Responsible AI** - Trust, transparency, bias, and compliance
10. **AI in Pricing, Promotions & Loyalty** - Personalized pricing, offers, and retention
11. **The Future of Hyper-Personalised Marketing** - Cookieless advertising, predictive personalization, and autonomous systems
