- **11 Labs, Arcads** - Voice synthesis

- **n8n, Zapier** - Workflow automation
- **LangGraph, AutoGen** - Multi-agent systems
- **Custom Python** - Pipeline scripts

### Quality Control (Module 7)
- **Brand Voice Systems** - Voice codification
- **HITL Patterns** - Human checkpoints
- **Verification Agents** - Multi-stage checks
