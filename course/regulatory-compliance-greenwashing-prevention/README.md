# Regulatory Compliance & Greenwashing Prevention

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks (can be delivered over 4-8 weeks)

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Greenwashing Is Now a Systemic Risk](Module_01_Why_Greenwashing_Is_Now_a_Systemic_Risk.md)
- Understand why disclosure inconsistency has become a regulatory flashpoint
- From marketing risk to regulatory enforcement
- Supervisory focus on ESG misrepresentation
- Why manual controls no longer scale
- **Exercise 1:** Identify typical greenwashing risk points across fund disclosures

### [Module 2: The Compliance Surface: Mapping All External Disclosures](Module_02_The_Compliance_Surface_Mapping_All_External_Disclosures.md)
- Define the full universe of documents that must be aligned
- SFDR disclosures, EET files, prospectuses, fact sheets, websites
- Structured vs. narrative ESG statements
- Jurisdiction-specific disclosure variations
- **Exercise 2:** Create a disclosure map for a single fund across all channels

### [Module 3: Cross-Document Consistency Engine](Module_03_Cross_Document_Consistency_Engine.md)
- Use AI to ensure all disclosures tell the same story
- NLP for semantic comparison across documents
- Detecting contradictions, omissions, and overstatements
- Aligning qualitative claims with quantitative data
- **Exercise 3:** Design an AI check for consistency between EET fields and a marketing fact sheet

### [Module 4: Preventing Greenwashing by Design](Module_04_Preventing_Greenwashing_by_Design.md)
- Move from reactive checks to preventive controls
- Embedding ESG claims into governed data models
- Linking sustainability objectives to measurable indicators
- Automated escalation of high-risk ESG claims
- **Exercise 4:** Build a preventive workflow for approving ESG marketing language

### [Module 5: Regulatory Horizon Scanner: Seeing What's Coming](Module_05_Regulatory_Horizon_Scanner_Seeing_Whats_Coming.md)
- Anticipate regulatory change before it becomes mandatory
- Monitoring global regulatory publications
- Extracting obligations from legal and technical texts
- Differentiating signal vs. regulatory noise
- **Exercise 5:** Define how a new regulation would impact current fund data processes

### [Module 6: Translating Regulation into Operational Change](Module_06_Translating_Regulation_into_Operational_Change.md)
- Turn legal text into actionable data and process updates
- Regulatory interpretation vs. implementation
- Updating data models, templates, and controls
- Managing overlapping and conflicting regimes
- **Exercise 6:** Create an AI-generated impact assessment for a regulatory update

### [Module 7: Auditability, Explainability & Regulatory Trust](Module_07_Auditability_Explainability_and_Regulatory_Trust.md)
- Ensure AI-assisted compliance stands up to scrutiny
- Traceability of AI findings
- "Show me the source" for detected inconsistencies
- Preparing for regulatory inspections
- **Exercise 7:** Draft an audit-ready report explaining an AI-flagged greenwashing risk

### [Module 8: Building a Continuous Compliance Platform](Module_08_Building_a_Continuous_Compliance_Platform.md)
- Move from periodic reviews to continuous assurance
- Integrating consistency checks and horizon scanning
- Measuring compliance effectiveness
- Scaling across products, jurisdictions, and languages
- **Capstone Project:** Design a Regulatory Compliance & Greenwashing Prevention platform for an asset manager
