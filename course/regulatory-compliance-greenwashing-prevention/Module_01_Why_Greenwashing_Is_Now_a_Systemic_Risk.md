## Lesson 1.1: From Marketing Risk to Regulatory Enforcement

### Historical Context

**Early Stage**
- Greenwashing as marketing concern
- Reputational risk
- Consumer protection focus
- Limited regulatory oversight

**Current State**
- Regulatory enforcement priority
- Active supervision
- Financial penalties
- Legal consequences

### Regulatory Evolution

**Key Milestones**
- SFDR implementation (EU)
- SEC ESG disclosure rules (US)
- FCA sustainability requirements (UK)
- Global regulatory alignment

**Enforcement Trends**
- Increased fines
- More frequent inspections
- Stricter penalties
- Cross-jurisdictional coordination

---

## Lesson 1.2: Supervisory Focus on ESG Misrepresentation

### Regulatory Priorities

**Focus Areas**
- ESG claims accuracy
- Disclosure consistency
- Evidence-based statements
- Measurable objectives

**Supervisory Actions**
- Targeted inspections
- Document reviews
- Data verification
- Enforcement actions

### ESG Misrepresentation Types

**Common Issues**
- Exaggerated sustainability claims
- Inconsistent disclosures
- Missing evidence
- Unsubstantiated objectives

## Lesson 1.3: Why Manual Controls No Longer Scale

### Manual Control Limitations

**Scale Challenges**
- Hundreds of funds
- Thousands of documents
- Multiple jurisdictions
- Frequent updates

**Quality Issues**
- Human error
- Inconsistent application
- Time-consuming
- Resource-intensive

### Automation Necessity

**Why AI is Essential**
- Volume of documents
- Complexity of regulations
- Speed of regulatory change
- Consistency requirements
