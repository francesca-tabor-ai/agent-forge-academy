# Measuring AI ROI in Operations
## Quantify, Communicate, and Defend the Financial Value of AI Systems

### Course Goal

Enable AI teams to quantify, communicate, and defend the financial value of AI systems in operational environments.

### Course Outcome

Participants can link AI-driven decisions directly to financial outcomes and present results that finance and leadership trust.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Financial rigor beats technical cleverness**
- **Conservative assumptions beat optimistic projections**
- **Attribution clarity beats correlation claims**
- **Finance trust beats technical validation**
- **Cash impact beats model accuracy**

**Tone:** analytical, financially rigorous, credibility-focused  
**Energy:** CFO review meeting, board presentation, finance audit  
**Outcome:** AI initiatives that get funded, scaled, and defended

---

## COURSE STRUCTURE

8 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

Duration: 3–4 weeks  
Format: Lectures + financial modeling labs + exec-style reviews  
Prerequisites: Shipped or operated at least one AI system

---

## DELIVERY FORMAT

- 8 × 90-minute live sessions
- Financial modeling labs with Excel/Python
- Executive-style ROI reviews
- Real case studies from operations
- Facilitated discussion and role plays

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

Within 90 days:

- ROI models built for AI initiatives
- Attribution logic documented and defensible
- Executive-ready narratives that pass finance scrutiny
- Confidence intervals and conservative assumptions in place
- AI projects funded based on financial rigor

---

## THE REAL GOAL

Not "better AI metrics."

But:

**"If finance doesn't care, your AI doesn't matter."**

Participants learn to build financial cases that finance and leadership trust, linking AI decisions directly to cash, working capital, and profit.

---

## KEY INSIGHT (EXPLICITLY TAUGHT)

**If finance doesn't care, your AI doesn't matter.**

This course teaches participants to quantify AI value in financial terms that matter to CFOs, boards, and investors — not just technical metrics that impress engineers.

---

## COURSE MODULES

### Module 1: Why Most AI ROI Claims Fail
Reset expectations about what counts as value.

### Module 2: Inventory Turns as a Value Lever
Translate operational improvement into cash.

### Module 3: Service Level Improvement
Quantify reliability in revenue terms.

### Module 4: Cost-to-Serve
Measure efficiency honestly.

### Module 5: Working Capital Impact
Tie AI to the balance sheet.

### Module 6: Experimental Design in Operations
Prove causality.

### Module 7: Time-to-Value & Payback Period
Speak the CFO's language.

### Module 8: Communicating ROI to Finance
Get buy-in.

### Capstone Project
Build a complete ROI model for a real or simulated AI system.

---

## ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE

- **Financial Modeling Labs (50%):** Hands-on ROI calculations per module
- **Capstone Project (40%):** Complete ROI model with attribution logic
- **Role Play Exercises (10%):** Defending ROI in finance reviews

**Success Criteria:** ROI model that survives finance scrutiny
