# Mathematical Systems of the Hospitality Industry: Quantitative Foundations of Revenue, Operations, Experience, and Scale

## Course Description

This course reveals how mathematical models silently govern pricing, capacity, service quality, customer behavior, and profitability in hospitality. Students learn to translate real-world hospitality problems into formal mathematical structures and to interpret quantitative outputs for strategic decision-making.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will be able to:

- Identify core mathematical principles underlying hospitality operations and strategy
- Formulate industry problems using equations, probabilistic models, and optimization frameworks
- Interpret metrics such as RevPAR, GOPPAR, CLV, and service-level targets mathematically
- Evaluate trade-offs between revenue, cost, risk, and guest experience
- Apply data-driven reasoning to pricing, staffing, forecasting, and personalization

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Hospitality as a Quantitative System
**Focus:** Framing the industry mathematically

**Key Topics:** Hospitality as a capacity-constrained service system, stocks vs. flows, deterministic vs. stochastic demand, units and normalization

**Core Mathematics:** Ratios and normalization, dimensional analysis, system boundaries

### Module 2: Demand, Price, and Consumer Choice
**Focus:** How guests respond to prices and offers

**Key Topics:** Demand curves, price elasticity, discrete choice, rate fences

**Core Mathematics:** Elasticity, logit choice models, utility functions

### Module 3: Revenue Management & Optimization
**Focus:** Maximizing revenue under uncertainty

**Key Topics:** Capacity constraints, overbooking, sell-up vs. sell-out, revenue vs. profit optimization

**Core Mathematics:** Newsvendor models, constrained optimization, expected value

### Module 4: Forecasting & Uncertainty
**Focus:** Predicting demand and measuring error

**Key Topics:** Time-series patterns, forecast accuracy, demand distributions, risk-aware planning

**Core Mathematics:** ARIMA-style models, probability distributions, error metrics

### Module 5: Operations, Queues, and Service Levels
**Focus:** Managing time, waits, and staffing

**Key Topics:** Arrival processes, waiting time vs. service cost, staffing for variability, service-level guarantees

**Core Mathematics:** Queueing theory, Little's Law, utilization ratios

### Module 6: Cost Structures & Profitability
**Focus:** Understanding where money is made (or lost)

**Key Topics:** Fixed vs. variable costs, contribution margins, break-even analysis, operating leverage

**Core Mathematics:** Linear cost models, break-even equations, profit functions

### Module 7: Customer Lifetime Value & Loyalty
**Focus:** Long-term economics of guest relationships

**Key Topics:** Retention vs. acquisition, discounting, behavioral drivers, value of personalization

**Core Mathematics:** Discounted cash flow, survival probabilities, expected value

### Module 8: Experience, Psychology, and Measurement
**Focus:** Quantifying subjective experience

**Key Topics:** Latent variables, survey scaling, review sentiment, experience vs. efficiency trade-offs

**Core Mathematics:** Weighted indices, factor models, text-based sentiment scoring

### Module 9: Data Science & AI in Hospitality
**Focus:** Learning systems at scale

**Key Topics:** Recommendation systems, classification and prediction, experimentation, algorithmic bias

**Core Mathematics:** Matrix factorization, logistic regression, hypothesis testing

### Module 10: Physical Systems & Sustainability
**Focus:** Energy, space, and environmental efficiency

**Key Topics:** Buildings as thermodynamic systems, energy intensity metrics, sustainability trade-offs

**Core Mathematics:** Heat transfer equations, normalized efficiency ratios, optimization

### Module 11: Integrated Systems Thinking
**Focus:** Connecting all models into one system

**Key Topics:** Feedback loops, revenue-service-cost interactions, short-term vs. long-term optimization

**Core Mathematics:** Systems dynamics, multi-objective optimization, sensitivity analysis

### Capstone Project
**Objective:** Model a real hospitality operation as a mathematical system, integrating demand forecasting, pricing, capacity, staffing, service levels, and customer value.

**Deliverable:** A quantitative model + executive interpretation
