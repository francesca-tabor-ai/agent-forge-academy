## 3.1 Intent Depth Scoring

### Understanding Intent Depth

**Intent Depth Levels:**

#### Level 1: Awareness (Low Intent)
- Persona is aware of problem but not actively seeking solution
- Generic questions, no specific constraints
- No urgency indicators
- Low willingness to pay

**Signals:**
- "What is [product category]?"
- "How does [product category] work?"
- Generic browsing behavior
- No specific use case mentioned

#### Level 2: Interest (Medium Intent)
- Persona is interested but not committed
- Some specific questions, vague constraints
- Moderate urgency
- Uncertain willingness to pay

**Signals:**
- "What are the best [product category]?"
- "I'm considering [product category]"
- Some use case specificity
- Comparison shopping

#### Level 3: Consideration (High Intent)
- Persona is actively considering solutions
- Specific questions, clear constraints
- High urgency indicators
- Clear willingness to pay signals

**Signals:**
- "I need [product category] that [specific constraint]"
- "What's the best [product category] for [specific use case]?"
- Clear outcome expectations
- Time-bound needs

#### Level 4: Decision (Very High Intent)
- Persona is ready to buy
- Very specific requirements
- Urgent need
- Strong willingness to pay

**Signals:**
- "I need to implement [product category] by [date]"
- "What's the fastest way to [outcome] with [product category]?"
- Budget constraints mentioned
- Decision criteria defined

### Intent Depth Scoring Framework

**Scoring Criteria (1-10 scale):**

1. **Specificity (0-2 points)**
   - 0: Generic questions
   - 1: Some specificity
   - 2: Highly specific use case

2. **Constraint Clarity (0-2 points)**
   - 0: No constraints mentioned
   - 1: Vague constraints
   - 2: Clear, specific constraints

3. **Outcome Definition (0-2 points)**
   - 0: No outcome mentioned
   - 1: Vague outcome
   - 2: Clear, measurable outcome

4. **Urgency (0-2 points)**
   - 0: No urgency
   - 1: Moderate urgency
   - 2: High urgency

5. **Willingness to Pay (0-2 points)**
   - 0: Price-only focus
   - 1: Budget mentioned but flexible
   - 2: Value-focused, willing to pay for fit

**Scoring Interpretation:**
- 8-10: Very High Intent (Priority 1)
- 6-7: High Intent (Priority 2)
- 4-5: Medium Intent (Priority 3)
- 0-3: Low Intent (Monitor only)

## 3.2 Constraint-Driven Buying

### Types of Constraints

#### 1. Budget Constraints
**Characteristics:**
- Clear budget limitations
- Price sensitivity indicators
- Value-focused (not just cheap)
- Willing to trade features for price

**Example:**
```
Constraint: "$50/month budget, need basic features"
Analysis:
- Clear budget limit
- Willing to accept feature tradeoffs
- Value-focused (not just cheapest)
- High intent if constraint is met
```

#### 2. Time Constraints
**Characteristics:**
- Urgent implementation needs
- Time-bound goals
- Quick setup requirements
- Efficiency-focused

**Example:**
```
Constraint: "Need to implement by end of quarter"
Analysis:
- High urgency
- Willing to pay for speed
- Implementation support needed
- High intent if time constraint met
```

#### 3. Skill Constraints
**Characteristics:**
- Limited technical skills
- Self-service preference
- Need for simplicity
- Training or support needs

**Example:**
```
Constraint: "Non-technical, need something I can set up myself"
Analysis:
- Simplicity is key
- Self-service required
- Willing to pay for ease of use
- High intent if skill constraint met
```

#### 4. Resource Constraints
**Characteristics:**
- Limited team size
- Solo or small team
- Limited infrastructure
- Need for efficiency

**Example:**
```
Constraint: "Solo consultant, need everything in one tool"
Analysis:
- Efficiency is critical
- All-in-one preferred
- Willing to pay for consolidation
- High intent if resource constraint met
```

### Constraint-Driven Persona Profiling

**Step 1: Identify Primary Constraint**
- Which constraint is most important?
- Is constraint flexible or fixed?
- How does constraint affect buying decision?

**Step 2: Understand Constraint Tradeoffs**
- What is persona willing to trade for constraint?
- Which features can be sacrificed?
- What outcomes are non-negotiable?

**Step 3: Design for Constraints**
- How can product address primary constraint?
- What messaging resonates with constraint?
- How can pricing align with constraint?

## 3.3 Tradeoffs Personas Explicitly Accept

### Understanding Explicit Tradeoffs

**Explicit Tradeoffs Indicate:**
- Persona has clear priorities
- Persona understands constraints
- Persona is realistic about limitations
- Persona is ready to make decisions

### Common Tradeoff Patterns

#### 1. Features vs Price
**Pattern:**
- Persona accepts fewer features for lower price
- Or: Persona pays more for specific features
- Tradeoff is explicit and accepted

**Example:**
```
Tradeoff: "I don't need advanced analytics, just basic reporting at a lower price"
Analysis:
- Clear feature prioritization
- Price sensitivity with flexibility
- Willing to accept limitations
- High intent if tradeoff is met
```

#### 2. Speed vs Customization
**Pattern:**
- Persona accepts less customization for faster implementation
- Or: Persona accepts longer setup for more control
- Tradeoff reflects urgency vs control needs

**Example:**
```
Tradeoff: "I need something I can set up this week, even if it's not perfectly customized"
Analysis:
- Urgency over perfection
- Willing to accept limitations
- Implementation speed is priority
- High intent if speed is delivered
```

#### 3. Simplicity vs Power
**Pattern:**
- Persona accepts less power for simplicity
- Or: Persona accepts complexity for advanced features
- Tradeoff reflects skill level and needs

**Example:**
```
Tradeoff: "I'd rather have something simple I can use than something powerful I can't figure out"
Analysis:
- Simplicity is priority
- Skill constraint acknowledged
- Willing to accept feature limitations
- High intent if simplicity is delivered
```

#### 4. Support vs Self-Service
**Pattern:**
- Persona accepts less support for self-service
- Or: Persona pays more for hand-holding
- Tradeoff reflects resource and skill constraints

**Example:**
```
Tradeoff: "I prefer to figure it out myself, even if it takes longer"
Analysis:
- Self-service preference
- Resource constraint (time over money)
- Willing to accept learning curve
- High intent if self-service is possible
```

### Using Tradeoffs for Persona Alignment

**Step 1: Identify Explicit Tradeoffs**
- What tradeoffs does persona explicitly mention?
- Which tradeoffs are acceptable?
- What tradeoffs are deal-breakers?

**Step 2: Design for Tradeoffs**
- How can product deliver on accepted tradeoffs?
- What messaging acknowledges tradeoffs?
- How can pricing reflect tradeoffs?

**Step 3: Validate Tradeoffs**
- Test if tradeoffs are actually acceptable
- Measure conversion with tradeoff-aligned offers
- Refine based on results

## 3.4 Early LTV Signals from AI-Discovered Personas

### LTV Indicators in Persona Signals

#### 1. Expansion Potential
**Signals:**
- Persona mentions growth plans
- Persona has scaling needs
- Persona values solutions that grow with them
- Persona has multiple use cases

**Example:**
```
Signal: "I'm starting solo but plan to hire a team in 6 months"
LTV Indicator:
- Growth trajectory
- Expansion potential
- Willing to pay for scalability
- Higher LTV than static persona
```

#### 2. Integration Needs
**Signals:**
- Persona mentions other tools
- Persona values integration
- Persona has ecosystem needs
- Persona is building workflows

**Example:**
```
Signal: "I need something that integrates with QuickBooks and Shopify"
LTV Indicator:
- Ecosystem engagement
- Workflow building
- Higher switching costs
- Higher retention potential
```

#### 3. Outcome Commitment
**Signals:**
- Persona has clear success metrics
- Persona is outcome-focused
- Persona values results over features
- Persona is willing to invest in outcomes

**Example:**
```
Signal: "I need to reduce support tickets by 50% in Q1"
LTV Indicator:
- Outcome commitment
- Success metrics defined
- Willing to pay for results
- Higher retention if outcomes delivered
```

#### 4. Constraint Evolution
**Signals:**
- Persona's constraints may evolve
- Persona has growth trajectory
- Persona values solutions that adapt
- Persona is building for scale

**Example:**
```
Signal: "I have $50/month budget now but expect to grow"
LTV Indicator:
- Budget growth potential
- Scaling trajectory
- Willing to pay more as needs grow
- Higher LTV over time
```

### LTV Scoring Framework

**Scoring Criteria (1-10 scale):**

1. **Expansion Potential (0-2.5 points)**
   - 0: No growth mentioned
   - 1.25: Some growth potential
   - 2.5: Clear growth trajectory

2. **Integration Needs (0-2.5 points)**
   - 0: No integration needs
   - 1.25: Some integration needs
   - 2.5: Strong ecosystem engagement

3. **Outcome Commitment (0-2.5 points)**
   - 0: No clear outcomes
   - 1.25: Vague outcomes
   - 2.5: Clear, committed outcomes

4. **Constraint Evolution (0-2.5 points)**
   - 0: Fixed constraints
   - 1.25: Some flexibility
   - 2.5: Clear evolution path

**Scoring Interpretation:**
- 8-10: Very High LTV (Priority 1)
- 6-7: High LTV (Priority 2)
- 4-5: Medium LTV (Priority 3)
- 0-3: Low LTV (Monitor only)

## Lab 3: Persona Monetisation Scoring Model

### Objective
Create a scoring model to evaluate and prioritize personas based on intent depth, constraint-driven buying, tradeoffs, and LTV signals.

### Tasks

1. **Persona Data Collection**
   - Collect 15-20 personas from AI discovery
   - Document questions, constraints, and outcomes for each
   - Note language patterns and tradeoffs
   - Record LTV indicators

2. **Intent Depth Scoring**
   - Score each persona on intent depth (0-10)
   - Use specificity, constraint clarity, outcome definition, urgency, and willingness to pay
   - Categorize personas by intent level
   - Prioritize high-intent personas

3. **Constraint Analysis**
   - Identify primary constraints for each persona
   - Understand constraint tradeoffs
   - Evaluate if constraints are addressable
   - Score personas on constraint-driven buying potential

4. **Tradeoff Evaluation**
   - Identify explicit tradeoffs for each persona
   - Evaluate if tradeoffs are acceptable
   - Assess if product can deliver on tradeoffs
   - Score personas on tradeoff alignment

5. **LTV Signal Analysis**
   - Identify LTV indicators for each persona
   - Score expansion potential, integration needs, outcome commitment, and constraint evolution
   - Calculate LTV scores (0-10)
   - Prioritize high-LTV personas

6. **Monetisation Scoring Model**
   - Combine intent depth, constraint alignment, tradeoff fit, and LTV scores
   - Create weighted scoring model
   - Rank personas by monetisation potential
   - Create prioritised persona roadmap

### Deliverables
- Persona Data Collection (15-20 personas)
- Intent Depth Scores
- Constraint Analysis
- Tradeoff Evaluation
- LTV Signal Analysis
- Persona Monetisation Scoring Model
- Prioritised Persona Roadmap (top 5-7 personas)

### Evaluation Criteria
- Persona data quality (15%)
- Intent depth scoring accuracy (20%)
- Constraint analysis depth (20%)
- Tradeoff evaluation insight (15%)
- LTV signal identification (15%)
- Scoring model rigor (10%)
- Roadmap prioritisation (5%)

### Scoring Model Template
```
Persona Monetisation Score = 
  (Intent Depth × 0.3) + 
  (Constraint Alignment × 0.2) + 
  (Tradeoff Fit × 0.2) + 
  (LTV Score × 0.3)

Where each component is scored 0-10
```

## Summary

**Key Takeaways:**

- **Intent Depth:**: Scoring personas on specificity, constraints, outcomes, urgency, and willingness to pay helps prioritize high-intent personas

- **Constraint-Driven Buying:**: Understanding budget, time, skill, and resource constraints helps design products and offers that align with persona needs

- **Explicit Tradeoffs:**: Personas that explicitly accept tradeoffs (features vs price, speed vs customization, etc.) indicate high intent and readiness to buy

- **LTV Signals:**: Early indicators of expansion potential, integration needs, outcome commitment, and constraint evolution help identify high-LTV personas

- **Monetisation Scoring:**: Combining intent depth, constraint alignment, tradeoff fit, and LTV signals creates a prioritised persona roadmap for monetisation
