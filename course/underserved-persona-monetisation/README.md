# Underserved Persona Monetisation

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8–10 weeks (cohort-based or self-paced)  
**Level:** Senior / Director  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Primary Role:** AI-Driven Growth & Persona Lead  
**Prerequisites:** Growth marketing fundamentals, understanding of AI visibility systems, product strategy experience

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- **Identify High-Intent**: Identify high-intent personas surfaced by AI before competitors do
- **Monetise Demand**: Monetise demand that AI already understands
- **Align Product,**: Align product, pricing, and messaging around truth
- **Improve Ai**: Improve AI recommendation frequency organically
- **Drive Durable**: Drive durable growth rooted in real customer needs

---

## Course Objective

This course teaches how to discover, validate, and monetise underserved personas that AI systems surface organically. You'll learn to identify high-intent segments, design persona-aligned products and offers, and create credible positioning that both AI and humans trust.

**Outcome:** Learners can identify high-intent personas surfaced by AI, design persona-aligned products and offers, and monetise them through credible positioning and AI-visible proof.

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why AI Exposes Persona Truth](Module_01_Why_AI_Exposes_Persona_Truth.md)
**Theme:** From Brand Narratives to Buyer Reality
- How LLMs infer personas from questions, constraints, and outcomes
- Why traditional segmentation hides real demand
- AI's bias toward specificity, utility, and truth
- Signals of underserved vs unprofitable personas
- **Practical Output:** Persona Exposure Audit

### [Module 2: Mining AI Answers for Persona Signals](Module_02_Mining_AI_Answers_for_Persona_Signals.md)
**Theme:** Listening Where the Truth Leaks Out
- Persona inference from AI recommendations
- Language patterns that signal unmet needs
- "Who should buy this?" vs "who actually buys this?"
- Competitive persona capture inside AI answers
- **Practical Output:** AI Persona Signal Map

### [Module 3: Intent Depth & Willingness to Pay](Module_03_Intent_Depth_and_Willingness_to_Pay.md)
**Theme:** Separating Curiosity from Revenue
- Intent depth scoring
- Constraint-driven buying (budget, time, health, skill)
- Tradeoffs personas explicitly accept
- Early LTV signals from AI-discovered personas
- **Practical Output:** Persona Monetisation Scoring Model

### [Module 4: Product & Pricing Gaps](Module_04_Product_and_Pricing_Gaps.md)
**Theme:** Where the Offer Actually Breaks
- Feature gaps vs perception gaps
- Packaging, sizing, and format mismatches
- Price-performance misalignment
- When to create SKUs vs reposition existing ones
- **Practical Output:** Persona–Product Gap Analysis

### [Module 5: Persona-Aligned Messaging](Module_05_Persona_Aligned_Messaging.md)
**Theme:** Specificity Beats Brand Voice
- Writing for persona constraints and outcomes
- Honest tradeoffs and "who this is not for"
- Proof-first messaging structures
- Avoiding overreach that damages AI trust
- **Practical Output:** Persona-Specific Messaging Framework

### [Module 6: Authentic Proof & Story Capture](Module_06_Authentic_Proof_and_Story_Capture.md)
**Theme:** Why AI Prefers Real Stories Over Claims
- Identifying high-signal customers
- Structuring testimonials for AI readability
- UGC prompts that surface outcomes, not hype
- Ethical and legal considerations
- **Practical Output:** Persona Proof Collection Plan

### [Module 7: Feeding Personas Back into AI Visibility Systems](Module_07_Feeding_Personas_Back_into_AI_Visibility_Systems.md)
**Theme:** Closing the Loop
- Updating content and product truth layers
- Persona-based intent clusters
- Avoiding fragmentation across teams
- Measuring AI recommendation frequency by persona
- **Practical Output:** Persona → AI Visibility Feedback Loop

### [Module 8: Measurement & Growth Experiments](Module_08_Measurement_and_Growth_Experiments.md)
**Theme:** Proving Persona Monetisation Works
- Persona-level conversion and retention
- Revenue attribution by persona
- Product–market fit signals
- Kill criteria for underperforming personas
- **Practical Output:** Persona Performance Dashboard

### [Module 9: Operating Model & Scale](Module_09_Operating_Model_and_Scale.md)
**Theme:** Making This a Repeatable Growth Engine
- Working with product & pricing teams
- Partnering with brand and research
- Aligning growth, experimentation, and AI visibility
- Portfolio-level persona strategies
- **Capstone Project:** Underserved Persona Monetisation Plan
