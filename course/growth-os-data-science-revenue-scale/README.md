# Growth OS: Data Science for Revenue & Scale

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Growth Fundamentals & Funnel Analytics](Module_01_Growth_Fundamentals_and_Funnel_Analytics.md)
- The full growth loop: acquisition, activation, retention, monetization
- Funnel vs lifecycle thinking
- Leading vs lagging growth indicators
- Funnel conversion diagnostics and drop-off analysis
- Common growth analytics anti-patterns
- **Hands-on:** Analyze a multi-step funnel and identify the highest-leverage improvement points

### [Module 2: Cohort Analysis & Retention Modeling](Module_02_Cohort_Analysis_and_Retention_Modeling.md)
- Cohort definitions and design choices
- Retention curves and decay patterns
- Behavioral vs calendar cohorts
- Interpreting retention shifts
- Linking retention to product and marketing actions
- **Hands-on:** Build and interpret cohort analyses for a subscription or marketplace product

### [Module 3: Lifetime Value (LTV) Modeling](Module_03_Lifetime_Value_LTV_Modeling.md)
- Conceptual foundations of LTV
- Historical vs predictive LTV
- Contribution margin and cost inclusion
- Segment-level LTV analysis
- Using LTV for budget and strategy decisions
- **Hands-on:** Build a predictive LTV model and evaluate its stability

### [Module 4: Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)](Module_04_Marketing_Mix_Modeling_MMM.md)
- When MMM is the right tool (and when it isn't)
- Core MMM concepts and assumptions
- Media saturation and diminishing returns
- Seasonality, trends, and external factors
- Translating MMM outputs into budget decisions
- **Hands-on:** Design and interpret an MMM for a multi-channel marketing setup

### [Module 5: Attribution & Incrementality](Module_05_Attribution_and_Incrementality.md)
- Rules-based vs data-driven attribution
- Incrementality and causal lift
- A/B testing vs observational attribution
- Common attribution failure modes
- Aligning attribution with business goals
- **Hands-on:** Compare attribution and incrementality results for the same channel

### [Module 6: Pricing & Promotion Optimization](Module_06_Pricing_and_Promotion_Optimization.md)
- Price elasticity and demand curves
- Discount effectiveness and cannibalization
- Promotion targeting and personalization
- Testing pricing changes safely
- Long-term vs short-term revenue trade-offs
- **Hands-on:** Design a pricing or promotion experiment with guardrails

### [Module 7: Experimentation for Growth Decisions](Module_07_Experimentation_for_Growth_Decisions.md)
- Growth experimentation frameworks
- Defining success metrics and guardrails
- Interpreting noisy growth experiments
- Scaling wins and killing false positives
- Institutionalizing growth learning
- **Hands-on:** Design an experiment roadmap for a growth team

### [Module 8: Communicating Growth Impact to Leadership](Module_08_Communicating_Growth_Impact_to_Leadership.md)
- Translating analytics into financial impact
- Storytelling with growth metrics
- Managing stakeholder expectations
- Handling uncertainty and trade-offs
- Building trust with finance and marketing leaders
- **Hands-on:** Create an executive-ready growth impact memo

### [Capstone: Build a Growth OS](Capstone_Build_a_Growth_OS.md)
- End-to-end revenue optimization system
- Diagnose a growth funnel
- Analyze cohorts and LTV
- Evaluate marketing effectiveness
- Propose pricing or promotion changes
- Quantify expected revenue impact
- **Final Deliverable:** Defend the growth strategy in a revenue review simulation

## Technology Stack

### Core Tools
- **Python:** pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels, scikit-learn
- **SQL:** Advanced queries for cohort analysis and funnel diagnostics
- **Visualization:** matplotlib, seaborn, plotly
- **Statistical Modeling:** R (optional), Python stats libraries

### Specialized Tools
- **MMM:** Robyn (Meta), LightweightMMM (Google), custom implementations
- **Attribution:** Markov chains, Shapley values, causal inference libraries
- **Experimentation:** Stats libraries, power analysis tools
- **LTV Modeling:** Survival analysis, probabilistic models
