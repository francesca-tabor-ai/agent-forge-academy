# Capstone: Build a Growth OS

**Duration:** Weeks 9-10  
**Theme:** *End-to-end revenue optimization system*

**Objective:**  
Students will build a complete Growth & Revenue Operating System that diagnoses growth funnels, analyzes cohorts and LTV, evaluates marketing effectiveness, proposes pricing changes, and quantifies expected revenue impact.

---

## Capstone Overview

### Project Structure

You will work through a comprehensive growth analysis scenario, applying all concepts from Modules 1-8 to build a complete Growth OS.

### Deliverables

1. **Funnel and Cohort Analysis**
2. **LTV and Marketing Effectiveness Models**
3. **Attribution or Incrementality Assessment**
4. **Pricing or Promo Recommendation**
5. **Executive Revenue Impact Summary**

### Final Requirement

**Defend the growth strategy in a revenue review simulation**

You will present your complete Growth OS to a panel (simulating company leadership) and defend your recommendations.

---

## Part 1: Funnel and Cohort Analysis

### Objective
Diagnose growth funnel and analyze cohort behavior.

### Dataset
You'll receive:
- User journey data (events, timestamps)
- Conversion data
- User attributes (channel, device, location, cohort)

### Tasks

1. **Funnel Mapping**
   - Map complete user journey
   - Identify all funnel stages
   - Calculate conversion rates
   - Identify drop-off points

2. **Funnel Diagnostics**
   - Calculate drop-off impact
   - Rank by revenue impact
   - Segment analysis (by channel, device, etc.)
   - Root cause hypotheses

3. **Cohort Analysis**
   - Create calendar cohorts
   - Create behavioral cohorts
   - Calculate retention curves
   - Analyze retention trends

4. **Insights and Recommendations**
   - Identify top 3 improvement opportunities
   - Quantify expected impact
   - Recommend specific actions

### Deliverable

**Funnel and Cohort Analysis Report** including:
- Funnel visualization and diagnostics
- Cohort retention analysis
- Top 3 improvement opportunities
- Expected revenue impact
- Specific recommendations

---

## Part 2: LTV and Marketing Effectiveness Models

### Objective
Build LTV models and evaluate marketing effectiveness.

### Dataset
You'll receive:
- Customer transaction history
- Marketing spend by channel
- Customer acquisition data
- Control variables (price, promotions, seasonality)

### Tasks

1. **LTV Modeling**
   - Calculate historical LTV
   - Build predictive LTV models
   - Segment LTV analysis
   - Identify high-value segments

2. **Marketing Mix Modeling**
   - Prepare data (adstock, seasonality)
   - Build MMM model
   - Calculate channel contributions
   - Identify optimal budget allocation

3. **Budget Reallocation**
   - Compare current vs optimal allocation
   - Calculate expected impact
   - Run scenario planning

4. **Insights and Recommendations**
   - Top 3 marketing optimization opportunities
   - Quantify expected impact
   - Recommend budget changes

### Deliverable

**LTV and Marketing Effectiveness Report** including:
- LTV model results and segment analysis
- MMM results and channel contributions
- Budget reallocation recommendations
- Expected revenue impact
- Implementation plan

---

## Part 3: Attribution or Incrementality Assessment

### Objective
Assess channel impact using attribution and incrementality.

### Dataset
You'll receive:
- Customer journey data
- Conversion data
- A/B test data (for incrementality)
- Channel spend data

### Tasks

1. **Attribution Analysis**
   - Implement multiple attribution models
   - Compare results
   - Identify discrepancies

2. **Incrementality Analysis**
   - Analyze A/B test data
   - Calculate incrementality lift
   - Compare to attribution

3. **Channel Impact Assessment**
   - Synthesize attribution and incrementality
   - Identify true channel value
   - Quantify impact of measurement method

4. **Recommendations**
   - Recommend measurement approach
   - Identify under/over-valued channels
   - Quantify impact of optimization

### Deliverable

**Attribution and Incrementality Assessment** including:
- Attribution model comparisons
- Incrementality analysis
- Channel impact assessment
- Measurement recommendations
- Expected impact

---

## Part 4: Pricing or Promo Recommendation

### Objective
Design pricing or promotion strategy backed by data.

### Dataset
You'll receive:
- Historical pricing data
- Sales and conversion data
- Customer data (LTV, segments)
- Promotion history

### Tasks

1. **Price Elasticity Analysis**
   - Estimate price elasticity
   - Build demand curves
   - Identify optimal price points

2. **Promotion Analysis** (if applicable)
   - Measure discount effectiveness
   - Measure cannibalization
   - Measure pull-forward

3. **Strategy Design**
   - Design pricing/promo strategy
   - Define targeting
   - Design experiments
   - Set guardrails

4. **Recommendations**
   - Specific pricing/promo recommendations
   - Expected revenue impact
   - Risk analysis
   - Implementation plan

### Deliverable

**Pricing or Promo Strategy** including:
- Price elasticity analysis
- Promotion effectiveness (if applicable)
- Strategy recommendations
- Expected revenue impact
- Risk analysis and guardrails

---

## Part 5: Executive Revenue Impact Summary

### Objective
Synthesize all analyses into executive-ready summary.

### Tasks

1. **Synthesize Findings**
   - Combine insights from all parts
   - Identify top opportunities
   - Resolve conflicts/priorities

2. **Quantify Total Impact**
   - Calculate combined revenue impact
   - Account for interactions
   - Provide range estimates

3. **Build Executive Memo**
   - One-page summary
   - Financial impact front and center
   - Clear recommendations
   - Risk and uncertainty

4. **Create Presentation**
   - 15-slide deck
   - Visual storytelling
   - Ready for Q&A

### Deliverable

**Executive Revenue Impact Summary** including:
- One-page executive memo
- 15-slide presentation deck
- Supporting materials (appendix)
- Total revenue impact quantification

---

## Final Presentation: Revenue Review Simulation

### Format

You will present your complete Growth OS to a panel simulating:
- CEO
- CFO
- CMO
- Head of Product

### Presentation Structure

1. **Opening** (2 min)
   - Context and problem
   - Overview of approach

2. **Key Findings** (5 min)
   - Top 3-5 insights
   - Financial impact

3. **Recommendations** (5 min)
   - Prioritized recommendations
   - Implementation plan
   - Expected outcomes

4. **Q&A** (10 min)
   - Panel questions
   - Defense of recommendations
   - Discussion of trade-offs

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Analysis Quality (30%):** Rigorous, accurate, comprehensive
- **Business Impact (30%):** Clear financial impact, actionable
- **Communication (20%):** Clear, compelling, executive-ready
- **Defense (20%):** Handles questions well, demonstrates understanding
