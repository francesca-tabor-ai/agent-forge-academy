# Designing AI That Operators Trust
## From Demo to Daily Use

### Course Promise

**"Your models actually get used — and trusted — in production."**

This is not a model accuracy course.  
It is a trust, adoption, and human-AI collaboration course.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Trust beats accuracy**
- **Usage beats performance**
- **Overrides are signals, not failures**
- **Explainability serves operators, not data scientists**
- **Feedback loops close the gap between model and reality**

**Tone:** practical, empathetic, operationally aware  
**Energy:** control room + product design + behavioral psychology  
**Outcome:** AI systems that operators rely on, override appropriately, and improve over time

---

## COURSE STRUCTURE

8 CORE MODULES + CAPSTONE PROJECT

Each module addresses a real adoption blocker that prevents AI from being trusted in operations.

---

## DELIVERY FORMAT

- **Duration:** 3–4 weeks
- **Format:** Interactive lectures + design labs + role play
- **Prerequisites:** Experience shipping AI systems
- Mixed cohorts: product, engineering, and operations leaders

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

Within 90 days:

- Higher override rates (as learning signals)
- Improved recommendation follow-through
- Faster time-to-decision
- Operators actively using AI recommendations
- Clear trust indicators and feedback loops

---

## THE REAL GOAL

Not "better models".

But:

**"AI systems that operators rely on, override appropriately, and improve over time through structured feedback."**
