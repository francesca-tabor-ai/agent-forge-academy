## Capstone Brief

### The Challenge

Design and build a GTM system that demonstrates your ability to:

1. **Capture Leads** (Inbound + Outbound)
   - Inbound: Form submissions, website interactions
   - Outbound: Prospecting, enrichment, sequencing

2. **Enrich and Validate Data**
   - Automatically enrich contact and company data
   - Validate data quality
   - Prevent duplicates
   - Maintain data hygiene

3. **Route and Prioritize Accounts**
   - Route leads to appropriate owners
   - Prioritize based on signals
   - Score leads and accounts
   - Manage ownership

4. **Trigger Workflows**
   - Event-driven automation
   - Outbound sequences
   - Sales workflows
   - Marketing automation

5. **Surface Analytics**
   - Pipeline health metrics
   - Activity vs outcome metrics
   - Automation health
   - Business impact

### System Requirements

**Must Have:**
- ✅ Lead capture (inbound or outbound)
- ✅ Data enrichment and validation
- ✅ Automated routing
- ✅ At least one AI-powered workflow
- ✅ Analytics dashboard or reporting
- ✅ Documentation

**Should Have:**
- ✅ Event-driven triggers
- ✅ Multiple integrations
- ✅ Error handling and monitoring
- ✅ Human-in-the-loop where appropriate

**Nice to Have:**
- ✅ Real-time dashboards
- ✅ Advanced AI features
- ✅ Multiple AI workflows
- ✅ Comprehensive monitoring

### Success Criteria

**Functional:**
- System works end-to-end
- All components integrated
- Data flows correctly
- Automations execute properly
- Analytics are accurate

**Technical:**
- Clean architecture
- Proper error handling
- Good documentation
- Scalable design
- Maintainable code/workflows

**Business:**
- Solves real GTM problems
- Demonstrates ROI
- Shows measurable impact
- Addresses stakeholder needs
