# The "Investment Siri" for Mass-Market Clients

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks (can be delivered over 6-8 weeks)  
**Level:** Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Understanding of wealth management, digital advice, conversational AI, natural language processing, regulatory compliance, product design

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- Design AI-powered digital investment advisors for mass-market clients
- Understand the economic drivers behind AI-led advice
- Define the role and boundaries of digital investment advisors
- Map end-to-end investment workflows with AI orchestration
- Create natural language, conversational investment experiences
- Implement suitability, guardrails, and embedded compliance
- Scale advice without linear headcount increases
- Build trust, explainability, and ethical AI design
- Deploy investment advisors as platform capabilities

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why the Mass Market Needs an "Investment Siri"](Module_01_Why_the_Mass_Market_Needs_an_Investment_Siri.md)
- Understand the economic and structural drivers behind AI-led advice
- The advice gap and affordability problem
- Why human-only advice does not scale
- Lessons from Siri, Alexa, and consumer AI adoption
- **Exercise 1:** Identify which client segments can be served by AI-first guidance vs. human advice

### [Module 2: Defining the Role of a Digital Investment Advisor](Module_02_Defining_the_Role_of_a_Digital_Investment_Advisor.md)
- Clarify what an "Investment Siri" should—and should not—do
- Guidance vs. recommendations vs. execution
- Goal-based investing for non-expert clients
- Question-led advisory journeys
- **Exercise 2:** Design a conversation flow for a first-time retail investor

### [Module 3: The AI Brain: Mapping End-to-End Investment Workflows](Module_03_The_AI_Brain_Mapping_End_to_End_Investment_Workflows.md)
- Position AI as the orchestration layer of the asset ecosystem
- The "AI brain" concept for asset managers
- Connecting product data, risk, ESG, and performance
- Eliminating administrative friction across workflows
- **Exercise 3:** Map an end-to-end investment workflow and identify AI intervention points

### [Module 4: Natural Language Investing: Conversational UX](Module_04_Natural_Language_Investing_Conversational_UX.md)
- Enable intuitive, voice- or chat-based investing interactions
- Conversational design for financial literacy gaps
- Translating complex concepts into plain language
- Context memory and follow-up questions
- **Exercise 4:** Write sample client-AI dialogues for common investment questions

### [Module 5: Suitability, Guardrails & Embedded Compliance](Module_05_Suitability_Guardrails_and_Embedded_Compliance.md)
- Keep mass-market AI advice regulator-ready by design
- Embedded suitability logic for retail clients
- Risk profiling through conversational inputs
- Jurisdiction-aware compliance constraints
- **Exercise 5:** Define guardrails that prevent unsuitable AI responses

### [Module 6: Scaling Advice Without Scaling Headcount](Module_06_Scaling_Advice_Without_Scaling_Headcount.md)
- Achieve operational leverage with AI-native models
- Cost-to-serve economics of digital advice
- Handling millions of simultaneous interactions
- Continuous learning from client behavior
- **Exercise 6:** Estimate cost savings of AI-led advice vs. traditional models

### [Module 7: Trust, Explainability & Ethical Design](Module_07_Trust_Explainability_and_Ethical_Design.md)
- Build confidence among clients, regulators, and firms
- "Why did the AI say this?"
- Bias, fairness, and inclusivity in mass advice
- Transparency disclosures for AI advisors
- **Exercise 7:** Draft an explainability statement for an AI-generated investment suggestion

### [Module 8: From Digital Assistant to Platform Capability](Module_08_From_Digital_Assistant_to_Platform_Capability.md)
- Move from pilot to ecosystem-wide deployment
- Integrating the Investment Siri into asset and distribution platforms
- Measuring success: engagement, outcomes, trust
- Evolving from reactive answers to proactive guidance
- **Capstone Project:** Design an "Investment Siri" for a mass-market wealth platform, including governance, UX, and compliance model
