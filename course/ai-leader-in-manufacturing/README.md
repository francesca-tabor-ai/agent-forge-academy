# The AI Leader in Manufacturing
## Strategic Decision-Making for AI at Scale

### Course Goal

Equip AI leaders to make sound strategic decisions about where AI creates value, where it doesn't, and how to scale it responsibly across complex manufacturing and supply-chain organizations.

### Course Outcome

Participants can set AI strategy, align it with business transformation, manage risk and ethics, and lead AI programs that deliver sustained operational and financial impact.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Strategic restraint beats automation for its own sake**
- **Process redesign often beats AI investment**
- **Build vs buy decisions determine long-term capability**
- **Organizational structure kills more AI than technology**
- **Ethics and accountability are leadership responsibilities**
- **Failure modes at scale are predictable and preventable**
- **Portfolio-level impact matters more than project ROI**
- **Board communication requires clarity, not hype**

**Tone:** strategic, pragmatic, leadership-focused  
**Energy:** executive decision-making + operational reality  
**Outcome:** sound AI strategy, responsible scaling, sustained impact

---

8 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

**Duration:** 3–4 weeks (executive format)  
**Format:** Executive seminars + strategy workshops + peer reviews  
**Prerequisites:** Experience leading AI or digital initiatives

- Board-ready AI roadmap
