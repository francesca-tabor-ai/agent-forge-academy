## 6.1 Conformity Assessments and Certification

### Understanding Conformity Assessment

**Definition:** The process of demonstrating that an AI system meets the requirements of the EU AI Act before it can be placed on the market or put into service.

**Purpose:**
- Verify compliance with EU AI Act
- Ensure safety and fundamental rights protection
- Build trust and confidence
- Enable market access

### When Conformity Assessment is Required

**High-Risk AI Systems:**
- **Mandatory:** All high-risk AI systems must undergo conformity assessment
- **Before Deployment:** Must be completed before placing on market or putting into service
- **Ongoing:** May require reassessment for significant changes

**Limited-Risk Systems:**
- **Not Required:** Limited-risk systems do not require conformity assessment
- **Voluntary:** May choose to undergo assessment for trust-building
- **Best Practice:** Consider assessment for transparency

### Types of Conformity Assessment

**1. Self-Assessment (Internal Control)**

**When Applicable:**
- Most high-risk AI systems
- Systems not requiring third-party assessment
- Provider conducts own assessment

**Process:**
- Provider assesses compliance
- Creates technical documentation
- Performs testing and validation
- Issues declaration of conformity

**Requirements:**
- Comprehensive technical documentation
- Risk management system
- Quality management system
- Post-market monitoring plan

**2. Third-Party Assessment (Notified Body)**

**When Required:**
- Specific high-risk categories
- Systems with significant risks
- Provider chooses third-party assessment
- Regulatory authority requires it

**Process:**
- Independent assessment by notified body
- Review of technical documentation
- Testing and validation
- Certification issued

**Notified Bodies:**
- Designated by member states
- Accredited and independent
- Expert in AI systems
- Authorized to issue certificates

### The Conformity Assessment Process

**Step 1: Preparation**

**Documentation:**
- Technical documentation
- Risk management documentation
- Quality management documentation
- Testing and validation results

**System Readiness:**
- System meets requirements
- Testing completed
- Documentation complete
- Compliance verified

**Step 2: Assessment**

**Self-Assessment:**
- Review documentation
- Verify compliance
- Test system
- Document findings

**Third-Party Assessment:**
- Submit to notified body
- Independent review
- Testing and validation
- Assessment report

**Step 3: Declaration of Conformity**

**Self-Assessment:**
- Provider issues declaration
- States compliance with EU AI Act
- Includes system information
- Signed by authorized representative

**Third-Party Assessment:**
- Notified body issues certificate
- Confirms compliance
- Valid for specified period
- May include conditions

**Step 4: CE Marking**

**Requirements:**
- Affix CE marking to system
- Visible and legible
- Indicates conformity
- Required for high-risk systems

**Step 5: Registration**

**EU Database:**
- Register system in EU database
- Provide required information
- Maintain registration
- Update as needed

### CE Marking Requirements

**What is CE Marking?**
A marking that indicates a product conforms with EU regulations and can be freely traded in the European Economic Area.

**Requirements:**
- **Mandatory:** For high-risk AI systems
- **Visible:** Must be affixed to system
- **Legible:** Clear and readable
- **Permanent:** Cannot be easily removed

**CE Marking Process:**
1. Complete conformity assessment
2. Issue declaration of conformity
3. Affix CE marking
4. Register in EU database

## 6.4 Managing Serious Incidents

### Understanding Serious Incidents

**Definition:** Incidents that result in or create a risk of death, serious injury, or significant harm to health, safety, fundamental rights, or the environment.

**Characteristics:**
- **Severity:** Significant harm or risk
- **Impact:** Affects individuals or society
- **Urgency:** Requires immediate attention
- **Reporting:** Must be reported to authorities

### Serious Incident Requirements (EU AI Act)

**1. Incident Detection**
- Systems to detect incidents
- Monitoring procedures
- Alert mechanisms
- Detection capabilities

**2. Incident Reporting**
- Report to authorities within 15 days
- Provide detailed information
- Update as information becomes available
- Maintain records

**3. Incident Management**
- Response procedures
- Mitigation measures
- Corrective actions
- Prevention measures

### Types of Serious Incidents

**1. Safety Incidents**
- Physical harm
- Injury or death
- Safety violations
- Hazard creation

**2. Fundamental Rights Incidents**
- Discrimination
- Privacy violations
- Autonomy violations
- Social exclusion

**3. System Failures**
- Critical system failures
- Widespread errors
- Performance degradation
- Security breaches

**4. Compliance Violations**
- Regulatory violations
- Certification issues
- Documentation failures
- Requirement non-compliance
