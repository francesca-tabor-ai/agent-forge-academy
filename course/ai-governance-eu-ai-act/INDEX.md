##  Regulatory Reference

### EU AI Act Framework
- **Risk Classification** - Unacceptable, high-risk, limited risk, minimal risk (Module 1)
- **Prohibited Practices** - Social scoring, manipulation, biometric categorization (Module 2)
- **High-Risk Domains** - Education, employment, essential services (Module 2)
- **Data Governance** - Quality, integrity, bias detection (Module 3)
- **Technical Documentation** - Model cards, system architecture (Module 4)
- **Transparency Requirements** - Disclosure, marking, explainability (Module 5)
- **Conformity Assessment** - CE marking, notified bodies (Module 6)
- **Impact Assessments** - FRIA for public bodies (Module 6)
- **Post-Market Monitoring** - Performance tracking, incident reporting (Module 6)

### Technical Practices
- **Compliance by Design** - Ethics integration (Module 2)
- **Bias Detection** - Technical strategies (Module 3)
- **Robustness Engineering** - Distribution shift, error handling (Module 4)
- **Security** - Jailbreaking, prompt injection protection (Module 4)
- **Human Oversight** - Stop buttons, override mechanisms (Module 5)
- **Explainability** - Interpretability tools (Module 5)
