# Creative Automation & Templatization

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 12 weeks  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Time Commitment:** 4-6 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Basic design knowledge, familiarity with marketing creative processes

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

✅ Design modular, scalable creative templates  
✅ Implement rules-based design logic for automation  
✅ Integrate data sources for dynamic creative generation  
✅ Automate creative workflows from brief to distribution  
✅ Ensure brand compliance at scale  
✅ Optimize creative performance through testing and measurement  
✅ Lead creative automation initiatives in your organization

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Foundations of Creative Automation](Module_01_Foundations_of_Creative_Automation.md)
- Definition and scope of creative automation
- Templatization vs. traditional design workflows
- Benefits: scalability, consistency, speed, and cost efficiency
- Common use cases: performance marketing, localisation, dynamic content
- Roles and responsibilities in automated creative ecosystems

### [Module 2: Design Systems & Modular Thinking](Module_02_Design_Systems_Modular_Thinking.md)
- Principles of modular and component-based design
- Atomic design applied to marketing assets
- Layout flexibility vs. brand control
- Responsive and adaptive design concepts
- Designing for variability (copy length, imagery, formats, languages)

### [Module 3: Templatization Strategy](Module_03_Templatization_Strategy.md)
- Template architecture and hierarchy
- Static vs. dynamic templates
- Rules-based design logic (constraints, fallbacks, priorities)
- Managing multiple sizes, ratios, and formats from a single source
- Versioning and template governance

### [Module 4: Adobe Ecosystem for Automation](Module_04_Adobe_Ecosystem_for_Automation.md)
- Adobe Creative Cloud Libraries for scalable assets
- Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects in templated workflows
- Smart Objects, Variables, Data Sets, Expressions
- Motion templates and dynamic animation principles
- Export automation and batch processing

### [Module 5: Workflow Integration & DAM](Module_05_Workflow_Integration_DAM.md)
- End-to-end creative workflows
- DAM fundamentals: structure, metadata, taxonomy, tagging
- Integration with workflow management tools (e.g., Workfront)
- Asset handoff, approvals, and lifecycle management
- Automation touchpoints across creative operations

### [Module 6: Data-Driven & Dynamic Creative](Module_06_Data-Driven_Dynamic_Creative.md)
- Data sources: product feeds, CSVs, APIs
- Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) fundamentals
- Personalisation logic (audience, region, product, language)
- Error handling and edge cases
- Performance implications of dynamic creative

### [Module 7: Generative AI for Creative Automation](Module_07_Generative_AI_for_Creative_Automation.md)
- Role of generative AI in modern creative pipelines
- Text, image, and layout generation use cases
- Prompt engineering for scalable outputs
- Human-in-the-loop review models
- Ethical, legal, and brand safety considerations

### [Module 8: Localisation & Transcreation at Scale](Module_08_Localisation_Transcreation_at_Scale.md)
- Designing templates for multilingual expansion
- Accommodating text expansion and cultural variation
- Collaboration with localisation and transcreation teams
- Managing regional brand flexibility
- QA strategies for global asset deployment

### [Module 9: Quality Control & Brand Governance](Module_09_Quality_Control_Brand_Governance.md)
- Automated vs. manual QA processes
- Brand rules enforcement in templates
- Accessibility and compliance considerations
- Testing templates before scale deployment
- Error prevention and rollback strategies

### [Module 10: Performance Marketing & Experimentation](Module_10_Performance_Marketing_Experimentation.md)
- Creative automation for A/B and multivariate testing
- Rapid iteration and learning loops
- Aligning creative structure with media platforms
- Measurement frameworks for automated creative
- Scaling high-performing creative systems

### [Module 11: Documentation & Operational Excellence](Module_11_Documentation_Operational_Excellence.md)
- Template documentation best practices
- Workflow mapping and process clarity
- Cross-team enablement and training
- Change management in automated systems
- Maintaining and evolving template libraries

### [Module 12: Emerging Trends & Future Directions](Module_12_Emerging_Trends_Future_Directions.md)
- AI-driven creative systems
- Real-time personalization
- Creative operations maturity models
- The evolving role of the Creative Automation Specialist
- Continuous improvement and innovation mindset
