# Building the Last Sales Team You'll Ever Train

**Designing, Orchestrating, and Scaling an AI-Agent Sales Organization**

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 modules + Capstone (can be delivered as cohort, async course, or internal enablement program)  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Format:** Strategy → Architecture → Agent Design → Live Implementation

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

✅ Reframe sales as an agentic system, not a department  
✅ Translate sales org charts into agent architectures  
✅ Build executive agents with delegation and prioritization logic  
✅ Deploy multichannel, multilingual, always-on outreach agents  
✅ Create self-healing RevOps and data systems  
✅ Automate research, qualification, and insight synthesis  
✅ Remove scheduling, content, and proposal drag  
✅ Scale from 10 → 100+ agents safely with governance

---

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: The AI-Native Sales Paradigm](Module_01_The_AI_Native_Sales_Paradigm.md)
**From Headcount to Orchestration**

- Understand why traditional sales orgs break at scale
- Reframe sales as an agentic system, not a department
- Define "hyper-specialisation" in AI terms
- Why recruiting, onboarding, and enablement are legacy problems
- Agent vs human economics
- The "background execution" principle
- Sales as a graph of decisions, not roles

**Exercise:** Decompose your current sales org into tasks, decisions, and signals

---

### [Module 2: Designing the Sales Org as a Multi-Agent System](Module_02_Designing_the_Sales_Org_as_a_Multi_Agent_System.md)
**Org Charts → Agent Graphs**

- Translate a sales org into an agent architecture
- Define managers, specialists, and executors
- Decide what escalates to a human (and what never should)
- Hierarchical vs mesh agent systems
- Manager agents vs execution agents
- Latency, autonomy, and trust thresholds
- The role of the human "executive loop"

**Exercise:** Build your full agent org map (CSO → Teams → Specialists)

---

### [Module 3: The Chief Sales Officer Agent](Module_03_The_Chief_Sales_Officer_Agent.md)
**Orchestration, Delegation, Escalation**

- Design a CSO agent that actually thinks
- Implement task delegation and prioritisation logic
- Create clean human escalation moments
- Executive agents vs task agents
- KPI interpretation and anomaly detection
- Cross-team coordination
- "Notify me only when it matters" logic

**Exercise:** Build a CSO agent that runs a daily/weekly sales standup autonomously

---

### [Module 4: Outreach Agents at Scale](Module_04_Outreach_Agents_at_Scale.md)
**Multichannel, Multilingual, Always-On**

- Deploy channel-specific outreach agents
- Design a Global BDR layer that never sleeps
- Maintain message quality across languages and formats
- Email vs voice vs video vs event outreach agents
- Cultural and linguistic context handling
- Personalisation at scale without hallucination
- Lead handoff protocols between agents

**Exercise:** Launch one prospect through 5 channels + 3 languages autonomously

---

### [Module 5: RevOps as an Autonomous Nervous System](Module_05_RevOps_as_an_Autonomous_Nervous_System.md)
**Data, Systems, and Feedback Loops**

- Turn RevOps into a self-healing system
- Centralise truth without human bottlenecks
- Automate competitive intelligence and monitoring
- CRM agents (HubSpot, Smartlead, Slack)
- Data warehouse as memory, not storage
- Competitive signal ingestion
- Agent-driven alerts and corrections

**Exercise:** Build an agent that detects pipeline anomalies and proposes fixes

---

### [Module 6: Research & Insight Agents](Module_06_Research_and_Insight_Agents.md)
**From Raw Data to Sales Leverage**

- Automate qualification and discovery prep
- Create insight synthesis loops from calls and signals
- Feed enablement automatically
- Lead scoring without static rules
- Intent signal interpretation
- Call intelligence → follow-ups → playbooks
- Continuous learning loops across agents

**Exercise:** Run a full discovery cycle without human prep

---

### [Module 7: Logistics & Enablement Without Friction](Module_07_Logistics_and_Enablement_Without_Friction.md)
**Sales Enablement That Updates Itself**

- Remove scheduling, content, and proposal drag
- Keep enablement always current
- Eliminate "where is the doc?" moments forever
- Meeting logistics agents
- Dynamic proposal generation
- Sequence and playbook versioning
- FAQ agents trained on live objections

**Exercise:** Auto-generate a proposal + FAQ from a discovery call

---

### [Module 8: Governance, Guardrails & Scale](Module_08_Governance_Guardrails_and_Scale.md)
**Trust, Safety, and Longevity**

- Prevent agent drift
- Maintain brand, legal, and ethical standards
- Scale from 10 → 100+ agents safely
- Prompt governance and version control
- Audit logs and explainability
- Human override design
- When not to automate

**Exercise:** Design your "kill switch" and escalation framework

---

### [Capstone: Build Your Autonomous Sales Org](Capstone_Build_Your_Autonomous_Sales_Org.md)
**The Last Team You'll Ever Train**

**Deliverable:** A fully operational AI sales organization:
- Executive agent
- Outreach, RevOps, Research, Enablement teams
- Clear human touchpoints
- Live dashboards and feedback loops

**Final Review:**
- System walkthrough
- Failure scenario simulations
- Scale readiness checklist
