## Lesson 3.1: Beyond Keywords - Intent Clusters

### Why Keywords Aren't Enough

Traditional SEO focuses on keywords. AI visibility requires understanding intent clusters.

**Keyword Approach:**
- "best CRM software"
- "CRM comparison"
- "top CRM tools"

**Intent Cluster Approach:**
- **Evaluative intent:** "Which CRM should I choose?"
- **Informational intent:** "How does CRM software work?"
- **Transactional intent:** "Where can I buy CRM software?"

### What Are Intent Clusters?

Intent clusters group related questions that serve the same underlying need:

**Example: CRM Software Intent Cluster**
- "What is the best CRM for small business?"
- "Which CRM has the best pricing?"
- "What CRM do most companies use?"
- "Should I use Salesforce or HubSpot?"

All serve the same intent: **Evaluating CRM options for purchase decision**

### Benefits of Intent Clustering

1. **Comprehensive coverage:** Address all variations of an intent
2. **Efficient optimization:** One strategy covers multiple queries
3. **Better understanding:** See the full customer journey
4. **Competitive advantage:** Identify gaps competitors miss

## Lesson 3.2: Informational vs Evaluative vs Transactional AI Prompts

### Three Core Intent Types

AI prompts fall into three primary categories:

#### 1. Informational Intent

**Characteristics:**
- User seeks knowledge or understanding
- No immediate purchase intent
- Questions start with "what," "how," "why"
- Often early in customer journey

**Examples:**
- "What is CRM software?"
- "How does AI work?"
- "Why do companies use project management tools?"

**AI Behavior:**
- Provides educational content
- Cites authoritative sources
- Explains concepts clearly
- May include examples

**Optimization Strategy:**
- Create comprehensive educational content
- Establish thought leadership
- Build authority in domain
- Answer "what" and "how" questions thoroughly

#### 2. Evaluative Intent

**Characteristics:**
- User comparing options
- Research phase before purchase
- Questions include comparisons, "best," "should I"
- High purchase intent

**Examples:**
- "What is the best CRM for startups?"
- "Should I use X or Y?"
- "Which tool has better features?"

**AI Behavior:**
- Compares multiple options
- Cites multiple sources
- Provides pros/cons
- May recommend specific solutions

**Optimization Strategy:**
- Create comparison content
- Highlight competitive advantages
- Address common objections
- Provide detailed feature comparisons

#### 3. Transactional Intent

**Characteristics:**
- User ready to purchase
- Questions about pricing, availability, purchase process
- Questions include "where to buy," "how much," "pricing"
- Immediate conversion potential

**Examples:**
- "Where can I buy CRM software?"
- "How much does Salesforce cost?"
- "What's the pricing for HubSpot?"

**AI Behavior:**
- Provides specific product information
- Cites official sources
- Includes pricing when available
- May link to purchase pages

**Optimization Strategy:**
- Ensure accurate product information
- Make pricing transparent
- Optimize product pages
- Provide clear purchase paths

## Lesson 3.3: Persona-Driven Question Patterns

### Understanding Personas in AI Queries

Different personas ask different types of questions. Understanding these patterns helps optimize content.

#### The Executive Persona

**Question Patterns:**
- Strategic, high-level questions
- Focus on ROI, business impact
- "What's the business case for..."
- "How does this impact..."

**Examples:**
- "What's the ROI of implementing a CRM?"
- "How does AI visibility impact brand perception?"
- "What are the strategic benefits of..."

**Content Strategy:**
- Executive summaries
- Business case content
- ROI calculators
- Strategic frameworks

#### The Practitioner Persona

**Question Patterns:**
- Tactical, implementation-focused
- "How do I..." questions
- Feature-specific queries
- Technical details

**Examples:**
- "How do I set up a CRM workflow?"
- "What features does X have?"
- "How do I integrate X with Y?"

**Content Strategy:**
- How-to guides
- Feature documentation
- Technical tutorials
- Implementation guides

#### The Researcher Persona

**Question Patterns:**
- Comparison-focused
- "What's the difference between..."
- "Which is better..."
- Deep research mode

**Examples:**
- "What's the difference between X and Y?"
- "Which CRM has better reporting?"
- "Compare X vs Y vs Z"

**Content Strategy:**
- Comparison tables
- Detailed feature comparisons
- Pros/cons analysis
- Comprehensive reviews

## Lesson 3.4: Pre-Purchase vs Post-Purchase AI Usage

### The Full Customer Journey

AI is used throughout the customer journey, not just for discovery.

#### Pre-Purchase Phase

**Common Queries:**
- "What is [product category]?"
- "What are the best [products]?"
- "How do I choose [product]?"
- "What features should I look for?"

**AI Behavior:**
- Provides educational content
- Compares options
- Cites multiple sources
- Helps decision-making

**Optimization Focus:**
- Educational content
- Comparison content
- Decision frameworks
- Feature explanations

#### Post-Purchase Phase

**Common Queries:**
- "How do I use [product]?"
- "How do I set up [feature]?"
- "What's the best way to [use case]?"
- "Troubleshooting [problem]"

**AI Behavior:**
- Provides how-to content
- Cites documentation
- Offers solutions
- May reference support resources

**Optimization Focus:**
- Documentation
- Tutorials
- Support content
- Use case examples

### The Opportunity

Many brands focus only on pre-purchase content. Post-purchase content is often overlooked but critical for:
- Customer satisfaction
- Retention
- Word-of-mouth
- AI visibility in support queries

## Lesson 3.5: Hidden Intents Brands Systematically Miss

### Common Gaps

Many brands miss important intent clusters:

#### 1. Problem-Aware Intent

**What It Is:**
Users know they have a problem but don't know the solution category.

**Examples:**
- "My team can't track customer interactions"
- "I'm losing sales because of poor follow-up"
- "How do I manage customer relationships better?"

**Why Brands Miss It:**
- Focus on product features, not problems
- Don't create problem-focused content
- Assume users know the solution category

**Opportunity:**
- Create problem-focused content
- Map problems to solutions
- Capture users before they know your category

#### 2. Alternative Solution Intent

**What It Is:**
Users considering alternative approaches to solve the same problem.

**Examples:**
- "Should I use a CRM or just spreadsheets?"
- "Is AI visibility worth the investment?"
- "Can I build this myself?"

**Why Brands Miss It:**
- Don't address alternatives
- Assume users are already committed to category
- Miss the "build vs buy" question

**Opportunity:**
- Address alternative solutions
- Make the case for your approach
- Compare to alternatives

#### 3. Risk-Averse Intent

**What It Is:**
Users concerned about risks, failures, or negative outcomes.

**Examples:**
- "What happens if CRM implementation fails?"
- "What are the risks of AI visibility?"
- "How do I avoid common mistakes?"

**Why Brands Miss It:**
- Focus on benefits, not risks
- Don't address failure scenarios
- Avoid discussing negative outcomes

**Opportunity:**
- Address risks proactively
- Provide risk mitigation content
- Build trust through transparency

#### 4. Comparison Across Categories

**What It Is:**
Users comparing your category to other categories.

**Examples:**
- "CRM vs project management tools"
- "AI visibility vs traditional SEO"
- "SaaS vs on-premise solutions"

**Why Brands Miss It:**
- Stay within category boundaries
- Don't think cross-category
- Miss category expansion opportunities

**Opportunity:**
- Create cross-category comparisons
- Expand category definitions
- Capture adjacent markets

## Practical Exercise 3: AI Intent Cluster Map

### Objective
Create a comprehensive map of AI intent clusters for your brand to identify coverage gaps and opportunities.

### Steps

#### Step 1: Intent Discovery (90 minutes)

1. **Query Collection:**
   - Collect 100+ queries related to your brand/category
   - Include variations, synonyms, related terms
   - Cover all question types (what, how, why, which, should)

2. **Query Categorization:**
   - Group queries by intent type (informational, evaluative, transactional)
   - Identify persona patterns
   - Note pre-purchase vs post-purchase

3. **Intent Cluster Formation:**
   - Group related queries into clusters
   - Name each cluster
   - Identify cluster themes

#### Step 2: Coverage Analysis (60 minutes)

1. **Current Coverage:**
   - For each intent cluster, assess:
     - Do you have content addressing this?
     - How comprehensive is your coverage?
     - How well does AI cite your content?

2. **Competitive Coverage:**
   - Test same queries for competitors
   - Identify where competitors have better coverage
   - Note competitive advantages

3. **Gap Identification:**
   - Intent clusters with no coverage
   - Intent clusters with weak coverage
   - Intent clusters where competitors dominate

#### Step 3: Hidden Intent Discovery (45 minutes)

1. **Problem-Aware Intent:**
   - What problems do users have before they know your category?
   - Do you have content addressing these problems?

2. **Alternative Solution Intent:**
   - What alternatives do users consider?
   - Do you address these alternatives?

3. **Risk-Averse Intent:**
   - What risks concern users?
   - Do you address these concerns?

4. **Cross-Category Intent:**
   - What adjacent categories do users compare?
   - Do you create cross-category content?

#### Step 4: Opportunity Prioritization (30 minutes)

1. **Create Opportunity Matrix:**
   ```
   Intent Cluster | Coverage Gap | Revenue Impact | Optimization Effort | Priority
   --------------|--------------|---------------|---------------------|----------
   Evaluative: Best CRM | High | High | Medium | P0
   ...
   ```

2. **Prioritize Opportunities:**
   - **P0:** High impact, manageable effort
   - **P1:** High impact, higher effort
   - **P2:** Medium impact, low effort
   - **P3:** Monitor and maintain

### Deliverables

1. **AI Intent Cluster Map:**
   - All identified intent clusters
   - Query examples for each cluster
   - Intent type classification

2. **Coverage Analysis:**
   - Current coverage assessment
   - Competitive comparison
   - Gap identification

3. **Hidden Intent Analysis:**
   - Problem-aware intents
   - Alternative solution intents
   - Risk-averse intents
   - Cross-category intents

4. **Opportunity Prioritization:**
   - Ranked list of opportunities
   - Action plans for P0/P1
   - Content strategy recommendations

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Completeness:** Comprehensive intent discovery
- **Accuracy:** Correct intent classification
- **Actionability:** Clear opportunities identified
- **Prioritization:** Logical ranking of initiatives

## Key Takeaways

- **Intent clusters > keywords:** Group related queries to understand full customer needs

- **Three core intent types:** Informational, evaluative, and transactional require different strategies

- **Personas matter:** Different personas ask different questions - optimize for your target personas

- **Full journey coverage:** Don't just focus on pre-purchase - post-purchase content matters too

- **Hidden intents are opportunities:** Problem-aware, alternative solution, risk-averse, and cross-category intents are often missed
