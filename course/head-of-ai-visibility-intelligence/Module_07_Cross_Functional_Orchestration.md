## Lesson 7.1: The Cross-Functional Challenge

### Why Cross-Functional Collaboration Is Critical

AI visibility touches multiple teams:

**Affected Teams:**
- **SEO:** Traditional search optimization
- **Content:** Content creation and strategy
- **Product Marketing:** Product positioning and messaging
- **E-commerce:** Product data and pages
- **PR/Comms:** Brand authority and perception
- **Product:** Product information and features

**The Challenge:**
- Each team has different goals
- Different metrics and KPIs
- Competing priorities
- Ownership confusion
- Resistance to change

### Common Failure Modes

**Failure Mode 1: No Ownership**
- Everyone thinks someone else owns it
- No accountability
- Nothing gets done

**Failure Mode 2: Competing Ownership**
- Multiple teams claim ownership
- Conflicting strategies
- Duplicate efforts

**Failure Mode 3: Siloed Efforts**
- Teams work independently
- No coordination
- Inconsistent results

**Failure Mode 4: Resistance**
- Teams resist new priorities
- Pushback on changes
- Slow adoption

## Lesson 7.2: Working with SEO & Content Teams

### The SEO Team Relationship

**Traditional SEO Focus:**
- Rankings and traffic
- Keyword optimization
- Technical SEO
- Link building

**AI Visibility Overlap:**
- Technical SEO fundamentals still matter
- Content optimization for AI
- Authority building
- Structured data

### Collaboration Strategy

**1. Align Goals:**
- SEO: Rankings + Traffic
- AI Visibility: Answer inclusion + Brand perception
- **Shared:** Authority, content quality, technical foundation

**2. Shared Metrics:**
- Traditional SEO metrics (for SEO team)
- AI visibility metrics (for AI visibility)
- **Overlap:** Authority signals, content quality, technical health

**3. Coordinated Efforts:**
- SEO optimizes for search + AI
- Content serves both humans and AI
- Technical SEO enables AI crawling

**4. Clear Boundaries:**
- SEO owns: Technical SEO, rankings, traffic
- AI Visibility owns: Answer optimization, AI-specific content
- **Shared:** Content strategy, authority building

### Content Team Relationship

**Traditional Content Focus:**
- Engagement and conversions
- Brand storytelling
- Thought leadership
- User experience

**AI Visibility Overlap:**
- Factual, authoritative content
- Comprehensive topic coverage
- Clear structure and formatting
- AI-friendly content formats

### Collaboration Strategy

**1. Dual Optimization:**
- Human-facing: Engagement, storytelling
- AI-facing: Facts, structure, authority
- **Both:** Comprehensive, accurate, useful

**2. Content Types:**
- **Human-first:** Marketing pages, landing pages
- **AI-first:** Knowledge base, documentation, FAQs
- **Both:** Blog posts, articles, guides

**3. Quality Standards:**
- Comprehensive coverage
- Factual accuracy
- Authoritative sources
- Clear structure

## Lesson 7.5: Integrating External AI Visibility Vendors

### When to Use Vendors

**Vendor Use Cases:**
- **Monitoring:** Automated answer tracking
- **Analysis:** Answer graph construction
- **Remediation:** Content creation and optimization
- **Reporting:** Executive dashboards

**Build vs Buy:**
- **Build:** Core capabilities, proprietary data, strategic advantage
- **Buy:** Standard monitoring, reporting, non-core functions

- What vendors do
- What you do
- Boundaries and handoffs
- Integration points

**2. Data Integration:**
- Vendor data into your systems
- Your data to vendors
- Shared dashboards
- Unified reporting

**3. Process Integration:**
- Vendor workflows
- Your workflows
- Handoff processes
- Escalation paths

**4. Vendor Management:**
- Regular reviews
- Performance metrics
- Relationship management
- Contract optimization

## Lesson 7.6: RACI Model for AI Visibility

### RACI Framework

**R = Responsible:** Does the work
**A = Accountable:** Owns the outcome
**C = Consulted:** Provides input
**I = Informed:** Kept in the loop

### AI Visibility RACI

**AI Visibility Strategy:**
- **R:** AI Visibility Team
- **A:** Head of AI Visibility
- **C:** SEO, Content, Product Marketing, PR
- **I:** Leadership, E-commerce

**Answer Monitoring:**
- **R:** AI Visibility Team (or vendor)
- **A:** Head of AI Visibility
- **C:** SEO, Content
- **I:** Product Marketing, PR

**Content Remediation:**
- **R:** Content Team
- **A:** Head of Content
- **C:** AI Visibility, SEO, Product Marketing
- **I:** PR, E-commerce

**Product Data Remediation:**
- **R:** E-commerce, Product Marketing
- **A:** Head of Product Marketing
- **C:** AI Visibility, SEO
- **I:** Content, PR

**Authority Building:**
- **R:** PR/Comms
- **A:** Head of PR/Comms
- **C:** AI Visibility, Content
- **I:** SEO, Product Marketing

**Technical Implementation:**
- **R:** SEO, Engineering
- **A:** Head of SEO
- **C:** AI Visibility
- **I:** Content, Product Marketing

## Lesson 7.7: Internal Operating Cadence

### Weekly Cadence

**Weekly AI Visibility Review:**
- **Attendees:** AI Visibility Team, SEO, Content
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Agenda:**
  - Review monitoring alerts
  - Discuss new issues
  - Update on remediation progress
  - Coordinate upcoming work

**Weekly Content Planning:**
- **Attendees:** Content, AI Visibility, SEO
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Agenda:**
  - Content gaps identified
  - Content creation priorities
  - AI visibility requirements
  - SEO alignment

### Monthly Cadence

**Monthly Cross-Functional Review:**
- **Attendees:** All teams (SEO, Content, Product Marketing, E-commerce, PR)
- **Duration:** 60-90 minutes
- **Agenda:**
  - AI visibility performance review
  - Competitive analysis
  - Remediation progress
  - Strategic initiatives
  - Resource allocation

**Monthly Executive Report:**
- **Attendees:** Leadership, Head of AI Visibility
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Agenda:**
  - Key metrics and trends
  - Major wins and losses
  - Competitive positioning
  - Strategic recommendations

### Quarterly Cadence

**Quarterly Strategy Review:**
- **Attendees:** All teams, Leadership
- **Duration:** Half day
- **Agenda:**
  - Strategic assessment
  - Goal setting
  - Resource planning
  - Roadmap alignment

## Practical Exercise 7: RACI Model and Operating Cadence

### Objective
Create a RACI model and operating cadence for your organization to enable effective cross-functional collaboration.

### Steps

#### Step 1: Team Mapping (30 minutes)

1. **Identify All Teams:**
   - SEO
   - Content
   - Product Marketing
   - E-commerce
   - PR/Comms
   - Engineering
   - Others

2. **Map Responsibilities:**
   - What does each team do today?
   - What should they do for AI visibility?
   - What are the overlaps?

#### Step 2: RACI Model Creation (90 minutes)

1. **Define AI Visibility Activities:**
   - Strategy and planning
   - Answer monitoring
   - Content remediation
   - Product data remediation
   - Authority building
   - Technical implementation
   - Reporting

2. **Assign RACI:**
   - For each activity, assign R/A/C/I
   - Ensure clear accountability
   - Avoid gaps or overlaps

3. **Review and Refine:**
   - Get team input
   - Resolve conflicts
   - Finalize model

#### Step 3: Operating Cadence Design (60 minutes)

1. **Define Meeting Types:**
   - Weekly reviews
   - Monthly cross-functional
   - Quarterly strategy
   - Ad-hoc as needed

2. **Design Agendas:**
   - What to discuss
   - Who attends
   - Duration
   - Outcomes

3. **Set Cadence:**
   - Weekly: Day and time
   - Monthly: Day and time
   - Quarterly: Schedule

#### Step 4: Communication Plan (30 minutes)

1. **Define Communication Channels:**
   - Slack/Teams channels
   - Email lists
   - Shared dashboards
   - Reports

2. **Set Communication Frequency:**
   - Daily updates
   - Weekly summaries
   - Monthly reports
   - Quarterly reviews

### Deliverables

1. **RACI Model:**
   - Complete RACI matrix
   - All activities defined
   - Clear accountability

2. **Operating Cadence:**
   - Meeting schedule
   - Agendas
   - Attendee lists
   - Outcomes

3. **Communication Plan:**
   - Channels and frequency
   - Report templates
   - Dashboard access

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Completeness:** All teams and activities covered
- **Clarity:** Clear accountability and responsibilities
- **Feasibility:** Realistic cadence and processes
- **Alignment:** Supports organizational goals

## Key Takeaways

- **Cross-functional collaboration is essential:** AI visibility requires coordination across multiple teams

- **Clear ownership matters:** RACI model prevents confusion and ensures accountability

- **Regular cadence enables coordination:** Weekly, monthly, and quarterly rhythms keep teams aligned

- **Team-specific strategies:** Different teams require different approaches and messaging

- **Vendor integration:** External vendors can complement internal capabilities when integrated properly
