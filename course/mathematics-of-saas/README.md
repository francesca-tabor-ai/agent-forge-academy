# Mathematics of SaaS: Modeling Growth, Retention, and Scale

## Course Overview

This course reveals how mathematical models drive SaaS growth, pricing, retention, infrastructure, experimentation, and long-term scalability. It translates business intuition into formal mathematical models.

## Target Audience

- Product managers
- Data scientists & analysts
- Founders / operators
- Strategy & finance professionals

## Course Goal

To reveal how mathematical models drive SaaS growth, pricing, retention, infrastructure, experimentation, and long-term scalability.

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

- Represent a SaaS business as a system of equations
- Compute and interpret unit economics
- Forecast retention by cohort
- Quantify pricing sensitivity
- Forecast growth from funnel data
- Design statistically valid experiments
- Build interpretable predictive models
- Predict system behavior under load
- Optimize under real-world constraints
- Model defensibility mathematically
- Produce probabilistic forecasts
- Reason about SaaS businesses at a systems level

## Course Structure

### Module 1: SaaS as a Mathematical System
**Theme:** Translating business intuition into formal models

**Key Concepts:** SaaS value chains as state systems, discrete vs continuous time models, stocks, flows, and feedback loops

**Core Topics:** Customers as state variables, revenue as a function of time, growth vs decay dynamics

### Module 2: Revenue Mechanics & Unit Economics
**Theme:** The algebra of recurring revenue

**Key Concepts:** MRR, ARR, ARPA, cohort-based accounting, unit-level profitability

**Core Topics:** Revenue aggregation models, CAC–LTV inequality, payback period mathematics

### Module 3: Churn, Retention, and Survival Analysis
**Theme:** Modeling customer lifetime probabilistically

**Key Concepts:** Survival functions, hazard rates, retention curves

**Core Topics:** Exponential vs non-parametric churn, cohort survival estimation, early-life churn risk

### Module 4: Pricing, Demand, and Behavioral Economics
**Theme:** How customers respond to price and value

**Key Concepts:** Elasticity, willingness-to-pay distributions, price discrimination

**Core Topics:** Elastic vs inelastic demand, tiered and usage-based pricing models, behavioral pricing thresholds

### Module 5: Growth Models & Funnels
**Theme:** Turning acquisition into equations

**Key Concepts:** Funnel mathematics, viral coefficients, growth compounding

**Core Topics:** Conversion probability chains, K-factor dynamics, saturation and diminishing returns

### Module 6: Experimentation & Causal Inference
**Theme:** Knowing whether changes actually work

**Key Concepts:** Hypothesis testing, statistical power, Bayesian vs frequentist inference

**Core Topics:** A/B testing frameworks, confidence intervals vs posteriors, false positives in SaaS experiments

### Module 7: Data Science & Predictive Models in SaaS
**Theme:** Turning data into forward-looking decisions

**Key Concepts:** Classification and regression, feature engineering, model evaluation

**Core Topics:** Churn prediction, lead scoring, upsell propensity models

### Module 8: Infrastructure, Reliability & Performance Modeling
**Theme:** Mathematics beneath SaaS operations

**Key Concepts:** Queueing theory, Little's Law, reliability metrics

**Core Topics:** Latency distributions, capacity planning, error budgets and SLOs

### Module 9: Optimization & Resource Allocation
**Theme:** Doing more with finite resources

**Key Concepts:** Constrained optimization, knapsack-style tradeoffs, marginal ROI

**Core Topics:** Cloud cost optimization, engineering prioritization, marketing budget allocation

### Module 10: Strategic Dynamics & Network Effects
**Theme:** Long-term competitive advantage

**Key Concepts:** Network externalities, game theory, path dependence

**Core Topics:** Adoption equilibria, lock-in and switching costs, platform dynamics

### Module 11: Forecasting, Planning & Risk
**Theme:** Seeing the future probabilistically

**Key Concepts:** Time series models, scenario analysis, uncertainty quantification

**Core Topics:** MRR/ARR forecasting, Monte Carlo simulations, downside risk modeling

### Module 12: Capstone - Build a Full SaaS Mathematical Model
**Theme:** Integration and synthesis

**Capstone Project:** Build a full-stack SaaS model covering growth, revenue, churn, infrastructure, and profitability

## Prerequisites

- Algebra and basic statistics
- Understanding of business operations (recommended)
- Basic familiarity with data analysis (optional)

## Assessment & Evaluation

- Problem Sets – 30%
- Case Studies – 25%
- Applied Modeling Projects – 25%
- Final Capstone Project – 20%

## Outcome

By the end of this course, learners will be able to **reason about SaaS businesses at a systems level** using mathematical models to drive growth, retention, and scale.
