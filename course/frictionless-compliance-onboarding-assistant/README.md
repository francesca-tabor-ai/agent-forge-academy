# Frictionless Compliance & Onboarding Assistant

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks (can be delivered over 4-8 weeks)  
**Level:** Intermediate → Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Understanding of financial compliance, KYC/AML requirements, regulatory frameworks, basic AI concepts, Python programming

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- Design AI-powered compliance tools that dramatically reduce advisor workload
- Build jurisdiction-aware regulatory intelligence systems
- Automate regulatory review of advisor communications
- Implement human-in-the-loop compliance governance frameworks
- Accelerate client onboarding with AI while maintaining controls
- Manage data quality, false positives, and risk in automated systems
- Ensure automation improves advisor experience and client trust
- Deploy enterprise-scale compliance and onboarding platforms

---

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Compliance Became the Advisor's Biggest Time Sink](Module_01_Why_Compliance_Became_the_Advisors_Biggest_Time_Sink.md)
- Understand where time is lost and why automation is unavoidable
- Advisor pain points: provider requests and suitability reports
- The cost of fragmented regulatory requirements
- Manual compliance vs. scalable compliance
- **Exercise 1:** Map a typical client onboarding and advice workflow to identify friction points

### [Module 2: Regulatory Intelligence: Teaching AI the Rules](Module_02_Regulatory_Intelligence_Teaching_AI_the_Rules.md)
- Learn how AI systems internalize regulatory boundaries
- Jurisdictional rule variation (GDPR, marketing rules, disclosure standards)
- Encoding regulatory logic into machines
- Static rules vs. adaptive learning models
- **Exercise 2:** Design a high-level regulatory rules matrix for three jurisdictions

### [Module 3: Automated Regulatory Review of Advisor Communications](Module_03_Automated_Regulatory_Review_of_Advisor_Communications.md)
- Reduce compliance review cycles without increasing risk
- Pre-checking emails, reports, and presentations
- Identifying prohibited language and missing disclosures
- Personalization vs. regulated advice
- **Exercise 3:** Create an AI compliance checklist for a personalized client email

### [Module 4: Human-in-the-Loop Compliance Governance](Module_04_Human_in_the_Loop_Compliance_Governance.md)
- Ensure explainability, accountability, and trust
- Why compliance cannot be fully autonomous
- Confidence scoring and exception thresholds
- Audit trails and supervisory review
- **Exercise 4:** Design a human-review workflow for flagged compliance exceptions

### [Module 5: Accelerating Client Onboarding with AI](Module_05_Accelerating_Client_Onboarding_with_AI.md)
- Cut onboarding time without weakening controls
- Identity verification (KYC) automation
- AML screening and transaction monitoring
- Document ingestion and validation
- **Exercise 5:** Compare manual vs. AI-assisted onboarding timelines

### [Module 6: Data Quality, False Positives & Risk Management](Module_06_Data_Quality_False_Positives_and_Risk_Management.md)
- Prevent automation from creating new bottlenecks
- Managing false positives in AML checks
- Data reconciliation across providers
- Handling incomplete or inconsistent client data
- **Exercise 6:** Define escalation rules for high-risk onboarding cases

### [Module 7: Advisor Experience & Client Trust](Module_07_Advisor_Experience_and_Client_Trust.md)
- Ensure automation improves relationships, not damages them
- Advisor confidence in AI-generated compliance outputs
- Explaining AI-assisted onboarding to clients
- Transparency and consent
- **Exercise 7:** Draft a client-facing explanation of AI-assisted onboarding

### [Module 8: From Point Solution to Compliance Platform](Module_08_From_Point_Solution_to_Compliance_Platform.md)
- Prepare for enterprise-scale deployment
- Integrating compliance AI with CRM and advisory systems
- Measuring ROI: time saved, errors reduced, regulator outcomes
- Monitoring regulatory change and model drift
- **Capstone Project:** Design a Frictionless Compliance & Onboarding Assistant for a multi-jurisdiction advisory platform
