# Scaling AI Across Global Supply Chains
## Enable leaders to scale AI reliably across regions, businesses, and operating models

### Course Goal

Enable leaders to scale AI reliably across regions, businesses, and operating models without fragmenting data, breaking trust, or overwhelming teams.

### Course Outcome

Participants can design AI platforms, governance models, and operating structures that sustain performance, adoption, and learning at global scale.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Scaling AI is not a technical problem — it is a coordination problem**
- **Global doesn't mean uniform. Design for regional variation, not against it**
- **Governance should enable scale, not prevent it**
- **Scale should improve AI, not dilute it**
- **Rolling out AI is rolling out change. Technical success means nothing without adoption**

**Tone:** strategic, pragmatic, coordination-focused  
**Energy:** systems thinking + organizational design + platform engineering  
**Outcome:** AI platforms that work globally, governance that enables rather than blocks, organizations that coordinate effectively

---

## COURSE STRUCTURE

9 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

Duration: 4–5 weeks  
Format: Architecture deep dives + org design workshops + failure case reviews  
Prerequisites: Experience deploying AI systems in production

## SUCCESS METRICS

Within 90 days:

- Multi-region deployment architecture designed
- Data governance framework implemented
- Operating model defined and operational
- Drift monitoring and management in place
- Cross-region learning mechanisms established
- Global AI performance measurement framework active

---

## THE REAL GOAL

Not "better AI models for global deployment".

But:

**"Scaling AI is not a technical problem — it is a coordination problem."**

Participants learn to design AI platforms, governance models, and operating structures that enable reliable scaling across regions, businesses, and operating models — avoiding the fragmentation, trust breakdown, and coordination failures that cause global AI initiatives to fail.

---

## KEY INSIGHT (EXPLICITLY TAUGHT)

**Scaling AI is not a technical problem — it is a coordination problem.**

This course teaches participants that successful global AI deployment requires:
- Technical architecture that accommodates regional differences
- Governance that enables rather than blocks
- Organizational design that coordinates effectively
- Continuous learning and adaptation
- Long-term resilience and improvement
