# Intelligent Document Intelligence Hub ("Prospectus Miner")

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks (can be delivered over 4-8 weeks)  
**Level:** Intermediate → Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Python programming, understanding of financial documents, basic NLP concepts, familiarity with fund structures

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- **and evaluate AI-powered document intelligence Development**: Design and evaluate AI-powered document intelligence systems for financial documents
- **Extract Complex**: Extract complex risk signals from 300+ page prospectuses using AI
- **"golden source" data Development**: Build "golden source" data systems with confidence scoring and validation
- **Generate Narrative**: Generate narrative intelligence and market insights from unstructured text
- **Detect Trends**: Detect trends across document universes for portfolio-level analysis

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: The Prospectus Problem: Why Manual Review No Longer Scales](Module_01_The_Prospectus_Problem_Why_Manual_Review_No_Longer_Scales.md)
- Understand the structural limits of human-led document review
- Why fund prospectuses exceed 300 pages
- Cognitive fatigue and hidden "red flags"
- Regulatory expectations for due diligence
- **Exercise 1:** Identify critical risk information hidden in a sample prospectus table of contents

### [Module 2: Document Intelligence Fundamentals](Module_02_Document_Intelligence_Fundamentals.md)
- Learn how AI reads what humans struggle to scan
- OCR for scanned and non-printable documents
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) for financial text
- Table extraction and layout awareness
- **Exercise 2:** Compare human vs. AI extraction of key prospectus sections

### [Module 3: Extracting Complex Risk Signals](Module_03_Extracting_Complex_Risk_Signals.md)
- Turn dense legal text into structured risk data
- Net derivative exposure and leverage disclosures
- Loss absorption features and capital hierarchy
- High-yield and illiquid asset allocations
- **Exercise 3:** Design an extraction checklist for complex risk metrics

### [Module 4: From Raw Text to a "Golden Source"](Module_04_From_Raw_Text_to_a_Golden_Source.md)
- Ensure extracted data is accurate, consistent, and auditable
- Confidence scoring and data validation
- Reconciling extracted data with structured feeds
- Version control and document change detection
- **Exercise 4:** Define validation rules for AI-extracted prospectus data

### [Module 5: Narrative Intelligence & Market Insight](Module_05_Narrative_Intelligence_and_Market_Insight.md)
- Move beyond data extraction to insight generation
- AI summarization of investment strategy narratives
- Detecting changes in fund manager tone or positioning
- Identifying market themes across multiple documents
- **Exercise 5:** Generate an AI-based narrative summary from unstructured fund commentary

### [Module 6: Trend Detection Across Document Universes](Module_06_Trend_Detection_Across_Document_Universes.md)
- Extract intelligence at portfolio and market level
- Cross-fund document analysis
- Identifying emerging risk or allocation trends
- Comparing stated strategy vs. disclosed exposures
- **Exercise 6:** Design a dashboard concept powered by document-derived insights

### [Module 7: Explainability, Auditability & Trust](Module_07_Explainability_Auditability_and_Trust.md)
- Ensure AI conclusions are defensible to regulators and clients
- Source attribution ("show me where this came from")
- Handling uncertainty and conflicting disclosures
- Human-in-the-loop review workflows
- **Exercise 7:** Create an explainable "red flag" report with source references

### [Module 8: Deploying the Prospectus Miner in Production](Module_08_Deploying_the_Prospectus_Miner_in_Production.md)
- Move from prototype to enterprise-scale deployment
- Integrating document intelligence into advisory platforms
- Scaling across thousands of documents
- Continuous learning as documents evolve
- **Capstone Project:** Design an Intelligent Document Intelligence Hub for a due-diligence or advisory platform

## Technology Stack

### Document Processing
- Tesseract OCR, AWS Textract, Google Document AI
- PDF parsing libraries (PyPDF2, pdfplumber, pdfminer)
- Layout analysis and table extraction
- Document structure recognition

### Natural Language Processing
- spaCy, NLTK (financial NLP)
- Financial BERT, FinBERT
- Named Entity Recognition (NER)
- Text summarization models

### Machine Learning & AI
- Transformers (BERT, GPT) for financial text
- Custom NER models for financial entities
- Classification models for risk detection
- Clustering for trend detection

### Data & Infrastructure
- PostgreSQL (document metadata)
- Elasticsearch (full-text search)
- Redis (caching)
- Apache Kafka (document processing pipeline)

### Tools & Frameworks
- Python (primary language)
- FastAPI (document processing APIs)
- Pandas, NumPy (data processing)
- Streamlit, Dash (dashboards)
