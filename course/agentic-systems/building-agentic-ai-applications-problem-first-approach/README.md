# Building Agentic AI Applications with a Problem-First Approach

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 5 weeks (4 modules + capstone)  
**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Python programming, basic understanding of LLMs, familiarity with business problem-solving frameworks

---

## What You'll Learn

Learn to make decisions tailored to business constraints, understand when & how to apply AI effectively & build a multi-agent application.

We teach RAG, evals, agents, MCP, multi-agents, context engineering, and all that jazz. But always as tools to solve a business problem. If you want a checklist of hype items without knowing when or why to use them, please don't join our course.

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

✅ **Develop Problem-First AI Intuition** - Identify where agentic AI can add value by reframing business challenges through a systems lens  
✅ **Build Evals-Driven Systems** - Frame AI problems through measurable outcomes rather than features or model choices  
✅ **Master Context Engineering** - Design smarter prompts using decomposition, meta-prompts, and algorithmic optimization  
✅ **Build Agentic Pipelines** - Integrate retrieval, memory, and self-reflective behavior in agentic systems  
✅ **Deploy Multi-Agent Solutions** - Analyze multi-agent coordination patterns and implement protocols like MCP/A2A  
✅ **Solve Real Business Problems** - Apply everything in a real-world capstone project

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Develop a Problem-First AI Intuition For Enterprise Use Cases](Module_01_Problem_First_AI_Intuition.md)
- Identify where agentic AI can add value by reframing business challenges through a systems lens
- Understand why traditional software assumptions fail in AI-driven environments
- Evaluate tradeoffs between model choices, latency, performance and cost

- Understand how evaluation acts as the backbone of reliable agentic systems

- Implement evaluation and guardrail techniques using LLM judges and semantic scoring

### [Module 4: Build and Optimize Agentic Pipelines](Module_04_Agentic_Pipelines.md)
- Integrate retrieval, memory, and self-reflective behavior in agentic systems
- Balance tradeoffs between accuracy, latency, and adaptability in agentic systems
- Analyze multi-agent coordination patterns and challenges & learn about protocols like MCP/A2A

- Build an agentic search system across three iterations, integrating RAG, MCP, and multi-agent components

## Course Philosophy

### Problem-First, Not Tool-First

This course is built on a fundamental principle: **AI tools exist to solve business problems, not the other way around.**

We don't start with "let's use RAG" or "let's build a multi-agent system." We start with:
- What business problem are we solving?
- What are the constraints (cost, latency, accuracy, compliance)?
- What are the failure modes we need to prevent?
- How do we measure success?

Only then do we select the appropriate tools: RAG, evals, agents, MCP, multi-agents, context engineering—as solutions to specific problems.

### What This Course Is NOT

❌ A checklist of AI buzzwords  
❌ A tutorial on every possible AI tool  
❌ A course that teaches tools without context  
❌ A "build cool demos" workshop

### What This Course IS

✅ A systematic approach to solving business problems with AI  
✅ A framework for making informed technology decisions  
✅ A deep dive into evaluation and iterative design  
✅ A practical guide to building production-ready systems
