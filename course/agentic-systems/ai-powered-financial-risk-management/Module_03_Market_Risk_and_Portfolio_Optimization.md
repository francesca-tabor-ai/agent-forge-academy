## Lesson 3.1: Value at Risk (VaR) Calculation

### VaR Methods

**Historical Simulation**
- Uses historical price movements
- Non-parametric approach
- Captures tail events
- Requires long history

**Parametric VaR**
- Assumes normal distribution
- Variance-covariance method
- Fast computation
- May underestimate tail risk

**Monte Carlo Simulation**
- Generate scenarios
- Flexible distributions
- Can incorporate correlations
- Computationally intensive

### Expected Shortfall (ES)
- Average loss beyond VaR
- More conservative than VaR
- Coherent risk measure
- Regulatory preference

## Lesson 3.2: AI-Driven Portfolio Optimization

### Traditional Methods
- Mean-variance optimization (Markowitz)
- Black-Litterman model
- Risk parity
- Limitations: Static, assumes normal returns

### Machine Learning Approaches
- Reinforcement learning for dynamic allocation
- Deep learning for return forecasting
- Ensemble methods for risk estimation
- Regime detection for adaptive strategies
