# ESG & Sustainable Investment Insights

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 weeks (can be delivered over 4-8 weeks)  
**Level:** Intermediate → Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Understanding of ESG investing, sustainability frameworks, risk management, natural language processing, data analysis, regulatory compliance

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- Design AI-powered ESG intelligence tools for asset managers
- Understand why traditional ESG data is insufficient
- Implement real-time ESG controversy monitoring
- Turn ESG alerts into actionable investment decisions
- Operationalize double materiality in practice
- Build double materiality analyzers for PAI calculations
- Ensure data quality, methodology, and auditability
- Manage governance, ethics, and reputational risk
- Build scalable ESG intelligence platforms

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Traditional ESG Data Is No Longer Enough](Module_01_Why_Traditional_ESG_Data_Is_No_Longer_Enough.md)
- Understand the structural limits of backward-looking ESG data
- Why ESG ratings lag real-world events
- The gap between controversy and formal disclosure
- Regulatory pressure on ESG credibility
- **Exercise 1:** Identify ESG risks that would not appear in standard ESG datasets until months later

### [Module 2: Real-Time ESG Controversy Monitoring](Module_02_Real_Time_ESG_Controversy_Monitoring.md)
- Detect emerging ESG risks before they hit portfolios and headlines
- Mining news, social media, and NGO reports
- NLP for controversy detection and severity scoring
- Separating signal from noise
- **Exercise 2:** Design an alert framework for emerging ESG controversies

### [Module 3: From Alerts to Actionable Investment Decisions](Module_03_From_Alerts_to_Actionable_Investment_Decisions.md)
- Turn real-time ESG signals into portfolio responses
- Linking controversies to holdings and issuers
- Short-term vs. structural ESG risks
- Engagement, escalation, or divestment workflows
- **Exercise 3:** Create a response playbook for a hypothetical ESG controversy

### [Module 4: Understanding Double Materiality in Practice](Module_04_Understanding_Double_Materiality_in_Practice.md)
- Move double materiality from theory to operational reality
- Financial materiality vs. impact materiality
- Regulatory expectations under SFDR and CSRD
- Mapping impacts across asset classes and regions
- **Exercise 4:** Map double materiality dimensions for a multi-asset portfolio

### [Module 5: The Double Materiality Analyzer](Module_05_The_Double_Materiality_Analyzer.md)
- Calculate PAIs using fragmented and non-standardized data
- Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) indicators explained
- Gathering data from alternative and proxy sources
- Estimation models and confidence scoring
- **Exercise 5:** Design a PAI data pipeline for greenhouse gas emissions and gender pay gaps

### [Module 6: Data Quality, Methodology & Auditability](Module_06_Data_Quality_Methodology_and_Auditability.md)
- Ensure ESG calculations withstand regulatory scrutiny
- Documenting methodologies and assumptions
- Reconciling AI-derived estimates with vendor data
- Versioning and restatement management
- **Exercise 6:** Draft an audit-ready methodology note for a PAI calculation

### [Module 7: Governance, Ethics & Reputational Risk](Module_07_Governance_Ethics_and_Reputational_Risk.md)
- Manage ESG intelligence responsibly
- Bias and misinformation in news and social data
- Ethical use of alternative ESG data
- Human-in-the-loop review for sensitive alerts
- **Exercise 7:** Design a governance framework for real-time ESG alerts

### [Module 8: Building the ESG Intelligence Platform](Module_08_Building_the_ESG_Intelligence_Platform.md)
- Scale ESG insight across the organization
- Integrating controversy monitoring and PAI analytics
- Dashboards for ESG, risk, and investment teams
- Measuring impact: risk mitigation, engagement outcomes
- **Capstone Project:** Design an ESG & Sustainable Investment Intelligence platform for a global asset manager
