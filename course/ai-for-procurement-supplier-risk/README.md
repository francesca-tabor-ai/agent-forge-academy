# AI for Procurement & Supplier Risk
## Smarter Buying Decisions Through Risk-Aware Intelligence

### Course Goal

Design AI systems that support smarter buying decisions by balancing price, reliability, risk, and long-term performance.

### Course Outcome

Participants can build supplier intelligence models that reduce disruptions, anticipate cost shocks, and improve total cost of ownership — not just purchase price.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Total cost beats lowest price**
- **Reliability is a hidden cost**
- **Risk awareness beats reactive firefighting**
- **Long-term performance beats short-term savings**
- **Transparency enables better decisions**

**Tone:** analytical, pragmatic, risk-aware  
**Energy:** supply chain resilience + financial risk management  
**Outcome:** fewer disruptions, better supplier relationships, improved TCO

---

## COURSE STRUCTURE

8 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

Duration: 4–5 weeks  
Format: Lectures + case studies + applied labs  
Prerequisites: Statistics, basic time-series analysis, Python

---

## DELIVERY FORMAT

- 8 × 90-minute live sessions
- Mixed technical + procurement cohorts
- Real supplier data and case studies
- Hands-on labs with Python
- Facilitated discussion and role plays

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

Within 90 days:

- Supplier reliability scores implemented
- Price volatility models in use
- Early warning systems detecting supplier distress
- Multi-supplier strategies reducing risk
- Procurement decisions based on TCO, not just price

---

## THE REAL GOAL

Not "better supplier selection algorithms".

But:

**"The cheapest supplier is often the most expensive long-term."**

Participants learn to build AI systems that help procurement teams make decisions that balance cost, reliability, and risk — avoiding the hidden costs of choosing suppliers based on price alone.

---

## KEY INSIGHT (EXPLICITLY TAUGHT)

**The cheapest supplier is often the most expensive long-term.**

This course teaches participants to quantify and model the hidden costs of supplier decisions, building AI systems that support smarter buying decisions.
