# Building Automated E-commerce Creative at Scale

**From single hero visuals → fully automated catalog-wide creative systems**

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8 modules + Final Project  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Format:** Mindset → Foundations → Systems → Automation → Scale

---

## What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

✅ Design reusable AI creative systems (not one-off prompts)  
✅ Refresh entire product catalogs in hours, not weeks  
✅ Generate consistent bundles, ads, and localized visuals at scale  
✅ Operate creative workflows like software  
✅ Build durable competitive advantage through automation

---

## Course Modules

### [Module 0: The Creative Shift (Mindset Reset)](Module_00_The_Creative_Shift.md)
**Why 2026 ≠ prompt engineering, but workflow engineering**

- Understand why automation replaces briefing
- Learn why "better prompts" is not the moat
- From key visual → creative graph
- Creative scalability as a growth lever
- Why brands lose by generating images one by one

**Assignment:** Deconstruct a past campaign into inputs, variants, constraints, and repetition points

---

### [Module 1: Foundations of Automated Creative Systems](Module_01_Foundations_of_Automated_Creative_Systems.md)
**Core building blocks of AI-native creative workflows**

- Nodes, branches, loops, batches
- Determinism vs variation
- Separation of concerns: concept generation, art direction, product insertion, scale execution
- Why loops beat randomness

**Tools Introduced:** Multi-model pipelines, creative agents vs static prompts, platforms like Pletor

**Assignment:** Sketch your first creative system on paper: one concept, multiple products, reusable structure

---

### [Module 2: Creative Intelligence Layer (Concepts at Scale)](Module_02_Creative_Intelligence_Layer.md)
**Automate idea generation without losing brand control**

- Turning briefs into structured creative inputs
- Using LLMs for concept ideation and visual language translation
- Designing prompt systems, not prompts
- Guardrails: what the model can and cannot invent

**Deliverable:** A reusable system prompt that outputs photorealistic, brand-aligned, non-caricatural visuals

---

### [Module 3: Art Direction as a System (Moodboards → Machines)](Module_03_Art_Direction_as_a_System.md)
**Replace manual art direction with reproducible AI logic**

- Moodboards as data, not inspiration
- Feeding visual references correctly
- One art direction = one sub-workflow
- Avoiding cross-contamination between styles

**Models & Techniques:** Generative moodboarding, reference locking, visual consistency heuristics

**Assignment:** Create 1 moodboard-driven workflow and 2 divergent art directions from the same catalog

---

### [Module 4: Product Reality & Consistency Engineering](Module_04_Product_Reality_and_Consistency_Engineering.md)
**Make AI respect real products, not hallucinated props**

- Why product inconsistency happens
- Angle alignment with packshots
- Descriptive redundancy (on purpose)
- Training models through examples, not wishes

**Best Practices:** Product ≠ outfit, explicit taxonomy, back-view, side-view, material fidelity

**Assignment:** Improve one weak product output using better angles, descriptions, and references

---

### [Module 5: Bundles & Catalog Logic](Module_05_Bundles_and_Catalog_Logic.md)
**Scale product bundles across an entire e-commerce catalog**

- Defining "bundle logic" (2 products, 3 products, variants)
- One need = one batch
- Looping over SKUs without breaking composition
- Maintaining scene integrity across runs

**Exercise:** Design a bundle system with inputs (products), outputs (consistent visuals), and loop logic

---

### [Module 6: Creative Loops & Automation](Module_06_Creative_Loops_and_Automation.md)
**Turn workflows into infinite creative machines**

- Loop mode explained
- Deterministic inputs × variable products
- When to lock vs when to randomize
- Batch execution strategies

**Hands-on:** Run the same creative logic across 10 products, 50 products, and 1 full catalog

---

### [Module 7: Performance & Distribution Readiness](Module_07_Performance_and_Distribution_Readiness.md)
**Design creatives for ads, localization, and channels**

- Creative volume vs creative diversity
- Localization without reshoots
- Aspect ratio & placement planning
- Feeding paid media systems at scale

**Use Cases:** Meta ads, TikTok variations, seasonal storefront refresh, regional campaigns

---

### [Module 8: QA, Iteration & System Scaling](Module_08_QA_Iteration_and_System_Scaling.md)
**Operate creative systems like software products**

- Debugging AI workflows
- Identifying failure nodes
- Versioning creative systems
- Scaling from internal use → API automation

**Final Project:** Build a full automated e-commerce creative system that:
- Generates multiple concepts
- Applies a fixed art direction
- Bundles products
- Loops across a catalog
- Outputs ready-to-use assets
