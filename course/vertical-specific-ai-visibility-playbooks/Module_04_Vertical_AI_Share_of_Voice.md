**Measuring Competitive Presence Inside Answers**

**Duration:** Week 4  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Move from generic share of voice to AI-native competitive analysis
- Track visibility where rankings don't exist
- Understand intent-cluster-based AI share of voice

## Lesson 4.1: The Limitations of Traditional Share of Voice

### Traditional Share of Voice Metrics

**What It Measures:**
- Brand mentions in media
- Social media mentions
- Search volume share
- Advertising share

**Why It Falls Short:**
- Doesn't measure AI visibility
- Ignores citation patterns
- Misses competitive displacement
- Can't track suppression
- Doesn't account for context

### The AI Visibility Gap

**Traditional Metrics Say:**
- High media mentions
- Strong social presence
- Good search rankings
- Strong brand awareness

**AI Reality:**
- Not cited in AI responses
- Competitors mentioned instead
- Suppressed in key queries
- Low AI visibility share

**The Problem:**
Traditional share of voice doesn't reflect AI visibility, where the real competitive battle happens.

## Lesson 4.2: Intent-Cluster-Based AI Share of Voice

### Understanding Intent Clusters

**Definition:** Groups of queries that represent the same user intent

**Examples:**

**E-commerce Intent Cluster: "Best Running Shoes"**
- "Best running shoes for marathon training"
- "Top running shoes 2024"
- "Best running shoes for beginners"
- "Running shoe recommendations"

**Healthcare Intent Cluster: "Diabetes Management"**
- "How to manage Type 2 diabetes"
- "Diabetes treatment options"
- "Best practices for diabetes care"
- "Diabetes management strategies"

**Finance Intent Cluster: "Retirement Planning"**
- "Best retirement investment strategies"
- "How to plan for retirement"
- "Retirement savings advice"
- "Retirement planning guide"

### Measuring Share of Voice by Intent Cluster

**Method:**
1. **Identify Intent Clusters:**
   - Group related queries
   - Map to user intents
   - Prioritize high-value clusters

2. **Test Queries in Cluster:**
   - Test 20-50 queries per cluster
   - Document brand mentions
   - Track citation frequency

3. **Calculate Share of Voice:**
   - Your mentions / Total mentions
   - Your citations / Total citations
   - Your visibility / Total visibility

**Example:**

**Intent Cluster: "Project Management Tools"**

**Query Results:**
- Query 1: Asana (cited), Monday.com (cited), Trello (cited)
- Query 2: Asana (cited), Monday.com (cited)
- Query 3: Monday.com (cited), Trello (cited)
- Query 4: Asana (cited), Trello (cited), Basecamp (cited)

**Share of Voice:**
- Asana: 3/10 = 30%
- Monday.com: 2/10 = 20%
- Trello: 3/10 = 30%
- Basecamp: 1/10 = 10%
- Others: 1/10 = 10%

### Why Intent Clusters Matter

**For Strategy:**
- Focus on high-value intent clusters
- Prioritize competitive clusters
- Track cluster-level performance

**For Measurement:**
- More accurate than aggregate metrics
- Better competitive intelligence
- Clearer strategic priorities

**For Optimization:**
- Cluster-specific optimization
- Targeted content strategies
- Competitive positioning

## Lesson 4.3: Brand Inclusion vs Exclusion Analysis

### Two Types of Competitive Analysis

#### Brand Inclusion Analysis

**Definition:** Measuring when your brand is included in AI responses

**Metrics:**
- Inclusion rate (% of queries where mentioned)
- Citation frequency (how often cited)
- Position in response (first, middle, last)
- Context (positive, neutral, negative)

**What It Tells You:**
- Your competitive position
- Visibility strength
- Authority level
- Competitive standing

**Example:**
- 40% inclusion rate = mentioned in 40% of relevant queries
- High inclusion = strong competitive position
- Low inclusion = weak competitive position

#### Brand Exclusion Analysis

**Definition:** Measuring when your brand is excluded from AI responses

**Metrics:**
- Exclusion rate (% of queries where not mentioned)
- Competitive displacement (competitors mentioned instead)
- Suppression indicators (deliberate exclusion)
- Opportunity gaps (queries where you should be mentioned)

**What It Tells You:**
- Competitive threats
- Suppression risks
- Visibility gaps
- Optimization opportunities

**Example:**
- 60% exclusion rate = not mentioned in 60% of relevant queries
- High exclusion = competitive threat
- Low exclusion = strong position

### Combining Inclusion and Exclusion

**Complete Picture:**
- Inclusion rate: 40%
- Exclusion rate: 60%
- **Analysis:** Weak competitive position, significant opportunity

**Competitive Comparison:**
- Your inclusion: 40%
- Competitor A: 60%
- Competitor B: 30%
- **Analysis:** Competitor A is leader, you're in middle, Competitor B is weak

## Lesson 4.4: Neutral Mentions vs Recommendations

### Understanding Mention Types

**Neutral Mentions:**
- Brand is mentioned without recommendation
- Factual information only
- No preference indicated
- Example: "Brand X offers project management software"

**Recommendations:**
- Brand is recommended or preferred
- Positive framing
- Preference indicated
- Example: "Brand X is a top choice for project management"

### The Value Difference

**Neutral Mentions:**
- Visibility but no preference
- Lower conversion potential
- Still valuable for awareness
- Can be improved to recommendations

**Recommendations:**
- Visibility with preference
- Higher conversion potential
- Stronger competitive position
- Optimal outcome

### Measuring Mention Quality

**Method:**
1. **Categorize Mentions:**
   - Recommendations
   - Neutral mentions
   - Negative mentions

2. **Calculate Quality Score:**
   - Recommendations: 3 points
   - Neutral mentions: 1 point
   - Negative mentions: -1 point

3. **Compare to Competitors:**
   - Your quality score
   - Competitor quality scores
   - Competitive gap

**Example:**

**Your Brand:**
- Recommendations: 10 (30 points)
- Neutral mentions: 20 (20 points)
- Negative mentions: 2 (-2 points)
- **Total: 48 points**

**Competitor:**
- Recommendations: 15 (45 points)
- Neutral mentions: 15 (15 points)
- Negative mentions: 1 (-1 point)
- **Total: 59 points**

**Analysis:** Competitor has higher quality mentions, stronger competitive position

## Lesson 4.5: Competitive Displacement Patterns

### Understanding Displacement

**Definition:** When competitors are mentioned instead of you in relevant queries

**Types:**

1. **Direct Displacement:**
   - Competitor mentioned, you're not
   - Clear competitive loss
   - High priority to address

2. **Position Displacement:**
   - Both mentioned, competitor first
   - Competitive disadvantage
   - Medium priority to address

3. **Context Displacement:**
   - Both mentioned, competitor framed better
   - Competitive disadvantage
   - Medium priority to address

### Identifying Displacement Patterns

**Method:**
1. **Test Competitive Queries:**
   - Queries where you should be mentioned
   - Document who is mentioned
   - Identify displacement cases

2. **Categorize Displacement:**
   - Direct displacement
   - Position displacement
   - Context displacement

3. **Analyze Patterns:**
   - Which competitors displace you
   - In which queries
   - Why displacement occurs

**Example:**

**Query: "Best CRM for small businesses"**

**Expected:** Your brand should be mentioned

**Actual:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho mentioned (you're not)

**Analysis:** Direct displacement by three competitors

**Root Cause:** Weak authority signals, insufficient citations, poor content optimization

### Defense Strategies

1. **Build Authority:**
   - Strengthen authority signals
   - Increase citation sources
   - Improve content quality

2. **Optimize Content:**
   - Align with citation patterns
   - Improve query relevance
   - Enhance competitive positioning

3. **Monitor Competitors:**
   - Track competitive citations
   - Identify displacement threats
   - Respond proactively

## Practical Exercise 4: Vertical AI Share of Voice Dashboard

### Objective
Create a comprehensive AI share of voice dashboard to track competitive presence and identify opportunities.

### Steps

#### Step 1: Intent Cluster Identification (60 minutes)

1. **Identify Intent Clusters:**
   - List 10-20 intent clusters
   - Group related queries
   - Prioritize high-value clusters

2. **Map Queries to Clusters:**
   - 20-50 queries per cluster
   - Ensure query coverage
   - Document cluster definitions

3. **Set Cluster Priorities:**
   - High-value clusters (focus first)
   - Medium-value clusters
   - Low-value clusters

#### Step 2: Query Testing & Data Collection (120 minutes)

1. **Test Queries:**
   - Test all queries in priority clusters
   - Document AI responses
   - Record brand mentions

2. **Collect Data:**
   - Your brand mentions
   - Competitor mentions
   - Citation sources
   - Mention context

3. **Organize Data:**
   - By intent cluster
   - By query
   - By competitor
   - By mention type

#### Step 3: Share of Voice Calculation (60 minutes)

1. **Calculate Cluster-Level SOV:**
   - Your mentions / Total mentions per cluster
   - Your citations / Total citations per cluster
   - Your visibility / Total visibility per cluster

2. **Calculate Aggregate SOV:**
   - Overall share of voice
   - Overall citation share
   - Overall visibility share

3. **Calculate Competitive SOV:**
   - Competitor share of voice
   - Competitive gaps
   - Market share estimates

#### Step 4: Inclusion/Exclusion Analysis (45 minutes)

1. **Calculate Inclusion Rate:**
   - % of queries where mentioned
   - By intent cluster
   - By competitor

2. **Calculate Exclusion Rate:**
   - % of queries where not mentioned
   - Displacement analysis
   - Opportunity identification

3. **Document Patterns:**
   - Inclusion patterns
   - Exclusion patterns
   - Competitive displacement

#### Step 5: Mention Quality Analysis (45 minutes)

1. **Categorize Mentions:**
   - Recommendations
   - Neutral mentions
   - Negative mentions

2. **Calculate Quality Scores:**
   - Your quality score
   - Competitor quality scores
   - Competitive comparison

3. **Identify Opportunities:**
   - Quality improvement opportunities
   - Competitive threats
   - Optimization priorities

#### Step 6: Dashboard Creation (90 minutes)

1. **Create Visual Dashboard:**
   - Share of voice charts
   - Inclusion/exclusion charts
   - Quality score charts
   - Competitive comparison charts

2. **Document Insights:**
   - Key findings
   - Competitive position
   - Opportunities
   - Threats

3. **Set Benchmarks:**
   - Current benchmarks
   - Target benchmarks
   - Success metrics

### Deliverables

1. **Intent Cluster Map:**
   - Complete cluster list
   - Query mapping
   - Priority ranking

2. **Query Test Results:**
   - All query responses
   - Brand mention data
   - Citation data

3. **Share of Voice Calculations:**
   - Cluster-level SOV
   - Aggregate SOV
   - Competitive SOV

4. **Inclusion/Exclusion Analysis:**
   - Inclusion rates
   - Exclusion rates
   - Displacement patterns

5. **Mention Quality Report:**
   - Quality scores
   - Competitive comparison
   - Improvement opportunities

6. **Share of Voice Dashboard:**
   - Visual dashboard
   - Key insights
   - Benchmarks and targets

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Completeness:** All sections completed
- **Accuracy:** Calculations correct
- **Depth:** Analysis goes beyond surface level
- **Actionability:** Insights lead to specific actions
- **Visualization:** Dashboard is clear and useful

## Key Takeaways

- **Traditional SOV is insufficient:** AI-native metrics are required
- **Intent clusters matter:** More accurate than aggregate metrics
- **Inclusion vs exclusion:** Both are critical for competitive analysis
- **Mention quality counts:** Recommendations > neutral mentions
- **Displacement is a threat:** Monitor and defend against competitive displacement
