# Vertical-Specific AI Visibility Playbooks

## Course Overview

**Duration:** 8-10 weeks  
**Level:** Senior / Director / Executive  
**Time Commitment:** 8-10 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Experience in AI strategy, marketing, or product leadership; understanding of industry-specific challenges

By completing this course, you will be able to:

- **Prevent Suppression**: Prevent suppression and misinformation before damage occurs
- **Advise Executives**: Advise executives with confidence and credibility
- **proprietary, defensible AI strategy IP Development**: Build proprietary, defensible AI strategy IP

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why AI Visibility Is Vertical-Specific](Module_01_Why_AI_Visibility_Is_Vertical_Specific.md)
- The End of Universal Best Practices: Understanding why AI visibility behaves differently by industry
- How AI models internalise industry norms
- Citation bias by vertical (data, authority, recency, regulation)
- Suppression vs ranking vs silence
- **Practical Output:** Vertical AI Maturity Assessment

### [Module 2: How AI Learns and Enforces Industry Rules](Module_02_How_AI_Learns_and_Enforces_Industry_Rules.md)
- Implicit Regulation Inside LLMs: How AI encodes compliance, safety, and credibility by sector
- Regulated vs unregulated vertical dynamics
- Soft suppression vs hard exclusion
- Industry-specific "safe language" patterns
- **Practical Output:** Vertical Suppression & Risk Map

### [Module 3: What AI Cites in Your Vertical](Module_03_What_AI_Cites_in_Your_Vertical.md)
- Authority Is Contextual: Identifying which sources AI trusts in your industry
- Primary vs secondary authority sources
- Role of standards bodies, regulators, trade publications
- Impact of PR vs product data vs UGC by vertical
- **Practical Output:** AI Citation Landscape by Vertical

### [Module 4: Vertical AI Share of Voice](Module_04_Vertical_AI_Share_of_Voice.md)
- Measuring Competitive Presence Inside Answers: Moving from generic share of voice to AI-native competitive analysis
- Intent-cluster-based AI share of voice
- Brand inclusion vs exclusion analysis
- Neutral mentions vs recommendations
- **Practical Output:** Vertical AI Share of Voice Dashboard

### [Module 5: Misinformation & Hallucination Risk by Industry](Module_05_Misinformation_and_Hallucination_Risk_by_Industry.md)
- Where AI Gets Dangerous: Detecting and prioritising AI-generated misinformation risks
- Industry-specific hallucination patterns
- Product misrepresentation vs advisory errors
- Legal, regulatory, and reputational thresholds
- **Practical Output:** Vertical AI Risk Register

### [Module 6: Building the Vertical AI Playbook](Module_06_Building_the_Vertical_AI_Playbook.md)
- Turning Patterns into Defensible IP: Codifying learnings into repeatable playbooks
- Playbook structure: What AI rewards, suppresses, converts, and creates risk
- Updating playbooks as models evolve
- Protecting proprietary insights
- **Practical Output:** Draft Vertical AI Visibility Playbook

### [Module 7: Guiding Product, PR & Compliance](Module_07_Guiding_Product_PR_and_Compliance.md)
- Orchestration, Not Advice: Translating playbooks into operational guidance
- Product data standards by vertical
- PR and authority-building strategies
- Compliance-safe content design
- **Practical Output:** Vertical-Specific Guidance Briefs

### [Module 8: Executive Advisory & Competitive Threats](Module_08_Executive_Advisory_and_Competitive_Threats.md)
- Making AI Strategy Board-Relevant: Advising leadership on AI-driven threats and opportunities
- Competitive win/loss narratives inside AI answers
- Scenario planning by vertical
- When AI visibility becomes material risk
- **Practical Output:** Executive AI Threat Brief

### [Module 9: Measurement, Adoption & Governance](Module_09_Measurement_Adoption_and_Governance.md)
- Ensuring the Playbook Actually Gets Used: Driving adoption across business units
- Adoption KPIs by team and function
- Tracking suppression and misinformation incidents
- Continuous improvement loops
- **Capstone Project:** Complete Vertical AI Visibility Program
