## Course Objective

This course teaches how to build and operate an **Experience & AI Product Operating System** — the product practices, decision frameworks, and design patterns required to embed AI into **user-facing experiences** responsibly and effectively.

**Outcome:** Learners can design and ship **AI-powered product experiences that users trust, adopt, and repeatedly use**.

## Course Modules

### [Module 1: AI Product Thinking & Opportunity Discovery](Module_01_AI_Product_Thinking_and_Opportunity_Discovery.md)
**Theme:** When should a product use AI?
- AI features vs traditional product features
- Identifying problems that benefit from intelligence
- Prediction, decisioning, and generation as product primitives
- Avoiding "AI for AI's sake"
- **Hands-on:** Evaluate existing product features and identify AI upgrade opportunities

### [Module 2: AI Feature Discovery & UX Design](Module_02_AI_Feature_Discovery_and_UX_Design.md)
**Theme:** Designing experiences, not models
- UX challenges unique to AI-powered features
- Communicating uncertainty and confidence
- Progressive disclosure in AI experiences
- Designing feedback loops for learning systems
- **Hands-on:** Design user flows for an AI-powered feature

### [Module 3: Decisioning & Personalization Systems](Module_03_Decisioning_and_Personalization_Systems.md)
**Theme:** Turning intelligence into action
- Rule-based vs ML-driven decisioning
- Personalization architectures and trade-offs
- Real-time vs batch decisioning
- Experimentation and guardrails in AI decisions
- **Hands-on:** Design a decisioning system for a personalized user journey

### [Module 4: GenAI Product Patterns](Module_04_GenAI_Product_Patterns.md)
**Theme:** Applying generative AI responsibly
- Core GenAI capabilities (text, image, audio)
- Common GenAI product patterns: Assistants, copilots, content generation
- Prompt design as a product concern
- Cost, latency, and quality trade-offs
- **Hands-on:** Design a GenAI-powered feature with clear user value

### [Module 5: Human-in-the-Loop & Trust Mechanisms](Module_05_Human_in_the_Loop_and_Trust_Mechanisms.md)
**Theme:** Designing for confidence, not blind automation
- When humans should intervene in AI systems
- Approval, override, and review patterns
- Designing feedback collection for learning
- Transparency and explainability at the UX level
- **Hands-on:** Add human-in-the-loop controls to an existing AI feature design

### [Module 6: Ethical & Responsible AI in Product Design](Module_06_Ethical_and_Responsible_AI_in_Product_Design.md)
**Theme:** Responsibility is a product requirement
- Bias, fairness, and representation in AI products
- Privacy and consent in AI-driven experiences
- Regulatory considerations (high-level)
- Long-term vs short-term impact trade-offs
- **Hands-on:** Conduct an ethical risk assessment for an AI feature

### [Module 7: Measuring AI Product Success](Module_07_Measuring_AI_Product_Success.md)
**Theme:** Adoption over accuracy
- Product metrics vs model metrics
- Measuring trust, engagement, and satisfaction
- Experimentation strategies for AI features
- Interpreting mixed or ambiguous results
- **Hands-on:** Define success metrics and experimentation plans

### [Capstone Project: Build an AI Product OS](Module_08_Capstone_Project_Build_an_AI_Product_OS.md)
**Theme:** End-to-end AI-powered user experience
- Identify a user problem suitable for AI
- Design the AI-powered experience and decisioning logic
- Incorporate GenAI and/or personalization patterns
- Address trust, ethics, and human oversight
- Define success metrics and rollout strategy
- **Final Deliverables:** Complete product proposal with all components
