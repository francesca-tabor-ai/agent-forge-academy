# Module 7: Regional Strategy & Place-Based Growth

**Focus:** Ensuring AI benefits are distributed beyond London and the South East.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand AI growth zones strategy and planning reform
- Recognize the role of clusters and devolution in place-based growth
- Analyze how to bridge R&D powerhouses with industrial bases
- Evaluate regional distribution of AI benefits

### Bridging the Gap

**Connecting R&D with Industrial Bases:**

**The Problem:**

## Key Takeaways

1. **AI growth zones:** Designated zones with planning reform attract data center investment (North/South Wales, Oxford-Cambridge)
2. **Devolution:** Mayors and Combined Authorities tailor skills and infrastructure to local strengths
3. **Bridging gap:** Connect R&D powerhouses (universities) with industrial bases (North/Midlands) to diffuse innovation
4. **Regional distribution:** Ensure AI benefits reach beyond London and South East
