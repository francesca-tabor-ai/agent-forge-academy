# Module 6: The Preventive Turn

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the shift from treatment to prevention
- Explore population health management approaches
- Analyze predictive analytics for early intervention
- Recognize the potential and challenges of preventive AI

## Core Topics

### Shifting to Prevention

**Using AI for Early Detection and Risk Stratification:**

**Traditional Model:**
- Reactive treatment
- Treat when problems occur
- Focus on acute care
- Limited prevention

**Preventive Model:**
- Early detection
- Risk identification
- Preventive interventions
- Proactive care

**AI's Role:**
- Identifies risks early
- Enables preventive action
- Shifts resources to prevention
- Better outcomes

**Benefits:**
- Better health outcomes
- Reduced costs
- Improved quality of life
- More efficient resource use

**Challenges:**
- Identifying at-risk populations
- Intervention effectiveness
- Resource allocation
- Privacy and consent

### Predictive Analytics

**Identifying Patients at Risk:**

**Hospital Admission Prediction:**
- Identify patients at risk
- Predict likelihood of admission
- Enable early intervention
- Prevent admissions

**How It Works:**
- Analyzes patient data
- Identifies risk factors
- Predicts admission likelihood
- Triggers interventions

**Interventions:**
- Early care coordination
- Preventive services
- Support programs
- Resource allocation

**Missed Appointment Prediction:**
- Predict likelihood of missed appointments
- Identify at-risk patients
- Enable proactive contact
- Reduce no-shows

**Fall Risk Prediction:**
- Identify patients at risk of falls
- Predict fall likelihood
- Enable preventive measures
- Reduce fall-related harm

**Benefits:**
- Early intervention
- Better outcomes
- Reduced costs
- Improved care

**Challenges:**

- Intervention effectiveness
- Privacy concerns
- Resource requirements

## Preventive Care Framework

**Key Elements:**
- **Early Detection:** Identify risks early
- **Risk Stratification:** Prioritize interventions
- **Preventive Interventions:** Act before problems occur
- **Population Health:** System-level approach

**Implementation:**
- Data infrastructure
- Predictive models
- Intervention programs
- Evaluation and improvement

## Key Takeaways

1. **Prevention focus:** AI enables shift from treatment to prevention
2. **Population health:** System-level analysis improves health outcomes
3. **Predictive analytics:** Early risk identification enables intervention
4. **Resource shift:** Prevention can be more efficient than treatment
