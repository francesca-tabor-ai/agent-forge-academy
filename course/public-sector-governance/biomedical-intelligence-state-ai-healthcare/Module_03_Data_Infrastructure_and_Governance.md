# Module 3: Data Infrastructure and Governance

## Learning Objectives

- Understand health data as national infrastructure
- Recognize interoperability and linkage requirements
- Explore secure data environments and privacy protection
- Apply Caldicott Principles and GDPR compliance

## Core Topics

### Data as National Infrastructure

**Treating Health Data as Strategic Asset:**

**Not Just a By-Product:**
- Health data is strategic resource
- Not just output of care
- Valuable for research and innovation
- Must be managed strategically

**Strategic Value:**
- Research and development
- Population health insights
- Service improvement
- Innovation enablement

**Infrastructure Thinking:**
- Data as foundational resource
- Requires investment and maintenance
- Enables other capabilities
- Strategic asset management

**Public Interest:**
- Data belongs to public
- Must serve public good
- Managed for public benefit
- Not ceded to private actors

### Interoperability & Linkage

**Breaking Down Data Silos:**

**The Problem:**
- Data silos between systems
- Primary care separate from hospitals
- Social care disconnected
- No longitudinal view

**Interoperability Requirements:**
- Systems can communicate
- Data can flow between systems
- Common standards and formats
- Integrated workflows

**Longitudinal Records:**
- Complete patient history
- Across care settings
- Over time
- Comprehensive view

**Benefits:**
- Better care coordination
- Reduced duplication
- Improved outcomes
- Research capabilities

**Challenges:**
- Technical integration
- Privacy and security
- Governance and consent
- Resource requirements

### Secure Environments

**Secure Data Environments (SDEs):**

**From Data Sharing to Data Access:**
- **Data Sharing:** Sending data out (risky)
- **Data Access:** Researchers come to data (safer)
- **Privacy Protection:** Data stays in secure environment
- **Controlled Access:** Researchers access under supervision

**How SDEs Work:**
- Data stays in secure environment
- Researchers access remotely
- No data leaves environment
- Results reviewed before release

**Benefits:**
- Privacy protection
- Security maintained
- Research enabled
- Trust preserved

**OpenSAFELY Case Study:**
- Secure analytics platform
- Analyzes NHS records
- No personal information exposed
- Privacy-preserving research

**Key Features:**
- Secure environment
- Privacy-preserving
- Research enabled
- Trust maintained

**Federated Data Platform:**
- Common platform connecting datasets
- Enables workflow logic
- Integrated systems
- Shared infrastructure

**Benefits:**
- Data integration
- Workflow coordination
- Reduced duplication
- Better insights

### Information Governance (IG)

**Applying Caldicott Principles:**

**Caldicott Principles:**
1. **Justify Purpose:** Every use of data must be justified
2. **Don't Use Unless Necessary:** Only use when necessary
3. **Use Minimum Necessary:** Use minimum data required
4. **Access on Strict Need-to-Know:** Limit access
5. **Everyone Understands Responsibilities:** Clear roles
6. **Comply with Law:** Legal compliance
7. **Duty to Share:** Share when appropriate for care

**GDPR Compliance:**
- **Lawful Basis:** Legal basis for processing
- **Purpose Limitation:** Process for specified purposes
- **Data Minimization:** Collect only necessary data
- **Accuracy:** Keep data accurate
- **Storage Limitation:** Retain only as long as needed
- **Security:** Protect data appropriately
- **Accountability:** Demonstrate compliance

**IG Framework:**
- **Policies:** Clear information governance policies
- **Procedures:** Standard operating procedures
- **Training:** Staff training and awareness
- **Monitoring:** Ongoing compliance monitoring
- **Audit:** Regular audits and reviews

## Data Governance Framework

**Key Elements:**
- **Strategic Management:** Data as strategic asset
- **Interoperability:** Systems work together
- **Security:** Secure environments and access controls
- **Privacy:** Caldicott Principles and GDPR compliance
- **Governance:** Clear policies and procedures

## Key Takeaways

1. **Data is infrastructure:** Health data is strategic asset, not by-product
2. **Interoperability essential:** Must break down silos for longitudinal records
3. **Secure environments:** SDEs protect privacy while enabling research
4. **Governance matters:** Caldicott Principles and GDPR provide framework
