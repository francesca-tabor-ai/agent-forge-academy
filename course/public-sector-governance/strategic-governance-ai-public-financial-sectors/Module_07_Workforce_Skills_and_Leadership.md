# Module 7: Workforce, Skills, and Leadership

**Objective:** To prepare the human workforce for the AI transition.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the "Copilot" model of AI augmentation
- Address upskilling needs in the public sector
- Establish leadership accountability for AI systems
- Build AI literacy at all organizational levels

## Core Topics

### 7.1 The "Copilot" Model

**Viewing AI as Augmentation, Not Just Automation:**

**Augmentation vs Automation:**
- **Automation:** Replacing human work
- **Augmentation:** Enhancing human capabilities
- **Copilot Model:** AI as assistant and collaborator
- **Value Creation:** Freeing humans for higher-value work

**Key Principles:**
- AI enhances, not replaces, human judgment
- Humans remain in control
- AI handles routine tasks
- Humans focus on complex, value-added work

**Benefits:**
- Increased productivity
- Better decision-making
- Enhanced capabilities
- Job enrichment

**Applications:**
- **Administrative Tasks:** Automating routine paperwork
- **Data Analysis:** AI assists with data processing
- **Research:** AI helps gather and synthesize information
- **Decision Support:** AI provides insights for human decisions

**Workforce Implications:**
- Shift in job roles and responsibilities
- Need for new skills
- Different ways of working
- Enhanced human-AI collaboration

**Challenges:**
- Resistance to change
- Skill gaps
- Trust in AI systems
- Job displacement concerns

### 7.2 Upskilling the Public Sector

**Target: 6% of Civil Service in Digital/Data Roles:**
- Current gap between current state and target
- Need for significant upskilling
- Recruitment and retention challenges
- Career development pathways

**Tiered Learning Approach:**

**Beginners:**
- Basic AI awareness
- Understanding AI capabilities and limitations
- Safe use of AI tools
- Ethical considerations

**Intermediate:**
- Practical AI skills
- Using AI tools effectively
- Understanding AI systems
- Risk awareness

**Advanced:**
- AI development and deployment
- Governance and risk management
- Strategic AI planning
- Leadership in AI transformation

**Leaders:**
- AI strategy and vision
- Governance and accountability
- Risk management
- Organizational transformation

**Learning Pathways:**
- **Formal Training:** Courses and certifications
- **On-the-Job Learning:** Practical experience
- **Mentoring:** Learning from experts
- **Communities of Practice:** Peer learning

**Key Skills:**
- **Technical:** Understanding AI systems
- **Governance:** Risk management and compliance
- **Ethics:** Ethical AI use
- **Communication:** Explaining AI to non-technical audiences

**Challenges:**
- Scale of upskilling needed
- Resource constraints
- Time for training
- Keeping skills current

**Strategies:**
- Prioritize critical roles
- Blend learning approaches
- Create learning pathways
- Measure progress

### 7.3 Leadership Accountability

**Senior Responsible Owner (SRO):**
- Ultimate accountability for AI systems
- Strategic oversight
- Risk ownership
- Decision-making authority

**SRO Responsibilities:**
- **Strategic:** Setting direction and vision
- **Governance:** Ensuring appropriate governance
- **Risk:** Owning and managing risks
- **Accountability:** Answerable for outcomes

**AI Literacy at Board/Executive Level:**
- Understanding AI capabilities and risks
- Strategic decision-making about AI
- Risk oversight
- Resource allocation

**Why Board Literacy Matters:**
- Strategic decisions require AI understanding
- Risk oversight needs AI knowledge
- Resource allocation requires AI awareness
- Accountability demands AI competence

**Board Responsibilities:**
- **Strategy:** AI strategy and vision
- **Risk:** AI risk oversight
- **Governance:** AI governance structures
- **Resources:** AI investment decisions

**Building Board Literacy:**
- **Training:** AI education for board members
- **Advisors:** Expert advisors and consultants
- **Briefings:** Regular AI briefings
- **Governance:** AI governance frameworks

**Leadership Challenges:**
- Keeping up with rapid change
- Balancing innovation and risk
- Making decisions with uncertainty
- Building organizational capability

**Success Factors:**
- Committed leadership
- Clear accountability
- Appropriate governance
- Continuous learning

## Organizational Transformation

**Cultural Change:**
- Embracing AI as augmentation
- Building trust in AI systems
- Encouraging experimentation
- Learning from failures

**Change Management:**
- Clear communication
- Stakeholder engagement
- Training and support
- Addressing concerns

**Capability Building:**
- Skills development
- Recruitment
- Retention
- Career pathways

**Governance:**
- Clear accountability
- Appropriate oversight
- Risk management
- Continuous improvement

## Key Takeaways

1. **AI as augmentation:** The "Copilot" model enhances human capabilities
2. **Upskilling is essential:** Significant investment needed in digital/data skills
3. **Leadership matters:** SROs and boards need AI literacy for accountability
4. **Organizational transformation:** Requires cultural change and capability building

4. What are the key responsibilities of a Senior Responsible Owner for AI systems?
