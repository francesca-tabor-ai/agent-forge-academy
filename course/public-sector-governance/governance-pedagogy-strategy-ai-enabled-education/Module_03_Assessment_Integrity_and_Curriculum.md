# Module 3: Assessment, Integrity, and Curriculum

**Focus:** Addressing the disruption of traditional testing and the necessary evolution of curriculum design.

## Learning Objectives

- Evaluate the shift from standardized testing to continuous AI-driven assessment
- Understand limitations of AI text detectors and AI-resilient assessment methods
- Integrate AI literacy into curriculum
- Navigate intellectual property issues in AI-generated content

## Core Topics

### Unit 3.1: The End of Standardised Testing?

**Evaluating the Shift to Continuous Assessment:**

**Traditional Model:**
- Episodic high-stakes exams
- Standardized assessments
- Snapshot evaluation
- Limited frequency

**AI-Enabled Model:**
- Continuous assessment
- Real-time evaluation
- Ongoing feedback
- Adaptive assessment

**Benefits:**
- More frequent assessment
- Real-time feedback
- Better learning insights
- Reduced exam stress

**Challenges:**
- Assessment design
- Quality assurance
- Validity and reliability
- Implementation complexity

**The Shift:**
- **From:** Episodic exams
- **To:** Continuous assessment
- **From:** Standardized tests
- **To:** Adaptive assessment
- **From:** Snapshot
- **To:** Ongoing evaluation

### Unit 3.2: Malpractice and Academic Integrity

**Understanding Limitations of AI Text Detectors:**

**AI Text Detectors:**
- Attempt to identify AI-generated text
- Limited accuracy
- High false positive rates
- Unreliable

**Limitations:**
- Cannot reliably detect AI text
- False positives common
- Easy to evade
- Not reliable for assessment

**AI-Resilient Assessment Methods:**

**Oral Vivas:**
- Spoken assessment
- Real-time evaluation
- Difficult to fake
- Demonstrates understanding

**In-Person Verification:**
- Face-to-face assessment
- Direct observation
- Authentic evaluation
- Reduced cheating

**Other Methods:**
- Project-based assessment
- Portfolio evaluation
- Practical demonstrations
- Continuous observation

**Benefits:**
- More authentic assessment
- Reduced cheating
- Better evaluation
- Real understanding

## Assessment and Curriculum Framework

**Key Elements:**
- **Continuous Assessment:** Real-time, ongoing evaluation
- **AI-Resilient Methods:** Oral vivas, in-person verification
- **AI Literacy:** Integrated into curriculum
- **IP Compliance:** Legal and ethical considerations

## Key Takeaways

1. **Continuous assessment:** AI enables shift from episodic exams to ongoing evaluation
2. **Detectors unreliable:** AI text detectors cannot reliably identify AI-generated work
3. **AI-resilient methods:** Oral vivas and in-person verification are more reliable
4. **AI literacy essential:** Must be integrated into curriculum across subjects
5. **IP matters:** Copyright and data protection must be considered

## Reflection Questions

1. How does continuous AI-driven assessment differ from traditional standardized testing?
2. Why are AI text detectors unreliable, and what are better assessment methods?
3. How should AI literacy be integrated into the curriculum?
4. What are the intellectual property implications of AI-generated content and student data?
5. How do you design assessments that are both AI-resilient and educationally valuable?
