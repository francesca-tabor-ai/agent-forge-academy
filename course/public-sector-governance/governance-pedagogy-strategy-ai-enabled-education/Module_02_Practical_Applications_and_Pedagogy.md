### Unit 2.4: The "Human in the Loop"

**Ensuring AI Enhances Teacher-Student Relationships:**

**AI as Enhancement:**
- AI supports teachers
- Does not replace teachers
- Enhances capabilities
- Augments judgment

**Professional Judgment:**
- Teachers make final decisions
- AI provides information
- Human judgment essential
- Professional expertise

**Teacher-Student Relationship:**
- Human connection maintained
- AI supports, not replaces
- Empathy and care
- Personal interaction

**Design Principles:**
- AI augments, not automates
- Human judgment central
- Relationship preserved
- Professional expertise valued

**Implementation:**
- Clear roles and boundaries
- Training on AI use
- Professional development
- Support and guidance

## Pedagogical Framework

**Key Principles:**
- **Enhancement:** AI enhances teaching, not replaces
- **Personalization:** Adapts to individual needs
- **Accessibility:** Supports all learners
- **Human-Centered:** Maintains human relationships

## Key Takeaways

1. **Workload reduction:** AI can significantly reduce teacher administrative burden
2. **Personalization:** Adaptive tutoring systems enable personalized learning at scale
3. **Accessibility:** AI supports SEND and EAL students through various tools
4. **Human-centered:** AI must enhance, not replace, teacher-student relationships
