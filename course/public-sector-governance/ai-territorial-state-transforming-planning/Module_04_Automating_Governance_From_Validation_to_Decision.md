# Module 4: Automating Governance: From Validation to Decision

**Learning Objective:** Explore how AI assists in the administrative workflow of planning, from validation to decision-making.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand AI automation in validation processes
- Analyze the future of AI-augmented decision-making
- Recognize benefits and limitations of automation
- Evaluate the role of human judgment

## Core Topics

### Topic A: Automating Validation

**The Integration of AI into Planning Portal:**

**Planning Portal Integration:**
- AI checks applications against rules
- Validates before human review
- Reduces invalid applications
- Speeds up process

**PlanX Services:**
- AI-powered validation
- Rule-based checking
- Automated compliance
- Pre-application advice

**How It Works:**
- Application submitted
- AI checks against rules
- Flags issues or confirms compliance
- Human reviews flagged cases

**Benefits:**
- Faster validation
- Reduced invalid applications
- Consistent checking
- Resource efficiency

**Doncaster Council Case Study:**
- Significant reduction in validation time
- Faster pre-application advice
- Improved efficiency
- Better service delivery

**Reducing Red Tape:**
- Automated checking
- Faster processing
- Clear guidance
- Reduced delays

### Topic B: The Future of Decision Making

**Government Tenders for AI Tools:**

**AI-Augmented Decision Making:**
- AI assists decision-making
- Not fully automated
- Human oversight maintained
- Augmented, not replaced

**Simple Householder Developments:**
- Routine, low-risk cases
- Clear rules and criteria
- Suitable for AI assistance
- Faster determinations

**Near-Instant Determinations:**
- Goal for routine cases
- Automated processing
- Fast outcomes
- Resource efficiency

**Freeing Up Planners:**
- Routine cases automated
- Planners focus on complex cases
- Better use of expertise
- Improved outcomes

**Complex Regeneration Projects:**
- Require human judgment
- Need professional expertise
- Complex trade-offs
- Strategic decisions

**The Balance:**
- **Routine Cases:** AI-augmented, fast
- **Complex Cases:** Human judgment, careful
- **Appropriate Use:** Right tool for right case
- **Human Oversight:** Always maintained

## Automation Framework

**Validation Automation:**
- Rule-based checking
- Automated compliance
- Human review of flags
- Faster processing

**Decision Automation:**
- AI assistance for routine cases
- Human judgment for complex cases
- Appropriate use of technology
- Maintained oversight

## Key Takeaways

1. **Validation automation:** AI checks applications against rules, reducing invalid applications
2. **Faster processing:** Automation speeds up validation and pre-application advice
3. **AI-augmented decisions:** Government exploring AI assistance for simple householder developments
4. **Near-instant goals:** Routine cases could be determined quickly, freeing planners for complex projects
5. **Human judgment:** Complex cases still require professional expertise

## Reflection Questions

1. How does AI automation in validation reduce invalid applications and speed up processing?
2. What are the benefits and risks of AI-augmented decision-making for planning?
3. How do you distinguish between routine cases suitable for AI and complex cases requiring human judgment?
4. What is the goal of "near-instant" determinations, and is it achievable?
5. How do you ensure human oversight is maintained while benefiting from AI automation?
