# Module 5: The Innovation Ecosystem and State Capacity

**Learning Objective:** Evaluate the role of the private sector (PropTech) and the government's role as a "systems integrator."

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the PropTech sector and its growth
- Recognize barriers to adoption and solutions
- Explore Open Digital Planning (ODP) collaboration
- Analyze DLUHC's shift to digital systems integrator

## Core Topics

### Topic A: The PropTech Sector

**The Growth of PropTech:**

**Projected Revenue:**
- £72bn revenue by 2032
- Significant growth sector
- Innovation and investment
- Market opportunity

**Barriers to Adoption:**

**Procurement:**
- Complex procurement processes
- Difficult for LPAs to access
- Limited resources
- Time-consuming

**Other Barriers:**
- Skills gaps
- Cultural resistance
- Integration challenges
- Cost concerns

**Open Digital Planning (ODP):**

**Network of Councils:**
- Councils collaborating
- Share data and software
- Common platforms
- Reduced duplication

**Back Office Planning System (BOPS):**
- Shared software system
- Common platform
- Reduced costs
- Better integration

**Benefits:**
- Cost sharing
- Knowledge sharing
- Standardization
- Innovation

**Collaboration Model:**
- Councils work together
- Share resources
- Common standards
- Collective benefit

### Topic B: Rebuilding State Capacity

**DLUHC as Digital Systems Integrator:**

**From Policy Department:**
- Policy development
- Guidance and regulation
- Limited technical involvement
- Coordination role

**To Digital Systems Integrator:**
- Design and operate systems
- Build technical infrastructure
- Integrate digital platforms
- Direct technical capability

**Key Shift:**
- **From:** Policy coordination
- **To:** System design and operation
- **From:** Guidance
- **To:** Technical infrastructure
- **From:** External coordination
- **To:** Direct capability

**Funding Innovation:**

**PropTech Innovation Fund:**
- Supports innovation pilots
- Funds local authority projects
- Enables experimentation
- Drives adoption

**Digital Planning Improvement Fund:**
- Supports digital planning improvements
- Funds infrastructure
- Enables modernization
- Builds capability

**Digital Planning Directory:**
- Connects LPAs with suppliers
- Vetted suppliers
- Overcomes procurement hurdles
- Enables access to innovation

**Benefits:**
- Faster procurement
- Access to innovation
- Reduced barriers
- Better tools

## Innovation Ecosystem

**Key Players:**
- **PropTech Companies:** Innovation and tools
- **LPAs:** Users and adopters
- **DLUHC:** Systems integrator
- **ODP Network:** Collaboration

**Enablers:**
- **Funding:** Innovation funds
- **Directory:** Supplier connections
- **Standards:** Common platforms
- **Collaboration:** ODP network

## Key Takeaways

1. **PropTech growth:** £72bn sector by 2032, significant innovation
2. **Procurement barriers:** Complex processes limit adoption, solutions needed
3. **ODP collaboration:** Councils sharing data and software reduces costs
4. **State as integrator:** DLUHC shifts from policy to systems integration
5. **Innovation support:** Funds and directories enable adoption

## Reflection Questions

1. What are the barriers to PropTech adoption in local planning authorities?
2. How does Open Digital Planning (ODP) enable collaboration and reduce costs?
3. What does it mean for DLUHC to be a "digital systems integrator"?
4. How do innovation funds and directories support PropTech adoption?
5. What is the role of the state vs. private sector in planning innovation?
