## Implementation Framework

**Operationalizing AI:**
1. **Planning:** Identify use cases and requirements
2. **Procurement:** Buy or build decisions
3. **Development:** Build or customize solutions
4. **Deployment:** Deploy with governance
5. **Operation:** Monitor and maintain
6. **Evaluation:** Measure outcomes and improve

**Governance:**
- Clear accountability
- Risk management
- Quality assurance
- Continuous improvement

## Key Takeaways

1. **AI Playbook guides practice:** Practical guidance for buying, building, and using AI
2. **Procurement is strategic:** Can shape market and enforce standards
3. **Evaluation must be human-centric:** Focus on user outcomes, not just technical metrics
4. **Skills gap must be addressed:** Need intelligent clients and operators
