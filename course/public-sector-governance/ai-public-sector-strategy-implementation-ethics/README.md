# AI in the Public Sector – Strategy, Implementation, and Ethics

## Course Overview

This course provides a structured guide to understanding, procuring, and deploying Artificial Intelligence within government organizations. It moves beyond the "hype" to focus on practical application, drawing directly from the UK Government's 2025/2026 strategic roadmap and the AI Playbook.

## Course Description

This course provides a structured guide to understanding, procuring, and deploying Artificial Intelligence within government organizations. It moves beyond the "hype" to focus on practical application, drawing directly from the UK Government's 2025/2026 strategic roadmap and the AI Playbook.

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence
**Objective:** Define AI, understand its sub-fields, and demystify the terminology used in government policy.

**Key Topics:**
- Defining AI: OECD definition and moving beyond science fiction
- The Fields of AI: Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing
- Generative AI: Large Language Models and probabilistic models
- Agentic AI: Autonomous systems and multi-step tasks

### Module 2: Strategic Context & Government Vision
**Objective:** Understand where AI fits into the UK's broader digital strategy and the machinery of government changes.

**Key Topics:**
- The "Digital Centre" of Government: GDS, CDDO, i.AI moving to DSIT
- The National AI Strategy: Three-pillar approach
- The Roadmap for Modern Digital Government: 6-point plan for 2025-2030
- The Economic Case: Productivity gains and transformation

### Module 3: The 10 Principles of Government AI Use
**Objective:** Master the core ethical and operational framework mandated by the AI Playbook.

**Key Topics:**
- Principles 1-3: Knowledge & Safety
- Principles 4-6: Control & Lifecycle
- Principles 7-10: Collaboration & Assurance

### Module 4: Ethics, Safety, and Trust
**Objective:** Navigate the risks associated with AI to build public trust and ensure compliance.

**Key Topics:**
- Bias and Fairness: Representational bias and Equality Act 2010
- Transparency: Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS)
- Privacy & Data Protection: DPIAs and GDPR principles
- Generative AI Risks: Hallucinations, deepfakes, misinformation

### Module 5: Building and Buying AI Solutions
**Objective:** Practical guidance for project managers, commercial leads, and technical teams.

**Key Topics:**
- Identifying Use Cases: High-value opportunities vs. use cases to avoid
- The "Build vs. Buy" Decision: IP rights and vendor lock-in
- Routes to Market: CCS frameworks and Dynamic Purchasing Systems
- The Multidisciplinary Team: Required roles for success

### Module 6: Security and Legacy Challenges
**Objective:** Address the technical debt and security threats specific to AI.

**Key Topics:**
- Cyber Security Risks: Prompt injection, data poisoning, model inversion
- Legacy Infrastructure: Out-of-date technology and poor data quality
- Secure by Design: Applying government cyber security standards

### Module 7: Governance and Accountability
**Objective:** Establish the oversight structures required to run AI responsibly.

**Key Topics:**
- Governance Boards: AI Governance Boards and Ethics Committees
- Accountability: Senior Responsible Owners (SROs) and liability chains
- AI Systems Inventory: Maintaining live inventory for audit purposes

### Module 8: Case Studies and Real-World Application
**Objective:** Analyze current public sector deployments to understand practical successes and failures.

**Key Topics:**
- GOV.UK Chat: RAG pilot lessons on accuracy and user trust
- Health & Social Care: NHS stroke detection, demand prediction, COVID-19 imaging
- Operational Efficiency: DWP mail sorting, MoJ consultation analysis
- User Research: Measuring trust and mental models for AI products

## Course Metadata

- **Category:** Public Sector Governance
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate to Advanced
- **Duration:** ~8-10 weeks
- **Best For:** Public sector professionals, project managers, commercial leads, technical teams, policy advisors, governance professionals
- **Industries:** Public Sector, Government, Legal & Compliance

## Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this course, participants will be able to:

1. Understand AI foundations and terminology used in government policy
2. Navigate the UK's strategic context and government vision for AI
3. Apply the 10 Principles of Government AI Use
4. Manage ethics, safety, and trust in AI deployments
5. Make informed decisions about building vs. buying AI solutions
6. Address security and legacy infrastructure challenges
7. Establish effective governance and accountability structures
8. Learn from real-world case studies and apply lessons learned

## Assessment

Each module includes:
- Learning objectives and key topics
- Real-world examples and case studies
- Reflection questions
- Practical application guidance

## Prerequisites

- Understanding of public sector operations
- Basic familiarity with technology and digital transformation
- Interest in AI governance and implementation

## Course Philosophy

This course emphasizes:
- **Practical Application:** Real-world examples from UK government
- **Beyond Hype:** Focus on practical, deployable AI
