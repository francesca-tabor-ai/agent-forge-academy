# Mastering the Agentic Economy

##  Course Overview

**Duration:** 7 weeks (8 total with capstone)  
**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** E-commerce fundamentals, basic AI/ML knowledge

---

##  What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

 Understand the fundamental shift from human-led to agent-led commerce  
 Build trust strategies for AI-mediated transactions  
 Implement Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) and secure payment rails  
 Optimize for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and measure RSOV  
 Design AI-first organizational structures and measure Return on Integrity  
 Navigate the legal landscape of autonomous agency  
 Audit for algorithmic bias using the ACES framework

##  Course Modules

### [Module 1: The Disruption of the Digital Funnel](Module_01_The_Disruption_of_the_Digital_Funnel.md)
- **Lesson 1.1:** The Rise of the Invisible Marketplace. Analyze how commerce is shifting into a fluid loop where AI agents discover, evaluate, and purchase products on behalf of users.
- **Lesson 1.2:** Zero-Click Intent. Explore the "flattening" of the sales funnel into a single step: intent to fulfillment.
- **Lesson 1.3:** Beyond Retail. Case studies on how B2B services, such as accounting and manufacturing, are becoming "agent-mediated".
- **Lab 1:** Market analysis of agent-mediated commerce opportunities

### [Module 2: The Psychology of AI Trust & The Integrity Gap](Module_02_The_Psychology_of_AI_Trust_and_The_Integrity_Gap.md)
- **Lesson 2.1:** The Ability vs. Integrity Paradox. Discuss why consumers rate AI high on technical ability but low on integrity, accountability, and honesty.
- **Lesson 2.2:** The Golden Triangle of Adoption. Implementing the three pillars of successful AI commerce: Trust, Transparency, and Personalization.
- **Lesson 2.3:** Managing Algorithm Aversion. Strategies for using "Hybrid Models" that pair AI efficiency with visible human validation to restore consumer trust.
- **Lab 2:** Design a trust-building strategy for your brand

### [Module 3: Technical Protocols & Infrastructure](Module_03_Technical_Protocols_and_Infrastructure.md)
- **Lesson 3.1:** The Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP). A deep dive into the open standard co-developed by OpenAI and Stripe for secure, tokenized transactions.
- **Lesson 3.2:** Identity & Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs). How agents prove ownership and establish trust relationships across organizational boundaries using Verifiable Credentials (VCs).
- **Lesson 3.3:** Secure Payment Rails. Comparing Stripe's Shared Payment Token (SPT) with Google's Agent Payments Protocol (AP2).
- **Lab 3:** Implement a basic ACP integration

### [Module 4: Marketing Strategy – Winning the "Evaluation Game"](Module_04_Marketing_Strategy_Winning_the_Evaluation_Game.md)
- **Lesson 4.1:** Retrieval-Share of Voice (RSOV). Measuring brand presence inside generative AI answers and identifying "Brand Omission" risks.
- **Lesson 4.2:** Machine-Readable Authority. Structuring product data (Schema.org) and "answer chunks" so AI agents can parse and recommend your brand.
- **Lesson 4.3:** Becoming "Agent-Preferred." Retooling fulfillment, loyalty, and data strategies to ensure your brand is the first choice for buying algorithms.
- **Lab 4:** Optimize product data for AI agent consumption

### [Module 5: Organizational Design & Measurement](Module_05_Organizational_Design_and_Measurement.md)
- **Lesson 5.1:** New Executive Roles. Defining the role of the Chief AI Officer (CAO) and specialized teams like "Reasoning Design".
- **Lesson 5.2:** Synthetic Consumers. Using LLMs to generate virtual consumer panels for rapid, cost-effective concept testing and demand forecasting.
- **Lesson 5.3:** Measuring Return on Integrity. Developing KPIs that track the value of AI for productivity, quality, and human-in-the-loop satisfaction.
- **Lab 5:** Design an AI-first organizational structure

### [Module 6: Governance, Liability, & KYA](Module_06_Governance_Liability_and_KYA.md)
- **Lesson 6.1:** Know Your Agent (KYA). The evolution of KYC (Know Your Customer) to verify agent identity and maintain accountability chains.
- **Lesson 6.2:** Tort Law & AI Agents. Analyzing negligence, products liability, and the distinction between "manufacturing" and "design" defects in AI.
- **Lesson 6.3:** Regulatory Readiness. Aligning AI workflows with the EU AI Act, HIPAA, and financial compliance standards.
- **Lab 6:** Create a compliance framework for agent-mediated transactions

### [Module 7: Auditing for Algorithmic Bias](Module_07_Auditing_for_Algorithmic_Bias.md)
- **Lesson 7.1:** The ACES Framework. Using the Agentic e-CommercE Simulator to audit agents for choice homogeneity and competitive neutrality.
- **Lesson 7.2:** Position & Ranking Biases. Understanding how agents exhibit strong, model-specific biases toward certain page layouts and "Top Pick" tags.
- **Lesson 7.3:** Counteracting Model Shocks. How to respond when model upgrades act as "demand shocks" that reshuffle market shares instantly.
- **Lab 7:** Conduct a bias audit using the ACES framework
