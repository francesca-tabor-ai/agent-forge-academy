## 💡 Study Tips

### Time Management
1. **Block dedicated study time** (8-10 hrs/week)
2. **Complete labs on time** (they build on each other)
3. **Use office hours** (don't struggle alone)
4. **Join study groups** (Discord channels)

### Learning Strategies
1. **Code along with examples** (don't just read)
2. **Break labs when learning** (fix them in safe env)
3. **Document your work** (helps with projects)
4. **Ask questions early** (faster resolution)

### Lab Success
1. **Read entire lab before starting**
2. **Set up environment first**
3. **Test incrementally** (don't wait until end)
4. **Use evaluation criteria** (guide your work)
