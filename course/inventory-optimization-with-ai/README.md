# Inventory Optimization with AI
## Surviving Uncertainty, Not Minimizing Formulas

### Course Goal

Design inventory policies that reduce stockouts and overstock simultaneously by accounting for uncertainty, constraints, and real-world volatility.

### Course Outcome

Participants can build AI-assisted inventory systems that adapt to inflation, long lead times, and demand volatility — and that operators trust in production.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Uncertainty is the problem, not the math**
- **Risk awareness beats optimization**
- **Operators must trust the system**
- **Real-world constraints override theory**
- **Survival beats perfection**

**Tone:** practical, risk-aware, operationally grounded  
**Energy:** supply chain operations + risk management + human-in-the-loop AI  
**Outcome:** inventory systems that work when it matters most

---

## COURSE STRUCTURE

**Duration:** 5–6 weeks

**Format:** Lectures + simulations + applied labs

**Prerequisites:** Demand forecasting basics, statistics, Python

9 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

Each module addresses a real inventory failure pattern that breaks production systems.

---

## DELIVERY FORMAT

- 9 × 90-minute live sessions
- Mixed technical + operations cohorts
- Real inventory scenarios only
- Hands-on simulations and labs
- Capstone project with real constraints

---

## SUCCESS METRICS

Within 90 days:

- Inventory policies that account for uncertainty
- Reduced stockouts without excess inventory
- Systems that adapt to volatility
- Operator trust in AI recommendations
- Clear risk thresholds and override mechanisms

---

## THE REAL GOAL

Not "optimal inventory levels".

But:

**"Inventory optimization is about surviving uncertainty, not minimizing formulas."**
