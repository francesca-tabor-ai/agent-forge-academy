# Capstone Project: Build a Leadership OS

**Duration:** Weeks 7-8  
**Theme:** End-to-end executive data & AI strategy  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Apply all course learnings to a real-world context
- Design a complete Leadership OS
- Integrate strategy, operating model, metrics, portfolio, communication, and culture
- Present and defend strategy at executive/board level

---

## Project Overview

### Objective

Design and present a complete **Leadership & Strategy Operating System** for a data & AI organization. This capstone integrates all course modules into a comprehensive, executive-ready strategy.

### Project Scope

Students will:

1. **Define Business Context:** Understand business strategy, goals, and constraints
2. **Design Data & AI Strategy:** Create a 3-year strategy aligned to business goals
3. **Design Operating Model:** Define organizational structure and operating model
4. **Build KPI Trees and OKRs:** Create measurement framework
5. **Prioritize Portfolio:** Manage portfolio of initiatives
6. **Prepare Executive Communication:** Create executive presentation and decision memo
7. **Plan Culture and Talent:** Design culture and talent strategy
8. **Present and Defend:** Present to mock executive committee or board

**Contents:**

- Value decomposition

- Recommendation

## Additional Guidance

### Tips for Success

**1. Start Early**
- Don't wait until the last minute
- Break work into manageable chunks
- Set milestones

**2. Use Frameworks**
- Apply course frameworks
- Don't reinvent the wheel
- Leverage templates

**3. Get Feedback**
- Seek feedback early and often
- Use office hours
- Peer review

**4. Be Realistic**
- Don't over-promise
- Be honest about constraints
- Show trade-offs

**5. Tell a Story**
- Make it compelling
- Connect the dots
- Show the journey

### Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall 1: Too Much Detail**
- **Problem:** Overwhelming with details
- **Solution:** Focus on key points, use appendices

**Pitfall 2: Not Aligned**
- **Problem:** Strategy not aligned to business
- **Solution:** Start with business context, validate alignment

**Pitfall 3: Unrealistic**
- **Problem:** Unrealistic plans or expectations
- **Solution:** Be honest about constraints, show trade-offs

**Pitfall 4: Poor Communication**
- **Problem:** Technical language, unclear messaging
- **Solution:** Use business language, tell a story

**Pitfall 5: Incomplete**
- **Problem:** Missing components or deliverables
- **Solution:** Use checklist, review requirements
