# Module 3: Advisor Content Generators: Personalized Performance Narratives

**Duration:** Week 3  
**Learning Objectives:**
- **Move Beyond**: Move beyond generic fund factsheets
- **Tailor Narratives**: Tailor narratives to client goals and time horizons
- **Explain Volatility**: Explain volatility in plain language
- **Align Tone:**: Align tone: reassurance vs. opportunity

---

## Lesson 3.1: Moving Beyond Generic Fund Factsheets

### Limitations of Generic Factsheets

**Standard Content**
- Same information for all clients
- Technical language
- No context personalization
- Limited engagement

**Client Experience**
- Generic and impersonal
- Difficult to understand
- Not aligned with goals
- Low engagement

### Personalized Narratives

**Benefits**
- Client-specific context
- Goal-aligned explanations
- Appropriate language level
- Higher engagement

**Components**
- Performance in context
- Goal relevance
- Risk explanation
- Actionable insights

---

## Lesson 3.2: Tailoring Narratives to Client Goals and Time Horizons

### Goal-Aligned Narratives

**Retirement-Focused**
- Long-term growth perspective
- Compounding benefits
- Retirement readiness
- Income generation focus

**Education-Focused**
- Medium-term perspective
- Target amount progress
- Timeline alignment
- Lower risk emphasis

**Wealth Transfer-Focused**
- Multi-generational view
- Legacy preservation
- Tax efficiency
- Transfer readiness

### Time Horizon Adaptation

**Short-Term Focus**
- Stability emphasis
- Liquidity considerations
- Capital preservation
- Reassuring tone

**Long-Term Focus**
- Growth potential
- Volatility context
- Compounding benefits
- Opportunity tone

---

## Lesson 3.3: Explaining Volatility in Plain Language

### Plain Language Principles

**Avoid Jargon**
- Technical terms explained
- Simple language
- Concrete examples
- Relatable analogies

**Contextual Explanation**
- Why volatility matters
- How it affects goals
- Historical perspective
- Expected outcomes

### Volatility Narratives

**For Risk-Averse Clients**
- Reassuring explanations
- Historical context
- Risk mitigation
- Stability focus

**For Growth-Oriented Clients**
- Opportunity framing
- Long-term perspective
- Growth potential
- Volatility as normal

---

## Lesson 3.4: Aligning Tone: Reassurance vs. Opportunity

### Tone Selection

**Reassurance Tone**
- Stability focus
- Risk mitigation
- Capital preservation
- Comforting language

**Opportunity Tone**
- Growth potential
- Market opportunities
- Long-term benefits
- Encouraging language

### Context-Based Tone

**Client Profile Matching**
- Risk profile alignment
- Goal alignment
- Time horizon consideration
- Emotional risk matching

## Exercise 3: Generate Three Different Performance Narratives for the Same Fund

### Objective
Create three personalized performance narratives for the same fund, each tailored to different client goals and contexts.

### Requirements

1. **Client Profiles**
   - Retirement-focused client (long-term, growth-oriented)
   - Education-focused client (medium-term, conservative)
   - Wealth transfer client (multi-generational, balanced)

2. **Narrative Generation**
   - Same fund performance data
   - Different narrative approaches
   - Goal-aligned explanations
   - Appropriate tone

3. **Personalization Elements**
   - Goal relevance
   - Time horizon context
   - Risk explanation
   - Tone alignment

4. **Deliverables**
   - Three narrative examples
   - Personalization framework
   - Tone guidelines
   - Implementation code

### Evaluation Criteria
- Narrative quality (35%)
- Personalization effectiveness (30%)
- Tone appropriateness (20%)
- Implementation quality (15%)

## Key Takeaways

- **Personalized Narratives**: Personalized narratives significantly improve client engagement compared to generic factsheets
- **Goal And**: Goal and time horizon alignment makes performance relevant to clients
- **Plain Language**: Plain language explanations improve understanding and trust
- **Tone Alignment**: Tone alignment with client profile enhances communication effectiveness
