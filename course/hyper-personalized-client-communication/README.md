## Course Modules

### [Module 1: The Personalization Paradox in Regulated Advice](Module_01_The_Personalization_Paradox_in_Regulated_Advice.md)
- Understand why personalization is hard but essential in modern advisory
- Why products increasingly look the same
- The difference between personalization and recommendation
- Regulatory boundaries: information vs. advice
- **Exercise 1:** Identify where personalization adds value without triggering suitability risk

### [Module 2: Client Context: Turning Life Goals into Inputs](Module_02_Client_Context_Turning_Life_Goals_into_Inputs.md)
- Translate qualitative client goals into structured data for AI systems
- Life-stage goals (retirement, education, wealth transfer)
- Time horizons, liquidity needs, and emotional risk
- Behavioral signals vs. declared preferences
- **Exercise 2:** Build a client "context profile" suitable for AI-driven communication

### [Module 3: Advisor Content Generators: Personalized Performance Narratives](Module_03_Advisor_Content_Generators_Personalized_Performance_Narratives.md)
- Use AI to generate client-specific explanations of fund performance
- Moving beyond generic fund factsheets
- Tailoring narratives to client goals and time horizons
- Explaining volatility in plain language
- **Exercise 3:** Generate three different performance narratives for the same fund, tailored to different client goals

### [Module 4: Compliance by Design in AI-Generated Content](Module_04_Compliance_by_Design_in_AI_Generated_Content.md)
- Ensure personalized content remains regulator-ready
- Pre-approved language libraries
- Guardrails to prevent implicit advice
- Version control and audit trails
- **Exercise 4:** Design a compliance approval workflow for AI-generated client communications

### [Module 5: Interactive 24/7 Chatbots: Always-On Client Support](Module_05_Interactive_24_7_Chatbots_Always_On_Client_Support.md)
- Deploy chatbots that enhance service without replacing advisors
- Appropriate use cases for advisory chatbots
- Handling routine queries (rates, terms, documentation)
- Escalation logic for complex or sensitive questions
- **Exercise 5:** Define chatbot boundaries: what it can and cannot answer

### [Module 6: Chatbots as Market Intelligence Engines](Module_06_Chatbots_as_Market_Intelligence_Engines.md)
- Turn client questions into actionable insight
- Identifying friction points in client portals
- Measuring question frequency and abandonment
- Detecting unmet information needs
- **Exercise 6:** Design a dashboard showing top client pain points based on chatbot interactions

### [Module 7: Human-in-the-Loop: Advisors + AI, Not Advisors vs. AI](Module_07_Human_in_the_Loop_Advisors_Plus_AI_Not_Advisors_vs_AI.md)
- Position AI as an augmentation tool, not a replacement
- Advisor review and override mechanisms
- Coaching advisors with AI-driven insights
- Maintaining authenticity in client relationships
- **Exercise 7:** Create a workflow where AI drafts content and the advisor finalizes it

### [Module 8: From Pilot to Platform](Module_08_From_Pilot_to_Platform.md)
- Prepare hyper-personalized communication tools for scale
- Integrating with CRM and advisory platforms
- Monitoring performance, bias, and drift
- Measuring success: engagement, retention, advisor efficiency
- **Capstone Project:** Design a Hyper-Personalized Client Communication Toolkit for a digital or hybrid advisory platform
