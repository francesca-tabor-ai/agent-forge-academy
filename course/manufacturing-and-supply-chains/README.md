# How Manufacturing & Supply Chains Actually Work
## Eliminate Naïve Modeling Assumptions

### Course Promise

**"By the end of this course, participants can engage confidently with operations, supply chain, and procurement teams — and design AI models that respect physical, economic, and human constraints."**

This is not a theoretical supply chain course.  
It is a practical systems understanding course for AI practitioners.

---

## THE VIBE (DESIGN PRINCIPLES)

- **Physical reality beats abstract models**
- **Systems thinking beats local optimization**
- **Latency beats accuracy**
- **Constraints beat assumptions**
- **Operations communication beats technical perfection**

**Tone:** practical, grounded, operationally-aware  
**Energy:** factory floor + data science lab  
**Outcome:** models that work in the real world, not just on paper

## COURSE STRUCTURE

8 CORE MODULES + 1 CAPSTONE PROJECT

---

## MODULE OVERVIEW

### Module 1: The Physical Reality of Supply Chains
Understand that supply chains are physical systems, not abstract graphs.

### Module 2: End-to-End Manufacturing Flows
See how decisions propagate downstream and upstream.

### Module 3: Push vs Pull Systems
Understand why demand signals break.

### Module 4: Bills of Materials (BOMs) & Lead Times
Learn the hidden complexity behind "simple" products.

### Module 5: Buffers, Bottlenecks & Constraints
Model the system's real limits.

### Module 6: Data Is Never Clean
Reset expectations about operational data.

### Module 7: Why Naïve AI Models Fail in Operations
Connect system understanding to modeling mistakes.

### Module 8: Talking to Ops Without Embarrassing Yourself
Build credibility with non-technical teams.

### Capstone: Operations-Aware AI Design
Apply course learnings to a real operational decision.
