## Lesson 8.1: AI Intent Coverage Readiness

### What Is Intent Coverage?

**Definition:** The percentage of high-value intents where you have content, trust signals, and readiness to bid.

**Calculation:**
```
Intent Coverage = (Ready Intents / Total High-Value Intents) × 100
```

**Components of Readiness:**
1. **Content:** High-quality content covering the intent
2. **Trust Signals:** Strong evidence, reviews, authority, freshness
3. **Creative:** AI-native creative formats ready
4. **Bidding Strategy:** Bid levels and allocation defined

### Measuring Intent Coverage

**Step 1: Intent Inventory**
- List all high-value intents
- Categorize by persona
- Rank by revenue potential
- Document intent characteristics

**Step 2: Readiness Assessment**
For each intent, assess:
- **Content Readiness:** Do we have quality content?
- **Trust Signal Readiness:** Do we have strong trust signals?
- **Creative Readiness:** Do we have AI-native creative?
- **Bidding Readiness:** Do we have bidding strategy?

**Step 3: Coverage Calculation**
- Count ready intents
- Calculate coverage percentage
- Identify coverage gaps
- Prioritize gap closure

### Coverage Targets

**Tier 1: Core Intents (Must Have)**
- Highest revenue potential
- Strategic importance
- **Target Coverage:** 100%

**Tier 2: Important Intents (Should Have)**
- High revenue potential
- Growth opportunities
- **Target Coverage:** 80%+

**Tier 3: Secondary Intents (Nice to Have)**
- Moderate revenue potential
- Expansion opportunities
- **Target Coverage:** 50%+

### Coverage Gaps

**Identifying Gaps:**
1. **Content Gaps:**
   - Missing content for intents
   - Low-quality content
   - Outdated content
   - Incomplete coverage

2. **Trust Signal Gaps:**
   - Weak evidence
   - Few reviews
   - Low authority
   - Outdated data

3. **Creative Gaps:**
   - Missing AI-native creative
   - Poor creative quality
   - Incomplete formats
   - Persona misalignment

4. **Bidding Gaps:**
   - Undefined bidding strategy
   - Missing bid levels
   - Unclear allocation
   - No optimization plan

### Closing Coverage Gaps

**Priority Framework:**
1. **High Value + High Gap:** Immediate priority
2. **High Value + Low Gap:** Maintain priority
3. **Low Value + High Gap:** Lower priority
4. **Low Value + Low Gap:** Lowest priority

**Action Plan:**
- Content creation/improvement
- Trust signal building
- Creative development
- Bidding strategy definition

## Lesson 8.2: Trust Signal Completeness by Persona

### Persona-Specific Trust Signals

**Why Persona-Specific:**
- Different personas value different signals
- Trust signals must match persona needs
- Persona alignment increases recommendations
- Completeness varies by persona

### Measuring Completeness

**Trust Signal Components:**
1. **Evidence:** Supporting data and facts
2. **Reviews:** User feedback and ratings
3. **Authority:** Brand recognition and expertise
4. **Freshness:** Recent, up-to-date information

**Completeness Score:**
```
Completeness = (Evidence Score + Review Score + Authority Score + Freshness Score) / 4
```

### Persona-Specific Assessment

**For Each Persona:**
1. **Evidence Assessment:**
   - Do we have relevant evidence?
   - Is evidence persona-appropriate?
   - Is evidence comprehensive?
   - Evidence score (0-100)

2. **Review Assessment:**
   - Do we have reviews?
   - Are reviews persona-relevant?
   - Are reviews positive?
   - Review score (0-100)

3. **Authority Assessment:**
   - Do we have authority for this persona?
   - Is authority recognized?
   - Is authority credible?
   - Authority score (0-100)

4. **Freshness Assessment:**
   - Is data recent?
   - Is content up-to-date?
   - Is information current?
   - Freshness score (0-100)

### Completeness Targets

**Tier 1 Personas (High Value):**
- **Target Completeness:** 80%+
- Strategic importance
- High revenue potential
- Priority investment

**Tier 2 Personas (Medium Value):**
- **Target Completeness:** 60-80%
- Growth opportunities
- Moderate revenue
- Moderate investment

**Tier 3 Personas (Low Value):**
- **Target Completeness:** 40-60%
- Lower priority
- Limited revenue
- Minimal investment

### Gap Analysis

**Identifying Gaps:**
- Evidence gaps by persona
- Review gaps by persona
- Authority gaps by persona
- Freshness gaps by persona

**Prioritizing Gaps:**
- High-value personas first
- High-impact gaps first
- Quick wins first
- Strategic priorities

## Lesson 8.3: Speed-to-Launch Benchmarks

### What Is Speed-to-Launch?

**Definition:** The time from paid AI ad platform launch to your first live campaign.

**Components:**
1. **Preparation Time:** Time to prepare before launch
2. **Launch Time:** Time from launch to first campaign
3. **Optimization Time:** Time to optimize performance
4. **Total Time:** End-to-end timeline

### Benchmark Categories

**Category 1: First Movers (0-30 days)**
- Prepared before launch
- Ready on day one
- First campaigns live quickly
- **Advantage:** Early market position

**Category 2: Fast Followers (31-60 days)**
- Prepared during launch
- Quick to market
- Campaigns live within 2 months
- **Advantage:** Learning from early movers

**Category 3: Standard Entry (61-90 days)**
- Standard preparation
- Normal timeline
- Campaigns live within 3 months
- **Position:** Competitive but not early

**Category 4: Late Entry (91+ days)**
- Delayed preparation
- Slow to market
- Campaigns live after 3 months
- **Risk:** Competitive disadvantage

### Speed Factors

**Factors That Accelerate:**
1. **Pre-Launch Preparation:**
   - Intent mapping complete
   - Trust signals built
   - Creative ready
   - Strategy defined

2. **Team Readiness:**
   - Team trained
   - Processes defined
   - Tools ready
   - Agency prepared

3. **Stakeholder Alignment:**
   - Budget approved
   - Contracts ready
   - Legal cleared
   - Brand aligned

4. **Platform Relationships:**
   - Early access
   - Beta participation
   - Preferred status
   - Dedicated support

**Factors That Delay:**
1. **Lack of Preparation:**
   - Intent mapping incomplete
   - Trust signals weak
   - Creative missing
   - Strategy undefined

2. **Team Not Ready:**
   - Team untrained
   - Processes unclear
   - Tools missing
   - Agency unprepared

3. **Stakeholder Misalignment:**
   - Budget not approved
   - Contracts pending
   - Legal issues
   - Brand concerns

4. **No Platform Relationships:**
   - No early access
   - Standard entry
   - Limited support
   - Delayed onboarding

### Speed Targets

**Target: First Mover (0-30 days)**
- Complete preparation before launch
- Ready on day one
- First campaigns within 30 days
- **Requirement:** Full preparation

**Target: Fast Follower (31-60 days)**
- Most preparation complete
- Quick to market
- First campaigns within 60 days
- **Requirement:** Core preparation

**Target: Standard Entry (61-90 days)**
- Standard preparation
- Normal timeline
- First campaigns within 90 days
- **Requirement:** Basic preparation

### Speed Optimization

**Acceleration Strategies:**
1. **Start Early:**
   - Begin preparation now
   - Don't wait for launch
   - Build readiness
   - Create advantage

2. **Prioritize:**
   - Focus on high-value intents
   - Prioritize core personas
   - Quick wins first
   - Strategic focus

3. **Parallel Work:**
   - Work on multiple areas simultaneously
   - Don't wait for sequential completion
   - Maximize efficiency
   - Accelerate timeline

4. **Leverage Resources:**
   - Use external expertise
   - Partner with agencies
   - Platform relationships
   - Accelerate learning

## Lesson 8.4: Share of Organic AI Recommendations as Pre-Paid Proxy

### Why Organic Share Is a Proxy

**The Hypothesis:**
Organic AI recommendation share predicts paid AI ad performance because:
- Same algorithm foundation
- Trust signal correlation
- Intent coverage alignment
- Competitive position indicator

### Using Organic Share as Proxy

**Pre-Paid Metrics:**
1. **Recommendation Share:**
   - Current organic share
   - Share by intent
   - Share by persona
   - Share trends

2. **Citation Frequency:**
   - How often cited
   - Citation position
   - Citation context
   - Citation quality

3. **Competitive Position:**
   - Share vs. competitors
   - Market position
   - Competitive strength
   - Displacement risk

4. **Trust Signal Strength:**
   - Evidence quality
   - Review strength
   - Authority level
   - Data freshness

### Proxy Metrics Dashboard

**Key Metrics:**
1. **Overall Share:**
   - Total recommendation share
   - Share trend
   - Target share
   - Gap to target

2. **Intent Share:**
   - Share by intent cluster
   - High-value intent share
   - Share gaps
   - Growth opportunities

3. **Persona Share:**
   - Share by persona
   - High-value persona share
   - Persona gaps
   - Persona opportunities

4. **Competitive Share:**
   - Share vs. competitors
   - Market position
   - Competitive gaps
   - Displacement risks

### Proxy to Paid Translation

**Translation Framework:**
- High organic share (20%+) = High paid potential
- Medium organic share (10-20%) = Medium paid potential
- Low organic share (<10%) = Low paid potential

**Strategic Implications:**
- High share = Defend with competitive bidding
- Medium share = Grow with balanced strategy
- Low share = Build with quality investment
