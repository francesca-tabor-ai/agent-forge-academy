## Lesson 7.1: Working with Paid Media Teams

### The New Skill Set

**Traditional Paid Media Skills:**
- Keyword research and optimization
- Bid management
- Ad creative development
- Landing page optimization
- Performance tracking

**AI-Native Paid Media Skills:**
- Intent cluster mapping
- Persona-weighted bidding
- Answer-level strategy
- Trust signal optimization
- AI recommendation tracking

### Skill Gap Analysis

**Identify Gaps:**
1. **Intent Understanding:**
   - Traditional: Keyword-focused
   - AI-Native: Intent-focused
   - **Gap:** Understanding intent clusters vs. keywords

2. **Bidding Strategy:**
   - Traditional: Keyword-level bidding
   - AI-Native: Answer-level bidding
   - **Gap:** Understanding answer slots vs. keywords

3. **Creative Development:**
   - Traditional: Ad copy, images, CTAs
   - AI-Native: Conversational responses, trust signals
   - **Gap:** Understanding AI-native creative formats

4. **Measurement:**
   - Traditional: CTR, CPC, conversion rate
   - AI-Native: Recommendation share, trust scores
   - **Gap:** Understanding new metrics

### Training Plan

**Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)**
- AI recommendation basics
- Intent cluster mapping
- Answer-level bidding concepts
- Trust signal understanding

**Phase 2: Application (Weeks 3-4)**
- Hands-on intent mapping
- Bidding strategy development
- Creative format creation
- Measurement setup

**Phase 3: Optimization (Weeks 5-6)**
- Performance analysis
- Strategy refinement
- Continuous improvement
- Best practices development

### Team Structure

**Option 1: Integrated Team**
- Paid media team learns AI-native skills
- Same team handles both traditional and AI-native
- **Pros:** Efficiency, consistency
- **Cons:** Learning curve, capacity

**Option 2: Dedicated Team**
- Separate team for AI-native media
- Specialized expertise
- **Pros:** Expertise, focus
- **Cons:** Duplication, coordination

**Option 3: Hybrid Approach**
- Core team with AI-native expertise
- Support from traditional team
- **Pros:** Balance, flexibility
- **Cons:** Coordination complexity

### Communication Framework

**Regular Updates:**
- Weekly team meetings
- Performance reviews
- Strategy discussions
- Learning sessions

**Documentation:**
- Strategy documents
- Process guides
- Best practices
- Lessons learned

**Knowledge Sharing:**
- Internal training
- Case studies
- Peer learning
- External resources

## Lesson 7.2: Educating Agencies on AI-Native Buying

### Agency Education Needs

**What Agencies Need to Know:**
1. **AI-Native Fundamentals:**
   - How AI recommendations work
   - Intent clusters vs. keywords
   - Answer-level bidding
   - Trust signal importance

2. **Strategic Differences:**
   - Different auction model
   - Different creative requirements
   - Different measurement
   - Different optimization

3. **Practical Implementation:**
   - How to map intents
   - How to develop creative
   - How to bid effectively
   - How to measure success

### Education Program

**Phase 1: Awareness (Month 1)**
- AI-native media overview
- Key differences from traditional
- Market opportunity
- Strategic importance

**Phase 2: Understanding (Month 2)**
- Deep dive into concepts
- Case studies
- Best practices
- Industry trends

**Phase 3: Application (Month 3)**
- Hands-on training
- Strategy development
- Creative development
- Measurement setup

**Phase 4: Mastery (Month 4+)**
- Advanced strategies
- Optimization techniques
- Continuous learning
- Innovation

### Agency Certification

**Certification Levels:**
1. **Awareness Level:**
   - Understand basics
   - Know key differences
   - Can discuss strategy
   - **Requirement:** Complete awareness training

2. **Practitioner Level:**
   - Can execute campaigns
   - Understand optimization
   - Can measure performance
   - **Requirement:** Complete training + pass exam

3. **Expert Level:**
   - Can develop strategies
   - Can train others
   - Can innovate
   - **Requirement:** Practitioner + experience + certification

### Agency Partnership Model

**Traditional Agency Model:**
- Agency manages campaigns
- Client provides direction
- Agency executes
- Client reviews

**AI-Native Partnership Model:**
- Collaborative strategy development
- Shared expertise
- Joint optimization
- Continuous learning

**Key Differences:**
- More collaboration
- Shared learning
- Joint ownership
- Continuous improvement

### Agency Selection Criteria

**What to Look For:**
1. **AI-Native Expertise:**
   - Understanding of AI recommendations
   - Experience with intent mapping
   - Knowledge of trust signals
   - Strategic thinking

2. **Learning Agility:**
   - Willingness to learn
   - Adaptability
   - Innovation mindset
   - Continuous improvement

3. **Collaboration:**
   - Partnership approach
   - Transparent communication
   - Shared ownership
   - Joint problem-solving

4. **Results Focus:**
   - Performance-oriented
   - Data-driven
   - Optimization-focused
   - ROI-minded

## Practical Exercise 7: Paid AI Ads Readiness Playbook & Internal Education Deck

### Objective
Create a comprehensive readiness playbook and internal education deck for executives and agencies.

### Steps

#### Step 1: Operating Model Design (120 minutes)
1. **Team Structure:**
   - Design team structure
   - Define roles and responsibilities
   - Map workflows
   - Document processes

2. **Agency Partnership:**
   - Define agency model
   - Create education program
   - Establish certification
   - Document partnership

3. **Process Design:**
   - Campaign launch process
   - Optimization process
   - Reporting process
   - Review process

#### Step 2: Procurement Readiness (90 minutes)
1. **Contract Preparation:**
   - Platform agreements
   - Agency contracts
   - Service agreements
   - Terms and conditions

2. **Budget Process:**
   - Budget approval process
   - Payment terms
   - Budget tracking
   - Financial controls

3. **Platform Relationships:**
   - Early access agreements
   - Partnership opportunities
   - Support arrangements
   - Platform contracts

#### Step 3: Stakeholder Alignment (90 minutes)
1. **Brand Alignment:**
   - Brand voice guidelines
   - Messaging standards
   - Creative guidelines
   - Brand protection

2. **Legal Alignment:**
   - Compliance requirements
   - Risk management
   - Terms and conditions
   - Legal review process

3. **Finance Alignment:**
   - Budget planning
   - ROI expectations
   - Financial controls
   - Reporting requirements

#### Step 4: Playbook Creation (120 minutes)
Create comprehensive playbook:

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Overview
   - Key readiness areas
   - Action items
   - Timeline

2. **Operating Model** (3-4 pages)
   - Team structure
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Workflows
   - Processes

3. **Agency Partnership** (2-3 pages)
   - Agency model
   - Education program
   - Certification
   - Partnership framework

4. **Procurement Readiness** (2-3 pages)
   - Contract preparation
   - Budget process
   - Platform relationships
   - Approval processes

5. **Stakeholder Alignment** (2-3 pages)
   - Brand alignment
   - Legal alignment
   - Finance alignment
   - Communication framework

6. **Action Plan** (1-2 pages)
   - Immediate actions
   - Short-term actions
   - Medium-term actions
   - Long-term actions

#### Step 5: Education Deck Creation (90 minutes)
Create internal education deck:

1. **Overview** (5-10 slides)
   - What are paid AI ads?
   - Why they matter
   - Market opportunity
   - Strategic importance

2. **How They Work** (10-15 slides)
   - AI recommendations
   - Intent clusters
   - Answer-level bidding
   - Trust signals

3. **Our Strategy** (10-15 slides)
   - Intent priorities
   - Bidding strategy
   - Creative approach
   - Measurement

4. **Readiness** (5-10 slides)
   - Current status
   - Gaps identified
   - Action plan
   - Timeline

5. **Next Steps** (3-5 slides)
   - Immediate actions
   - Key milestones
   - Success metrics
   - Questions

### Deliverables
1. **Paid AI Ads Readiness Playbook** (15-20 pages)
   - Operating model
   - Agency partnership
   - Procurement readiness
   - Stakeholder alignment
   - Action plan

2. **Internal Education Deck** (30-50 slides)
   - Executive overview
   - How paid AI ads work
   - Our strategy
   - Readiness status
   - Next steps

3. **Supporting Materials:**
   - Process documents
   - Contract templates
   - Budget templates
   - Communication templates

### Evaluation Criteria
- **Completeness:** All readiness areas addressed
- **Clarity:** Clear, actionable guidance
- **Alignment:** Stakeholder expectations aligned
- **Actionability:** Specific, implementable actions
- **Presentation:** Professional, executive-ready format
