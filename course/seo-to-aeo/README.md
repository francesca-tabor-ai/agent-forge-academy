## Key Concepts

### The Transition Analogy

Traditional SEO was like trying to be the most popular book on a shelf; you just needed a catchy cover to get a person to pick you up (the click). Modern AEO/GEO/AIVO is like being a verified source in an encyclopedia; you don't need to be picked up by the human, you need to be so accurate and authoritative that the author (the AI) chooses to quote you in the final answer that everyone reads.

### Core Principles

1. **Citation Over Clicks**: Optimize for being cited in AI responses, not just ranking high
2. **Entity-Level Authority**: Build authority at the brand/entity level, not just page level
3. **Cross-Model Persistence**: Ensure visibility across all AI models, not just one
4. **Ground Truth**: Provide explicit, machine-readable facts to prevent hallucinations
5. **Continuous Monitoring**: Track visibility decay and maintain optimization efforts

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can businesses start tracking visibility in AI engine responses?

Start by testing a set of relevant queries across major AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Document which queries result in citations or mentions of your brand. Track this over time to measure your AI Visibility Score. See Module 6, Lesson 6.1 for detailed implementation.

### What metrics distinguish high-quality AI citations from basic mentions?

High-quality citations include:
- Accurate information about your brand/products
- Proper attribution and context
- Complete representation of your value proposition
- Positive or neutral sentiment
- Prominent placement in AI responses

Basic mentions may be:
- Incorrect or outdated information
- Minimal context
- Negative sentiment
- Buried in long responses

See Module 6, Lesson 6.1 for the Citation Quality Score framework.

### How do AI crawlers differ from traditional Google search bots?

AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, etc.):
- Mine data for training and inference, not just indexing
- May crawl less frequently than Googlebot
- Don't send direct referral traffic (zero-click outcomes)
- Prioritize structured data and entity information
- Require explicit permission via robots.txt and llms.txt

Traditional search bots (Googlebot):
- Index content for search results
- Crawl frequently for fresh content
- Send direct referral traffic through clicks
- Focus on page-level optimization
- Controlled via robots.txt

See Module 1, Lesson 1.3 for detailed comparison.
