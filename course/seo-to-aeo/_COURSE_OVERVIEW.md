# The AI Visibility Roadmap: From Legacy SEO to Agentic Commerce

## Course Objective

To equip digital marketers and business leaders with the strategic and technical frameworks required to navigate the transition from traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), culminating in the AI Visibility Optimization (AIVO) standard.

## Course Description

The digital marketing landscape is undergoing a fundamental transformation. Traditional SEO, built on the premise of "ten blue links" and click-through rates, is collapsing as zero-click outcomes are projected to exceed 70% by 2025. Meanwhile, AI-powered answer engines and generative models are reshaping how consumers discover, evaluate, and purchase products.

This course provides a comprehensive roadmap for businesses to transition from legacy SEO tactics to the new era of AI-driven visibility. You'll learn how to optimize for AI crawlers, structure content for answer engines, and build the infrastructure necessary for agentic commerce—where autonomous AI agents act on behalf of consumers.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understand why traditional search is failing and how consumer behavior is shifting toward conversational discovery
- Master technical SEO fundamentals that serve as the foundation for AI visibility
- Implement Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) strategies for Google AI Overviews and Perplexity
- Apply Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) techniques to increase citation density and model inclusion
- Prepare for the AIVO standard and agentic commerce infrastructure
- Track and measure AI visibility using new KPIs beyond traditional traffic metrics

## The Transition Analogy

Traditional SEO was like trying to be the most popular book on a shelf; you just needed a catchy cover to get a person to pick you up (the click). Modern AEO/GEO/AIVO is like being a verified source in an encyclopedia; you don't need to be picked up by the human, you need to be so accurate and authoritative that the author (the AI) chooses to quote you in the final answer that everyone reads.

## Course Structure

This course is divided into 6 modules, each building upon the previous:

1. **The Collapse of the Referral Economy** - Understanding why traditional search is failing
2. **Technical SEO as the AI Bedrock** - Building the foundation for AI visibility
3. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** - Optimizing for direct answers
4. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** - Increasing citation density and model inclusion
5. **The Future: AIVO & Agentic Commerce** - Preparing for autonomous agents
6. **Monitoring the New Scoreboard** - Tracking AI visibility and attribution
