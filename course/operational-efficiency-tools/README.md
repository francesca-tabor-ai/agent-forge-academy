## Course Modules

### [Module 1: Why Operations Are the Hidden Bottleneck](Module_01_Why_Operations_Are_the_Hidden_Bottleneck.md)
- Understand why operational overload limits growth and innovation
- The explosion of fund and share-class complexity
- Manual reporting and dependency on data teams
- Product onboarding as a structural choke point
- **Exercise 1:** Identify top operational pain points across data access and PDD workflows

### [Module 2: Natural Language as the New User Interface](Module_02_Natural_Language_as_the_New_User_Interface.md)
- Remove technical barriers to data access
- Why SQL and BI tools don't scale to non-technical users
- Natural Language Interfaces (NLI) explained
- Context-aware querying across massive datasets
- **Exercise 2:** Convert common operational questions into natural-language queries

### [Module 3: Querying Massive Fund Universes Safely](Module_03_Querying_Massive_Fund_Universes_Safely.md)
- Enable instant insight across 100,000+ funds and 300,000+ share classes
- Semantic understanding of financial terminology
- Handling ambiguity and follow-up clarification
- Aggregation, filtering, and comparison logic
- **Exercise 3:** Design a safe-response framework for ambiguous user queries

### [Module 4: On-Demand Reporting & Insight Generation](Module_04_On_Demand_Reporting_and_Insight_Generation.md)
- Replace static reports with conversational intelligence
- Generating summaries, comparisons, and trend views
- Role-based reporting (ops, product, compliance)
- Confidence indicators and source traceability
- **Exercise 4:** Define a set of "daily operational questions" solvable via NLI

### [Module 5: AI-Built Product Due Diligence (PDD)](Module_05_AI_Built_Product_Due_Diligence_PDD.md)
- Automate red-flag detection during product onboarding
- The traditional PDD burden and review fatigue
- Identifying complex derivatives and structured exposures
- Virtual assets, leverage, and embedded optionality
- **Exercise 5:** Create a red-flag taxonomy for product due diligence

### [Module 6: From Red Flags to Review Decisions](Module_06_From_Red_Flags_to_Review_Decisions.md)
- Streamline governance without lowering standards
- Risk scoring and materiality thresholds
- Exception-based escalation to committees
- Reducing false positives without missing risks
- **Exercise 6:** Design an AI-assisted PDD workflow with human checkpoints

### [Module 7: Explainability, Accountability & Trust](Module_07_Explainability_Accountability_and_Trust.md)
- Ensure operational AI is defensible and auditable
- "Why did the AI flag this?"
- Source attribution and evidence trails
- Managing overrides and disagreements
- **Exercise 7:** Draft an explainable AI summary for a flagged product risk

### [Module 8: Building the Operational Intelligence Layer](Module_08_Building_the_Operational_Intelligence_Layer.md)
- Move from point tools to an operational platform
- Integrating NLI and AI-PDD into core systems
- Measuring impact: time saved, errors reduced
- Scaling across teams and jurisdictions
- **Capstone Project:** Design an Operational Efficiency Toolset combining Natural Language Data Access and AI-Built PDD
