# Mastering the NexusIQ Commerce Ecosystem

##  Course Overview

**Duration:** 7 weeks  
**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** E-commerce knowledge, basic AI/ML concepts, API familiarity

---

##  What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

 Understand the conversational commerce revolution and market dynamics  
 Design and implement unified knowledge graphs from fragmented data sources  
 Build RAG-based conversational AI that maintains brand identity  
 Deploy omnichannel distribution systems with cross-channel consistency  
 Implement governance and compliance workflows for regulated industries  
 Measure and optimize CCIS performance with actionable analytics  
 Prepare organizations for future commerce technologies

##  Course Modules

### [Module 1: Foundations of CCIS & The Conversational Revolution](Module_01_Foundations_of_CCIS_and_The_Conversational_Revolution.md)
- The rise of conversational commerce (71% of shoppers expect real-time communication)
- Solving the "Data Silo" problem (PIM, DAM, CMS fragmentation)
- Introduction to NexusIQ as unified intelligence solution
- **Lab 1:** Analyze data silo problems in your organization

### [Module 2: The Intelligence Engine & Knowledge Engineering](Module_02_The_Intelligence_Engine_and_Knowledge_Engineering.md)
- Building unified knowledge graphs from multiple data sources
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture
- Product & allergen ontology development
- **Lab 2:** Build a knowledge graph from sample PIM/DAM data

### [Module 3: Conversational Capabilities & Brand Persona](Module_03_Conversational_Capabilities_and_Brand_Persona.md)
- Dynamic persona engine for brand-appropriate tone
- Multi-turn context management and intent recognition
- Multimodal interactions (text, voice, rich media)
- **Lab 3:** Configure persona engine for different brand identities

### [Module 4: Omnichannel Distribution & Consistency](Module_04_Omnichannel_Distribution_and_Consistency.md)
- Cross-channel synchronization (Amazon, WhatsApp, Web)
- Automated content updates and FAQ generation
- Real-time listing health monitoring
- **Lab 4:** Implement cross-channel content synchronization

### [Module 5: Governance, Compliance, & Risk Management](Module_05_Governance_Compliance_and_Risk_Management.md)
- Regulatory guardrails (FDA, FALCPA compliance)
- Human-in-the-loop workflows for sensitive content
- Privacy & security (GDPR/CCPA standards)
- **Lab 5:** Build compliance validation workflow

### [Module 6: Analytics, Insights, & Business Impact](Module_06_Analytics_Insights_and_Business_Impact.md)
- Measuring KPIs (Deflection Rate, Response Accuracy, Freshness Score)
- Revenue attribution and intent heatmaps
- Closing the intelligence loop (conversations → product innovation)
- **Lab 6:** Build analytics dashboard and revenue attribution model

### [Module 7: The Roadmap to Future-Proofing](Module_07_The_Roadmap_to_Future_Proofing.md)
- Building CCIS-ready organizations (team structure, roles)
- Immersive & voice commerce integration
- Predictive intelligence and autonomous agents
- **Lab 7:** Design future-ready CCIS architecture
