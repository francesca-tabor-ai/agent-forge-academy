# Module 1: Foundations of Financial Fraud

**Duration:** Week 1  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand types of financial fraud
- Learn fraud detection challenges
- Compare traditional vs. AI approaches
- Build a basic fraud classifier

---

## Lesson 1.1: Types of Financial Fraud

### Payment Fraud
- Card-not-present (CNP) fraud
- Card-present fraud
- Account takeover
- Payment fraud schemes

### Identity Fraud
- Identity theft
- Synthetic identity fraud
- Document forgery
- Biometric spoofing

### Money Laundering
- Structuring (smurfing)
- Trade-based laundering
- Cryptocurrency laundering
- Shell companies

---

## Lesson 1.2: Fraud Detection Challenges

### Data Imbalance
- Rare fraud events
- Class imbalance problems
- Cost-sensitive learning

### Evolving Fraud Patterns
- Adversarial attacks
- Concept drift
- Model adaptation

### Real-Time Requirements
- Low latency (<100ms)
- High throughput
- Scalability

---

## Lesson 1.3: Traditional vs. AI Approaches

### Traditional Methods
- Rule-based systems
- Statistical models
- Manual review
- Limitations: Static, high false positives

### AI-Enhanced Methods
- Machine learning classifiers
- Deep learning for patterns
- Anomaly detection
- Network analysis
