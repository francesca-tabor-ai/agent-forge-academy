# Insight OS: From Raw Data to Executive Decisions

##  Course Overview

**Duration:** 6–8 weeks (can be delivered as live, cohort-based, or self-paced)  
**Level:** Intermediate → Advanced  
**Time Commitment:** 6-8 hours per week  
**Prerequisites:** Basic SQL knowledge, familiarity with data analysis concepts, business acumen

---

##  What You'll Master

By completing this course, you will be able to:

✅ Define **meaningful metrics** aligned with business decisions  
✅ Build **reliable analytical datasets** that leadership can trust  
✅ Diagnose **root causes**, not just symptoms  
✅ Communicate insights that **change decisions**  
✅ Design a **scalable Insight Operating System** for your organization

---

## 🎯 Course Objective

This course teaches how to build an **Insight Operating System** — the analytical foundation that transforms raw data into **trusted, decision-ready insights** used by product leaders, operators, and executives.

Students will learn how to answer the critical question:

> **"What's happening — and why?"**

for real business problems.

---

##  Course Modules

### [Module 1: Business Context & Metric Thinking](Module_01_Business_Context_and_Metric_Thinking.md)
- **Theme:** *What should we even measure?*
- How businesses actually make decisions
- North Star metrics vs operational metrics
- Leading vs lagging indicators
- KPI trees & metric hierarchies
- Metric anti-patterns (vanity, proxy, noisy metrics)
- **Hands-on:** Build KPI trees for subscription business, marketplace, and e-commerce funnel
- **Output:** A decision-aligned KPI framework

### [Module 2: SQL for Analytics & Performance Tracking](Module_02_SQL_for_Analytics_and_Performance_Tracking.md)
- **Theme:** *Turning data into answers*
- Analytical SQL mindset (not transactional SQL)
- Time-based analysis & cohorts
- Funnel analysis
- Window functions for trends & rankings
- Common SQL pitfalls in analytics
- **Hands-on:** Write SQL for funnel drop-offs, retention cohorts, week-over-week performance
- **Output:** Production-grade SQL queries that leadership can trust

### [Module 3: Analytical Modeling & Metrics Layers](Module_03_Analytical_Modeling_and_Metrics_Layers.md)
- **Theme:** *Making insights repeatable*
- Raw vs transformed data
- Metrics layers & semantic models
- Dimensions, facts, and grain
- Single source of truth design
- Working with Analytics Engineering concepts
- **Hands-on:** Design metrics models for orders, revenue, customer activity
- **Output:** A scalable analytics model that powers dashboards & ad-hoc analysis

### [Module 4: Dashboarding That Drives Action](Module_04_Dashboarding_That_Drives_Action.md)
- **Theme:** *Dashboards are products*
- Choosing the right visualization
- Designing dashboards for different audiences (Operators, Product Managers, Executives)
- Signal vs noise
- Dashboard failure patterns
- **Hands-on:** Build operational and executive summary dashboards
- **Output:** Dashboards that answer questions, not just display charts

### [Module 5: Root-Cause Analysis & Diagnostics](Module_05_Root_Cause_Analysis_and_Diagnostics.md)
- **Theme:** *Why did this happen?*
- Diagnostic analytics vs descriptive analytics
- Drill-down strategies
- Segmentation & slicing
- Correlation vs causation (at analyst level)
- Writing analytical narratives
- **Hands-on:** Investigate simulated business issues (conversion drop, revenue decline, retention anomaly)
- **Output:** A clear root-cause narrative with evidence and confidence levels

### [Module 6: Storytelling & Executive Communication](Module_06_Storytelling_and_Executive_Communication.md)
- **Theme:** *Insights only matter if people believe them*
- Insight framing for executives
- Structuring a data story
- Writing insight summaries & recommendations
- Handling pushback & ambiguity
- Decision memos vs dashboards
- **Hands-on:** Present insights to mock leadership panel, convert analysis into one-page executive brief
- **Output:** Executive-ready communication that leads to action

### [Module 7: Self-Serve Analytics & Operating Models](Module_07_Self_Serve_Analytics_and_Operating_Models.md)
- **Theme:** *Scaling insight without becoming a bottleneck*
- What self-serve analytics really means
- Guardrails vs freedom
- Metrics ownership & governance
- Analyst as enabler, not report factory
- Working with Product, Data Science & Engineering
- **Hands-on:** Design self-serve analytics strategy, define ownership and SLAs
- **Output:** A scalable Insight OS design, not just individual analyses

### [Capstone Project: Build an Insight OS](Module_08_Capstone_Project_Build_an_Insight_OS.md)
- **Theme:** *End-to-end real-world simulation*
- Define KPIs
- Write analytical SQL
- Build dashboards
- Diagnose a business issue
- Deliver executive recommendations
- **Final Deliverables:** KPI tree, SQL analysis, Dashboard, Root-cause report, Executive summary
- **Graduation Requirement:** Defend insights in a leadership-style review
