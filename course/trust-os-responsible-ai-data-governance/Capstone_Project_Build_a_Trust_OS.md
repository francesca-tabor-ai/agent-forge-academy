# Capstone Project: Build a Trust OS

**Duration:** Weeks 7-8  
**Theme:** *End-to-end responsible AI system design*

**Project Objective:** Design and defend a complete Trust Operating System for a real-world AI use case.

---

## Project Overview

### What You'll Build

You will design a comprehensive Trust OS that includes:

1. **AI Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan**
2. **Bias and Explainability Documentation**
3. **Privacy and Compliance Artifacts**
4. **Model Governance and Approval Workflow**
5. **Governance Operating Model**

### Learning Outcomes

By completing this project, you will demonstrate your ability to:

- Identify and assess risks across the AI lifecycle
- Design bias mitigation and explainability approaches
- Operationalize data protection and privacy requirements
- Create model governance and approval processes
- Design organizational governance structures
- Integrate trust into operational workflows
- Communicate and defend governance decisions

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### Comprehensive Assessment (40%)

**Criteria:**
- All components addressed
- Appropriate depth and detail
- Integration across components
- Real-world applicability

**Scoring:**
- Excellent (36-40): Comprehensive, well-integrated, highly applicable
- Good (32-35): Complete, mostly integrated, applicable
- Satisfactory (28-31): Mostly complete, some integration, somewhat applicable
- Needs Improvement (<28): Incomplete, limited integration, limited applicability

### Technical Quality (30%)

**Criteria:**
- Accuracy of assessments
- Appropriateness of approaches
- Quality of analysis
- Technical correctness

**Scoring:**
- Excellent (27-30): Highly accurate, excellent approaches, strong analysis
- Good (24-26): Accurate, good approaches, solid analysis
- Satisfactory (21-23): Mostly accurate, adequate approaches, basic analysis
- Needs Improvement (<21): Inaccuracies, poor approaches, weak analysis

### Practical Applicability (20%)

**Criteria:**
- Practicality of solutions
- Feasibility of implementation
- Resource considerations
- Real-world constraints

**Scoring:**
- Excellent (18-20): Highly practical, very feasible, well-considered
- Good (16-17): Practical, feasible, considered
- Satisfactory (14-15): Somewhat practical, mostly feasible, basic consideration
- Needs Improvement (<14): Impractical, infeasible, not considered

### Communication and Presentation (10%)

**Criteria:**
- Clarity of communication
- Organization and structure
- Visual aids and formatting
- Professional presentation

**Scoring:**
- Excellent (9-10): Very clear, well-organized, excellent visuals
- Good (8): Clear, organized, good visuals
- Satisfactory (7): Mostly clear, adequate organization, basic visuals
- Needs Improvement (<7): Unclear, disorganized, poor visuals

## Defense Requirements

### Presentation Format

**Duration:** 30 minutes presentation + 15 minutes Q&A

**Structure:**
1. **Introduction (2 minutes)**
   - Use case overview
   - Trust OS objectives

2. **Risk Assessment (5 minutes)**
   - Key risks identified
   - Risk prioritization
   - Mitigation strategies

3. **Bias and Explainability (5 minutes)**
   - Fairness approach
   - Explainability strategy
   - Bias mitigation

4. **Privacy and Compliance (5 minutes)**
   - Privacy assessment
   - Compliance approach
   - Rights operationalization

5. **Model Governance (5 minutes)**
   - Approval workflow
   - Audit framework
   - Monitoring approach

6. **Organizational Governance (5 minutes)**
   - Governance model
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Enforcement mechanisms

7. **Integration and Implementation (3 minutes)**
   - System integration
   - Implementation plan
   - Success metrics

### Defense Panel

**Panel Composition:**
- Senior governance leader
- Technical expert
- Business stakeholder
- Legal/compliance expert

**Panel Role:**
- Ask challenging questions
- Assess depth of understanding
- Evaluate practical applicability
- Provide feedback

### Defense Questions (Sample)

**Risk Management:**
- How did you prioritize risks?
- What trade-offs did you consider?
- How would you handle a risk that materializes?

**Bias and Fairness:**
- Why did you choose these fairness definitions?
- How would you handle conflicting fairness requirements?
- What if bias is detected post-deployment?

**Privacy and Compliance:**
- How would you handle a data subject rights request?
- What if lawful basis changes?
- How do you ensure ongoing compliance?

**Model Governance:**
- How do you balance speed and thoroughness in approval?
- What if a model needs urgent deployment?
- How do you handle third-party models?

**Organizational Governance:**
- How do you balance autonomy and oversight?
- What if teams resist governance?
- How do you scale governance?

**Integration:**
- How do you integrate governance without slowing innovation?
- What if governance requirements conflict with business needs?
- How do you measure success?
