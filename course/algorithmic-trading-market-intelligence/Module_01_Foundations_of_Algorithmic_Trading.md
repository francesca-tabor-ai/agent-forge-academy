# Module 1: Foundations of Algorithmic Trading

**Duration:** Week 1  
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand market microstructure and order types
- Learn execution strategies and routing algorithms
- Master backtesting frameworks and performance metrics
- Build a basic backtesting system

---

## Lesson 1.1: Market Microstructure

### Order Types
- Market orders
- Limit orders
- Stop orders
- Iceberg orders
- TWAP/VWAP orders

### Market Participants
- Market makers
- High-frequency traders
- Institutional investors
- Retail traders

### Execution Venues
- Exchanges
- Dark pools
- ECNs (Electronic Communication Networks)
- ATS (Alternative Trading Systems)

---

## Lesson 1.2: Execution Strategies

### Implementation Shortfall
- Minimize execution cost
- Balance market impact vs. timing risk
- Optimal execution algorithms

### Order Routing
- Smart order routing (SOR)
- Liquidity aggregation
- Best execution requirements

---

## Lesson 1.3: Backtesting Frameworks

### Components
- Historical data management
- Signal generation
- Portfolio construction
- Execution simulation
- Performance analysis

### Common Pitfalls
- Look-ahead bias
- Survivorship bias
- Overfitting
- Transaction costs

Create a backtesting framework with data loading, signal generation, and performance metrics.

- Backtesting engine
