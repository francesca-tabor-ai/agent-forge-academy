# Financial Mathematics & Risk Modeling

## Course Overview

Design and implement sophisticated financial models for pricing, risk management, and portfolio optimization using mathematical and AI techniques.

## Learning Outcomes

- Price derivatives using mathematical models
- Calculate risk metrics (VaR, CVaR)
- Optimize portfolios
- Model stochastic processes
- Implement Monte Carlo simulations
- Build regulatory compliance frameworks

## Prerequisites

- Strong mathematical background
- Understanding of finance
- Programming experience (Python preferred)

## Course Structure

This course is organized into modules covering:
- Derivative Pricing Models
- Risk Metrics and Measurement
- Portfolio Optimization
- Stochastic Processes
- Monte Carlo Methods
- Regulatory Compliance
