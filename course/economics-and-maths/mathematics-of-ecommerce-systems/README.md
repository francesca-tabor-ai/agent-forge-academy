# Mathematics of E-Commerce Systems: From Demand to Delivery

## Course Overview

This course teaches how to understand, model, and optimize e-commerce systems using mathematical tools across data, economics, logistics, and decision science.

## Level

Advanced undergraduate / graduate / professional

## Prerequisites

- Calculus
- Probability & statistics
- Linear algebra (recommended)
- Basic economics

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

- Model customer behavior using probability and statistics
- Design and analyze experiments for e-commerce
- Forecast demand under uncertainty
- Optimize pricing using economic principles
- Calculate customer lifetime value and model retention
- Manage inventory under uncertainty
- Optimize logistics and fulfillment
- Detect fraud using probabilistic methods
- Build personalization and recommendation systems
- Model platform reliability and scaling
- Integrate all components into end-to-end system

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Mathematical Foundations of Digital Commerce
**Objective:** Establish the core quantitative language of e-commerce systems.

**Key Topics:** Discrete vs continuous modeling, random variables in customer behavior, expectations/variance/distributions, scaling laws

**Core Mathematics:** Probability theory, linear & nonlinear functions, logarithmic growth

### Module 2: Customer Funnels & Behavioral Probability
**Objective:** Model how users move through digital funnels using probability and statistics.

**Key Topics:** Funnel decomposition, conditional probability, Markov chains, behavioral noise and bias

**Core Mathematics:** Bayes' theorem, Bernoulli & Binomial models, Markov transition matrices

### Module 3: Experimentation, Causality & Inference
**Objective:** Quantify what causes performance changes in e-commerce.

**Key Topics:** A/B testing, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, causal inference vs correlation

**Core Mathematics:** z-tests, t-tests, bootstrap methods, Bayesian updating, potential outcomes framework

### Module 4: Demand Modeling & Forecasting
**Objective:** Predict future demand under uncertainty.

**Key Topics:** Time series behavior, seasonality and trends, demand distributions, forecast error propagation

**Core Mathematics:** ARIMA-style models, autocorrelation functions, error metrics (MAPE, RMSE)

### Module 5: Pricing, Elasticity & Revenue Optimization
**Objective:** Optimize prices using economic and mathematical principles.

**Key Topics:** Price elasticity, revenue vs profit maximization, constraints and fairness, dynamic pricing logic

**Core Mathematics:** Differential calculus, optimization under constraints, elasticity estimation

### Module 6: Customer Lifetime Value & Retention Models
**Objective:** Quantify long-term customer value and churn risk.

**Key Topics:** Lifetime value decomposition, discounted cash flows, churn modeling, survival analysis

**Core Mathematics:** Geometric series, hazard functions, survival distributions

### Module 7: Inventory Theory & Supply Uncertainty
**Objective:** Manage stock mathematically under uncertain demand.

**Key Topics:** Inventory trade-offs, stockout vs overstock costs, service levels, risk pooling

**Core Mathematics:** Newsvendor model, normal approximations, quantiles & safety stock formulas

### Module 8: Logistics, Geometry & Network Optimization
**Objective:** Minimize fulfillment and delivery costs using geometric and network models.

**Key Topics:** Warehouse geometry, routing problems, queuing theory, capacity planning

**Core Mathematics:** Graph theory, Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP), queueing models (M/M/1)

### Module 9: Fraud, Risk & Decision Thresholds
**Objective:** Control risk using probabilistic inference.

**Key Topics:** Fraud as a classification problem, expected loss minimization, decision theory

**Core Mathematics:** Bayesian inference, expected value, cost-sensitive decision rules

### Module 10: Personalization, AI & Learning Systems
**Objective:** Model adaptive systems that learn from user behavior.

**Key Topics:** Recommender systems, ranking models, exploration vs exploitation, feedback loops

**Core Mathematics:** Linear algebra (matrix factorization), logistic regression, multi-armed bandits

### Module 11: Platform Stability, Reliability & Scaling
**Objective:** Quantify system robustness and performance under load.

**Key Topics:** Traffic spikes, failure rates, latency modeling, revenue impact of downtime

**Core Mathematics:** Poisson processes, reliability theory, queueing stability conditions

### Capstone Project: End-to-End E-Commerce System Model
**Objective:** Integrate all modules into a coherent mathematical system.

**Options:**
- Full-stack revenue optimization model
- Inventory + pricing + demand integration
- Personalization-driven CLV optimization
