# Mathematical Foundations of Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG)
*From Demand Signals to Scalable Operations*

## Course Overview

This course reveals how mathematics underpins decision-making in CPG across pricing, demand forecasting, manufacturing, packaging, logistics, marketing, and sustainability. It blends applied math, economics, statistics, physics, and data science with real-world CPG use cases.

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

- Map CPG problems to mathematical structures
- Model demand and consumer behavior quantitatively
- Build and evaluate demand forecasts
- Optimize inventory policies balancing cost, service, and risk
- Identify and quantify operational losses
- Design packaging with mathematically grounded efficiency
- Quantify and manage quality risks and costs
- Make defensible pricing and assortment decisions
- Quantify ROI of marketing and promotional actions
- Apply modern analytics and AI at scale in CPG
- Design cost-efficient and resilient supply chains
- Integrate sustainability into quantitative decision-making

## Prerequisites

- Algebra and basic statistics
- Understanding of business operations (recommended)
- Basic familiarity with data analysis (optional)

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Mathematical Thinking in CPG Systems
**Goal:** Build a systems-level mindset for understanding CPG as an interconnected, quantitative ecosystem.

**Topics:** CPG value chain as a mathematical system, stocks/flows/feedback loops, deterministic vs stochastic decision environments, KPI hierarchies and trade-offs

**Key Concepts:** Systems modeling, conservation laws (flow balance), variance propagation

### Module 2: Demand Modeling & Consumer Behavior
**Goal:** Quantify how consumers respond to price, promotions, and product attributes.

**Topics:** Demand curves and elasticity, cross-elasticity and cannibalization, choice modeling (utility theory), behavioral economics in FMCG

**Key Models:** Price elasticity, multinomial logit, saturating response curves

### Module 3: Forecasting & Uncertainty
**Goal:** Model demand uncertainty and translate it into operational decisions.

**Topics:** Time series decomposition, promotion-induced volatility, forecast accuracy metrics, bias vs variance trade-offs

**Key Models:** Moving averages, exponential smoothing, MAPE, RMSE, probability distributions of demand

### Module 4: Inventory Theory & Service Levels
**Goal:** Balance cost, service, and risk using inventory mathematics.

**Topics:** Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), safety stock under uncertainty, service level definitions (α, β), expiry and obsolescence risk

**Key Models:** EOQ, safety stock equations, Little's Law

### Module 5: Manufacturing & Operations Efficiency
**Goal:** Optimize production systems mathematically.

**Topics:** Capacity, throughput, and bottlenecks, line balancing, changeovers and SKU complexity, OEE and yield losses

**Key Models:** OEE decomposition, queueing theory, constraint optimization

### Module 6: Packaging Geometry & Physical Constraints
**Goal:** Understand how geometry and physics affect cost, quality, and sustainability.

**Topics:** Package volume vs product volume, palletization and cube efficiency, heat and mass transfer in packaging, barrier properties and shelf life

**Key Models:** Packing density, heat diffusion time scales, gas and moisture transmission

### Module 7: Quality, Risk & Process Control
**Goal:** Control variability and ensure compliance using statistical methods.

**Topics:** Process capability (Cp, Cpk), defect rates and Six Sigma metrics, underfill/overfill risk, giveaway optimization

**Key Models:** Normal distribution assumptions, DPMO, probability of spec violations

### Module 8: Pricing, Profit & Portfolio Optimization
**Goal:** Maximize profit under real-world constraints.

**Topics:** Contribution margin logic, break-even analysis, price ladders and constraints, portfolio-level optimization

**Key Models:** Constrained profit maximization, linear and nonlinear optimization

### Module 9: Marketing Analytics & Experimentation
**Goal:** Measure and optimize marketing effectiveness.

**Topics:** Advertising response functions, promo lift modeling, A/B testing fundamentals, causal inference basics

**Key Models:** Diminishing returns curves, difference-in-means estimators, lift and attribution metrics

### Module 10: Data Science & AI in CPG
**Goal:** Apply modern analytics at scale.

**Topics:** Classification and regression in CPG data, demand sensing, computer vision for shelves, model evaluation metrics

**Key Models:** Logistic regression, tree-based models, precision, recall, F1

### Module 11: Supply Chain Networks & Logistics
**Goal:** Optimize flows across complex networks.

**Topics:** Network flow optimization, transportation cost modeling, multi-echelon supply chains, bullwhip effect

**Key Models:** Linear programming, variance amplification metrics

### Module 12: Sustainability & Lifecycle Mathematics
**Goal:** Quantify environmental trade-offs.

**Topics:** Lifecycle assessment (LCA), carbon intensity metrics, material substitution trade-offs, waste and circularity models

**Key Models:** Emissions intensity ratios, mass balance, optimization under sustainability constraints

### Capstone Module: Integrated CPG Decision System
**Goal:** Synthesize all modules into a unified mathematical view.

**Capstone Project Examples:**
- End-to-end SKU profitability model
- Packaging redesign with cost, carbon, and logistics trade-offs
- Price + promo + inventory integrated optimization
