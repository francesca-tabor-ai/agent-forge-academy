# The Mathematics of Beauty & Cosmetics

## Course Overview

Beauty and cosmetics appear subjective and emotional, yet they are governed by precise mathematical, physical, chemical, and economic principles. This course explores how geometry, chemistry, optics, psychology, economics, and data science quantify aesthetic perception, optimize cosmetic formulation, and predict consumer behavior in the global beauty industry.

Students will learn to model beauty as a measurable, optimizable system, bridging science, art, and technology.

## Course Level

- Upper Undergraduate / Graduate
- Executive & Industry Training
- Interdisciplinary STEM / Design Elective

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

- Apply geometric and proportional models to aesthetic analysis
- Use chemical and physical equations to optimize cosmetic formulations
- Model human perception using psychophysical laws
- Analyze beauty markets with economic and statistical tools
- Build data-driven personalization and recommendation systems
- Predict and interpret beauty trends using mathematical models

## Prerequisites

- Algebra and basic statistics
- Introductory chemistry (recommended)
- Basic familiarity with data analysis (optional)

## Assessment & Evaluation

- Analytical Problem Sets – 30%
- Industry Case Analyses – 20%
- Applied Modeling Project – 20%
- Final Capstone Project – 30%

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Beauty as a Quantifiable System
**Focus:** Objectivity vs. subjectivity in beauty, measurement frameworks in aesthetics, cultural invariants in perception

**Key Concepts:** Metrics, variables, and constraints; Mathematical abstraction of aesthetics

**Deliverable:** Concept paper on measurable beauty

### Module 2: Geometry and Proportion in Aesthetics
**Focus:** Symmetry and proportionality, Golden Ratio and facial mapping, geometric manipulation through makeup

**Mathematical Tools:** Ratio analysis, deviation metrics, spatial geometry

**Applications:** Facial analysis software, contouring and highlighting strategies

### Module 3: Mathematical Design of Cosmetic Products
**Focus:** Fibonacci sequences in packaging, visual balance and ergonomics, gradient geometry in cosmetic application

**Models:** Fibonacci spirals, gradient and curvature functions

**Case Study:** Product and packaging optimization

### Module 4: Chemistry of Cosmetic Formulation
**Focus:** Stoichiometry and concentration balancing, pH, viscosity, and stability optimization

**Key Models:** Ingredient ratio equations, optimization constraints

**Application:** Creams, serums, and foundations

### Module 5: Diffusion, Penetration, and Skin Transport
**Focus:** Skin as a layered diffusion medium, controlled delivery of actives

**Key Equation:** J = -D(dC/dx)

**Application:** Retinoids, peptides, and hydrators

### Module 6: Reaction Kinetics and Shelf-Life Modeling
**Focus:** Oxidation and degradation processes, UV and thermal stability

**Key Equation:** C_t = C_0 e^(-kt)

**Application:** Stability testing and expiration modeling

### Module 7: Optics of Skin, Hair, and Color
**Focus:** Reflection, absorption, and scattering, skin reflectance and hair shine

**Models:** BRDF, refractive index models

**Applications:** Virtual try-on technologies, shine and gloss measurement

### Module 8: Color Science and Shade Matching
**Focus:** RGB, CMYK, LAB color spaces, undertone detection and shade expansion

**Tools:** Color conversion matrices, distance metrics in LAB space

### Module 9: Psychology and Perceptual Mathematics
**Focus:** Psychophysics and sensory thresholds, visual, tactile, and olfactory perception

**Key Law:** S = k ln(I/I_0)

**Applications:** Pigment saturation, fragrance intensity design

### Module 10: Economics of the Beauty Industry
**Focus:** Price elasticity, luxury vs. mass-market dynamics, revenue concentration

**Models:** Pareto distribution, elasticity equations

### Module 11: Data Science and AI in Beauty
**Focus:** Regression-based skin analysis, customer clustering and segmentation, recommendation engines

**Methods:** Linear regression, k-means clustering, neural networks

### Module 12: Trend Dynamics and Market Cycles
**Focus:** Cyclic trends, viral diffusion, fractal growth patterns

**Models:** Sinusoidal functions, logistic growth, power-law decay

## Capstone Project

Students apply mathematical modeling or data science to a real-world beauty problem.

**Examples:**
- AI skincare personalization system
- Mathematical formulation optimizer
- Beauty trend forecasting model
- Virtual try-on reflectance simulation
- Economic pricing and demand model
