# Mathematics of Advertising Systems: From Attention to Optimization

## Course Overview

This course reveals how mathematics governs advertising performance, pricing, targeting, creativity, measurement, and scaling. Learners progress from core metrics to causal inference, optimization, machine learning, and economic design.

## Prerequisites

- Basic algebra
- Probability
- Familiarity with digital advertising concepts (recommended)

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, students will be able to:

- Translate campaign goals into measurable variables
- Compare heterogeneous campaigns on a common scale
- Predict likelihood of clicks or conversions
- Design statistically valid experiments
- Allocate spend efficiently under constraints
- Translate predicted value into bidding strategies
- Assign conversion credit mathematically
- Measure long-term brand and media effects
- Build models suitable for bidding and ranking
- Learn optimal creatives and bids dynamically
- Set acquisition bids based on lifetime value
- Prevent overexposure and design efficient reach strategies
- Design measurement systems under privacy constraints

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Advertising as a Quantitative System
**Goal:** Establish advertising as a measurable, model-driven system.

**Topics:** Advertising funnels as mathematical objects, random variables, deterministic vs stochastic outcomes

**Key Metrics & Models:** CTR, CVR, reach vs frequency, expected value per impression

### Module 2: Core Performance Metrics & Unit Economics
**Goal:** Understand how value and cost are normalized and compared.

**Topics:** Normalization across pricing models, unit economics of media buying, performance benchmarking

**Key Metrics:** eCPM, eCPC, CPA, ROAS, marginal ROI

### Module 3: Probability, Uncertainty & User Behavior
**Goal:** Model user actions as probabilistic events.

**Topics:** Bernoulli and binomial processes, conversion probability estimation, entropy and uncertainty

**Key Models:** Logistic regression, Shannon entropy, mutual information

### Module 4: Experimentation & Causal Inference
**Goal:** Separate correlation from true advertising impact.

**Topics:** Counterfactual reasoning, randomized experiments vs observational data, bias and confounding

**Key Models:** A/B testing, difference-in-differences, incremental lift, propensity scores

### Module 5: Optimization & Budget Allocation
**Goal:** Allocate spend efficiently under constraints.

**Topics:** Constrained optimization, diminishing returns, spend-response curves

**Key Models:** S-curves, marginal ROI, Lagrangian budget optimization

### Module 6: Auctions & Market Design
**Goal:** Understand how ads are priced and allocated in real time.

**Topics:** Auction theory fundamentals, private vs social value, incentive compatibility

**Key Models:** Second-price auctions, expected value of a bid, impression valuation

### Module 7: Attribution & Credit Assignment
**Goal:** Allocate value fairly across channels and touchpoints.

**Topics:** Path dependency, cooperative game theory, attribution bias

**Key Models:** Shapley value attribution, heuristic vs data-driven attribution

### Module 8: Time Dynamics & Long-Term Effects
**Goal:** Model how advertising effects persist over time.

**Topics:** Memory and decay, lagged responses, temporal aggregation

**Key Models:** Adstock, time-series regression, Marketing Mix Models (MMM)

### Module 9: Machine Learning Systems in Advertising
**Goal:** Use predictive models at scale.

**Topics:** Feature engineering, calibration and reliability, ranking and selection

**Key Models:** Conversion prediction models, Expected Calibration Error (ECE), NDCG, CTR@k

### Module 10: Exploration, Learning & Adaptation
**Goal:** Balance learning with performance.

**Topics:** Exploration vs exploitation, online learning, regret minimization

**Key Models:** Multi-armed bandits, regret analysis

### Module 11: Customer Value & Retention Economics
**Goal:** Link advertising to long-term business value.

**Topics:** Lifetime value modeling, churn and retention, discounted cash flows

**Key Models:** CLV, survival analysis, hazard functions

### Module 12: Attention, Creativity & Human Constraints
**Goal:** Model attention as a scarce resource.

**Topics:** Wear-in and wear-out, frequency fatigue, viewability geometry

**Key Models:** Optimal frequency curves, attention thresholds, viewability metrics

### Module 13: Privacy, Measurement Limits & Robustness
**Goal:** Operate under regulatory and data constraints.

**Topics:** Aggregation limits, privacy-preserving measurement, signal loss

**Key Models:** k-anonymity, differential privacy

### Capstone Project
**Deliverable:** A full mathematical advertising system design including campaign objective formulation, experiment design, budget optimization, attribution method, and long-term value assessment.
