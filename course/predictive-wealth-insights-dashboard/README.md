## Course Modules

### [Module 1: From Rear-View Mirrors to Predictive Wealth Intelligence](Module_01_From_Rear_View_Mirrors_to_Predictive_Wealth_Intelligence.md)
- Understand why traditional dashboards fail to support forward-looking advice
- Limits of backward-looking performance reporting
- Why inflows and client behavior move before markets
- The shift from data-bridge to insight partner
- **Exercise 1:** Identify decisions today that rely on lagging indicators

### [Module 2: Alternative Data in Wealth Management](Module_02_Alternative_Data_in_Wealth_Management.md)
- Learn how non-traditional data sources create early signals
- Social media sentiment and investment narratives
- Online forum mood and crowd psychology
- Platform search behavior as intent data
- **Exercise 2:** Map alternative data sources to potential investment signals

### [Module 3: Forecasting Fund Flows & Investor Demand](Module_03_Forecasting_Fund_Flows_and_Investor_Demand.md)
- Anticipate which fund categories will be "in favor"
- Sentiment scoring and trend acceleration
- Linking search traffic to inflow probability
- Separating noise from signal
- **Exercise 3:** Design a simple inflow forecast model using sentiment and search data

### [Module 4: Turning Signals into Advisor-Ready Insights](Module_04_Turning_Signals_into_Advisor_Ready_Insights.md)
- Reduce complexity for frontline users
- Insight layering: raw data → signal → recommendation
- Confidence bands and probability scoring
- Avoiding false certainty
- **Exercise 4:** Convert a predictive signal into an advisor-facing insight card

### [Module 5: Dynamic Portfolio Rebalancing & Drift Management](Module_05_Dynamic_Portfolio_Rebalancing_and_Drift_Management.md)
- Automate portfolio oversight without losing control
- Overnight drift monitoring for DPM portfolios
- Threshold-based vs. AI-suggested rebalancing
- Liquidity, tax, and transaction cost considerations
- **Exercise 5:** Define drift thresholds and rebalancing triggers for a model portfolio

### [Module 6: AI Decision Support vs. Autonomous Execution](Module_06_AI_Decision_Support_vs_Autonomous_Execution.md)
- Define the right level of automation
- Suggestive vs. automatic rebalancing
- Human-in-the-loop governance
- Handling market stress and exceptional events
- **Exercise 6:** Design an escalation model for AI-driven rebalancing actions

### [Module 7: Explainability, Risk & Model Governance](Module_07_Explainability_Risk_and_Model_Governance.md)
- Ensure predictive tools are trusted and defensible
- Explaining forecasts to advisors and clients
- Managing model drift and data decay
- Bias detection in alternative data
- **Exercise 7:** Create an explainability framework for a predictive insight

### [Module 8: Building the Wealth Insights Platform](Module_08_Building_the_Wealth_Insights_Platform.md)
- Move from analytics experiment to enterprise platform
- Dashboard design for different user personas
- Integration with portfolio and advisory systems
- Measuring impact: alpha, retention, advisor efficiency
- **Capstone Project:** Design a Predictive "Wealth Insights" Dashboard for a discretionary or hybrid advisory platform
