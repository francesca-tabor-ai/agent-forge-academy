# UBTIF Quick Reference

A one-page cheat sheet for interview preparation.

## The Three Core Questions

1. **Can you do the job?** (Skills & problem-solving)
2. **How do you work?** (Behavior, judgment, ownership)
3. **Should we trust you with bigger scope?** (Impact & growth)

---

## STAR+I Story Framework

| Element | What to Include |
|---------|----------------|
| **S**ituation | Business context (who/what/why) |
| **T**ask | Your responsibility (scope & ownership) |
| **A**ctions | Specific decisions you made |
| **R**esults | Measurable outcomes with numbers |
| **I**nsights | What you learned / would do differently |

### Rules
- ✅ Speak in "I", not "we"
- ✅ Include numbers (%, revenue, latency, users, headcount)
- ✅ One story = one main challenge

---

## Core Competencies (Signal 2+ per answer)

- **Ownership** – End-to-end responsibility
- **Judgment** – Tradeoffs with incomplete data
- **Execution** – Results under constraints
- **Collaboration** – Influence without authority
- **Growth** – Learn, adapt, improve

---

## Coding Interview (5-Step Loop)

1. **Clarify** – Restate problem & constraints
2. **Plan** – Outline approach
3. **Implement** – Clean code
4. **Analyze** – Time & space complexity
5. **Validate** – Test edge cases

---

## System Design (5 Parts)

1. Requirements (functional + non-functional)
2. High-level architecture
3. Data model & APIs
4. Scaling & bottlenecks
5. Reliability, cost, tradeoffs

---

## Decision-Making (DAT Framework)

When asked "Why did you choose that?":

- **D**ata – What information you had
- **A**lternatives – What you considered
- **T**radeoffs – Why this option won

---

## Interviewer Scoring (5 Dimensions)

| Dimension | Pass Signal |
|-----------|-------------|
| Role Skills | Solves at expected level |
| Ownership | Acts beyond narrow scope |
| Judgment | Defensible decisions |
| Communication | Clear, structured, concise |
| Growth | Learns from mistakes |

**One weak dimension can fail you.**

---

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] 10–12 STAR+I stories prepared
- [ ] Stories include numbers/metrics
- [ ] Cover: failure, conflict, leadership, ambiguity, impact
- [ ] Each story maps to 2+ competencies
- [ ] Practiced 2–3 minute timing
- [ ] Ready for DAT follow-ups
- [ ] Technical framework practiced

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ "We" instead of "I"
- ❌ Vague results without numbers
- ❌ Rambling, multiple challenges
- ❌ No ownership/judgment shown
- ❌ Weak communication
- ❌ No insights demonstrated
