# Unified Big Tech Interview Framework (UBTIF)

A comprehensive framework to help users prepare for interviews at Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and other Big Tech companies.

## Overview

The Unified Big Tech Interview Framework (UBTIF) answers three questions interviewers always ask, regardless of company:

1. **Can you do the job?** (Skills & problem-solving)
2. **How do you work?** (Behavior, judgment, ownership)
3. **Should we trust you with bigger scope?** (Impact & growth)

---

## 1. Story Framework: STAR+I (Universal Behavioral Model)

This is STAR upgraded to satisfy Amazon and everyone else.

### STAR+I Structure

- **Situation** – Business context (who/what/why)
- **Task** – Your responsibility (scope & ownership)
- **Actions** – Specific decisions you made (most important)
- **Results** – Measurable outcomes
- **Insights** – What you learned / would do differently

### Company Requirements

- **Amazon** requires STAR
- **Google/Meta/Microsoft** heavily value the Insights portion

### Mandatory Rules

- ✅ Speak in "I", not "we"
- ✅ Include numbers (%, revenue, latency, users, headcount)
- ✅ One story = one main challenge (don't ramble)

---

## 2. Leadership & Competency Layer (Company-Agnostic)

Every answer should signal at least 2 of these traits:

### Core Universal Competencies

| Category | What Interviewers Look For |
|----------|---------------------------|
| **Ownership** | You take responsibility end-to-end |
| **Judgment** | You make tradeoffs with incomplete data |
| **Execution** | You deliver results under constraints |
| **Collaboration** | You influence without authority |
| **Growth** | You learn, adapt, and improve |

### Company Mapping

- **Amazon** → Leadership Principles
- **Google** → Problem solving & role signals
- **Meta** → Impact & speed
- **Microsoft** → Growth mindset

**Key Insight:** If your story shows ownership + judgment + results, it passes all four companies.

---

## 3. Technical Problem-Solving Framework (Coding & Design)

This is the expected mental model across Big Tech.

### A. Coding Interview Flow (5-Step Loop)

1. **Clarify** – Restate problem & constraints
2. **Plan** – Outline approach before coding
3. **Implement** – Clean, readable code
4. **Analyze** – Time & space complexity
5. **Validate** – Test edge cases aloud

#### Key Signals

- Structured thinking
- Communication
- Tradeoff awareness

### B. System Design Framework (Mid–Senior)

1. **Requirements** (functional + non-functional)
2. **High-level architecture**
3. **Data model & APIs**
4. **Scaling & bottlenecks**
5. **Reliability, cost, tradeoffs**

---

## 4. Decision-Making Framework (Used in Follow-Ups)

When asked "Why did you choose that?", use **DAT**:

- **Data** – What information you had
- **Alternatives** – What you considered
- **Tradeoffs** – Why this option won

### Company Mapping

This maps cleanly to:

- **Amazon's** "Are Right, A Lot"
- **Google's** reasoned decision-making
- **Meta's** speed vs correctness
- **Microsoft's** learning-oriented thinking

---

## 5. Interviewer Scoring Model (What They Write Down)

Behind the scenes, interviewers usually score you like this:

| Dimension | Pass Signal |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Role Skills** | Solves problems at expected level |
| **Ownership** | Acts beyond narrow job scope |
| **Judgment** | Makes defensible decisions |
| **Communication** | Clear, structured, concise |
| **Growth** | Learns from mistakes |

**Important:** One weak dimension can fail you, even if others are strong.

## 6. How to Prepare Using This Framework

### Build a Story Bank

- **10–12 STAR+I stories**
- Cover: failure, conflict, leadership, ambiguity, impact
- Each story maps to multiple competencies

### Practice Like an Interview

- Time-box answers (2–3 minutes)
- Practice follow-up drilling
- Say numbers out loud

## 8. Common Interview Questions & Framework Application

### Behavioral Questions

**"Tell me about a time when..."**

- Use STAR+I framework
- Signal 2+ competencies
- Include numbers
- Speak in "I"

**"Why did you choose that approach?"**

- Use DAT framework (Data, Alternatives, Tradeoffs)

### Technical Questions

**Coding Problems**

- Follow 5-step loop: Clarify → Plan → Implement → Analyze → Validate

**System Design**

- Follow 5-part framework: Requirements → Architecture → Data/APIs → Scaling → Tradeoffs

---

## 9. Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ Speaking in "we" instead of "I"
- ❌ Vague results without numbers
- ❌ Rambling stories with multiple challenges
- ❌ Not showing ownership or judgment
- ❌ Weak communication or structure
- ❌ No insights or learning demonstrated

---

## 10. Success Checklist

Before your interview, ensure you have:

- [ ] 10–12 STAR+I stories prepared
- [ ] Each story includes numbers/metrics
- [ ] Stories cover: failure, conflict, leadership, ambiguity, impact
- [ ] Each story maps to 2+ competencies
- [ ] Practiced timing (2–3 minutes per story)
- [ ] Ready for follow-up questions (DAT framework)
- [ ] Technical problem-solving approach practiced
- [ ] System design framework memorized (if applicable)
