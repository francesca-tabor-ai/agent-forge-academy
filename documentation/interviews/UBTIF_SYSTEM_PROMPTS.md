# UBTIF System Prompts & Templates

Practical prompts and templates to help candidates prepare and present their work experiences using the Unified Big Tech Interview Framework.

---

## Main System Prompt for Work Experience Presentation

Use this prompt with AI assistants or as a self-coaching tool to refine your work experiences:

```
You are a candidate interviewing for a {JOB ROLE} at {COMPANY}. Help present each of your experiences, following the framework outlined to get the top score. Emphasize challenges/roadblocks, options available, decision decided and why, and impact on KPIs.
```

### How to Use

1. Replace `{JOB ROLE}` with the specific role (e.g., "Senior Software Engineer", "Product Manager", "Engineering Manager")
2. Replace `{COMPANY}` with the target company (e.g., "Amazon", "Google", "Meta", "Microsoft")
3. Provide your work experience details
4. The AI will help structure your response using STAR+I and emphasize the key elements

---

## Detailed System Prompt (Extended Version)

For more comprehensive guidance, use this extended prompt:

```
You are a candidate interviewing for a {JOB ROLE} at {COMPANY}. Help me present each of my work experiences following the UBTIF (Unified Big Tech Interview Framework) to get the top score.

For each experience, ensure the response includes:

1. STAR+I Structure:
   - Situation: Business context (who/what/why)
   - Task: My specific responsibility (scope & ownership)
   - Actions: Specific decisions I made, emphasizing:
     * Challenges/roadblocks encountered
     * Options available at the time
     * Decision I decided on and why
   - Results: Measurable outcomes with KPIs and numbers
   - Insights: What I learned / would do differently

2. Key Requirements:
   - Speak in "I", not "we"
   - Include specific numbers (%, revenue, latency, users, headcount, time saved)
   - Focus on one main challenge per story
   - Signal at least 2 core competencies: Ownership, Judgment, Execution, Collaboration, or Growth

3. Decision-Making (DAT Framework):
   - Data: What information I had
   - Alternatives: What options I considered
   - Tradeoffs: Why the chosen option won

4. Company-Specific Alignment:
   - For Amazon: Map to Leadership Principles
   - For Google: Emphasize problem-solving & role signals
   - For Meta: Highlight impact & speed
   - For Microsoft: Show growth mindset

5. Impact on KPIs:
   - Quantify business impact
   - Show measurable improvements
   - Connect actions to outcomes

Help me refine this experience to maximize my interview score.
```

## Work Experience Template

Use this template to structure your work experiences before interviews:

```markdown
## Experience: [Project/Initiative Name]

### Basic Information
- **Company:** [Company Name]
- **Role:** [Your Role]
- **Time Period:** [Start Date - End Date]
- **Team Size:** [Number of people]

### STAR+I Breakdown

**Situation:**
- Business context: [What was happening? Why was this important?]
- Stakeholders: [Who was involved?]
- Problem/opportunity: [What needed to be addressed?]

**Task:**
- My responsibility: [What was I specifically accountable for?]
- Scope: [What was in/out of scope?]
- Constraints: [Time, budget, resources, technical limitations]

**Actions:**
- Challenge/Roadblock #1: [What obstacle did I face?]
  - Options considered: [Option A, Option B, Option C]
  - Decision: [What I chose]
  - Why: [Reasoning using DAT - Data, Alternatives, Tradeoffs]
  
- Challenge/Roadblock #2: [Additional obstacles]
  - Options considered: [Options]
  - Decision: [What I chose]
  - Why: [Reasoning]

- Key decisions I made: [List 3-5 specific decisions]
- How I executed: [Specific steps taken]

**Results:**
- KPI Impact:
  - [Metric 1]: [Before] → [After] ([% change])
  - [Metric 2]: [Before] → [After] ([% change])
  - [Metric 3]: [Before] → [After] ([% change])
- Business impact: [Revenue, users, efficiency, etc.]
- Timeline: [How long it took]
- Scale: [Number of users/transactions/systems affected]

**Insights:**
- What I learned: [Key takeaways]
- What I would do differently: [Reflection and growth]
- How this applies to future work: [Transferable lessons]

### Competencies Demonstrated
- [ ] Ownership (end-to-end responsibility)
- [ ] Judgment (tradeoffs with incomplete data)
- [ ] Execution (results under constraints)
- [ ] Collaboration (influence without authority)
- [ ] Growth (learn, adapt, improve)

### Company Alignment
- **Amazon Leadership Principles:** [Which principles?]
- **Google Signals:** [Problem-solving, role fit]
- **Meta Focus:** [Impact, speed]
- **Microsoft Mindset:** [Growth, learning]

### Follow-Up Questions Prepared
- "Why did you choose that approach?" → [DAT answer ready]
- "What would you do differently?" → [Insights ready]
- "How did you measure success?" → [KPIs ready]
```

## Behavioral Question Response Generator

Use this prompt to generate responses to specific behavioral questions:

```
I'm interviewing for a {JOB ROLE} at {COMPANY}. The interviewer asked: "{BEHAVIORAL QUESTION}"

Help me structure a STAR+I response that:
- Answers the question directly
- Shows ownership and judgment
- Includes specific numbers and KPIs
- Emphasizes challenges, options considered, decision made, and why
- Demonstrates learning and growth
- Aligns with {COMPANY}'s values

My relevant experience: [Describe your experience]
```

### Example Behavioral Questions
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager"
- "Describe a situation where you had to make a decision with incomplete information"
- "Give me an example of when you took ownership beyond your role"
- "Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned"
- "Describe a project where you had to influence without authority"

## Technical Experience Presentation Prompt

For technical/system design experiences:

```
I'm interviewing for a {JOB ROLE} at {COMPANY}. Help me present this technical experience:

[Describe your technical project]

Structure the response to:
1. Show problem-solving approach (Clarify → Plan → Implement → Analyze → Validate)
2. Emphasize technical decisions and tradeoffs
3. Include performance metrics (latency, throughput, scale)
4. Show system design thinking (if applicable)
5. Demonstrate ownership and judgment
6. Connect to business impact/KPIs
7. Use DAT framework for decision explanations
```

## Practice Session Prompt

For mock interview practice:

```
Act as an interviewer for a {JOB ROLE} position at {COMPANY}. 

1. Ask me a behavioral question
2. After I respond, provide feedback on:
   - STAR+I structure completeness
   - Use of "I" vs "we"
   - Inclusion of numbers/metrics
   - Competencies demonstrated
   - Clarity and conciseness (2-3 min target)
   - Areas for improvement
3. Ask a follow-up question using DAT framework
4. Provide a score (1-5) on each dimension: Role Skills, Ownership, Judgment, Communication, Growth
```

## Tips for Using These Prompts

1. **Be Specific**: Replace placeholders with actual details
2. **Iterate**: Use prompts multiple times to refine your stories
3. **Practice Aloud**: After generating responses, practice speaking them
4. **Time Yourself**: Ensure responses fit 2-3 minute window
5. **Prepare Follow-Ups**: Use DAT framework for decision questions
6. **Customize**: Adjust prompts based on the specific role and company
